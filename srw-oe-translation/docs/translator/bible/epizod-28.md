# Episode 28 — The fall of Sveet: Amandra Kamandra is unmasked as the true Posaydal and defeated, Olibee is rescued, Grados leaves ISTO; Pailsen dies; Zi prepares for the Death Stinger rematch

The L-Gaim arc's finale plus two side arcs. Connect Force breaks Nei Moh Han's fleet in Gastogal orbit (Gosterro joins, hunting Eiji), descends and beats Giwaza's fleet and Le Cain off Sveet. Le Cain's aide Roan Dimitrich unexpectedly pulls the Grados forces back: a coup in Grados, engineered by Roan with Le Cain's father, takes Grados out of ISTO. In Sveet the shocking L-Gaim twist plays out: arms dealer Amandra Kamandra severs the Bio-Relation link to "Posaydal" (actually the body double Mian Quu Hau Asher), takes Olibee as his new puppet and pilots the Ohji as the True Posaydal. He is beaten when Mian shuts down the Bio-Relation System; Daba rescues Olibee, ISTO accepts a ceasefire in Pentagona, and Giwaza's bid to seize power collapses into negotiation. Amm quietly grieves over Daba's feelings for Olibee. Then the VOTOMS Red Shoulder Document arc: Gregore's squad deserts a mission to kill Yoran Pailsen at the Deraida Highlands; Chirico meets the Perfect Soldier Ypsilon, Zaki learns he was a PS prototype, Pailsen is captured by Rochina and then shot by Byman. Finally, Planet Zi: Connect Force regroups at the Ultrasaurus for the Death Stinger rematch, setting up Episode 29.
