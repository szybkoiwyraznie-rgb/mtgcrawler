# Episode 34 (groups 251-254) — Into Genesis: the Mucus Emperor falls, and Connect Force says goodbye

Connect Force breaches Mucus Genesis's outer shell. White Base holds the entry point, and Vasage clears the way. Inside, the energy conduit is sealed by a regenerating "valve" until Simon drills out its energy node and the Macross Quarter blasts through. At the core, the Mucus Emperor (named by Sheryl) reveals its true form: machinery that humans embedded long ago, which turned the gentle Recoral into the Mucus. With Suol's reinforcements pressing Genesis from outside, Seishiro's Shiden Sword opens the core, and Vasage fuses with it, so the Mucus go dormant everywhere to be reborn as new cosmic life. A long epilogue follows the cast as they disband, return home or take new posts, ending with Seishiro and Himari launching on patrol, her "date" with him set for their leave in Japan.

## Scene 251 — Into Genesis (White Base / Hangar; outer shell; White Base / Bridge)
Wilder and Zero brief the plan: the Ultrasaurus's Gravity Cannon erases the membrane over the entry point, White Base leads, Turner follows, and the Macross Quarter strikes the Genesis Core. They have about 18 hours. White Base must reach the entry within 10 turns, while a periodic shockwave (special trap) blasts units back and the tissue regrows. Vasage's attack breaks the deadlock. White Base, out of ammunition and with its engines failing, stays behind with Colonel Herman to hold the line of retreat as the others go in.

## Scene 252 — The Hopes Entrusted (Genesis conduit; Turner / Interior)
Warp-speed conduits carry them almost to the core until a regenerating "valve" door blocks them. Reaction missiles and the Macross Cannon fail. Vasage says Simon can find and drill out the energy node in the wall. Kamina: "Believe in the me who believes in you!" Simon pierces the node, and the Macross Cannon blows a hole. Turner's group stays behind to guard the exit. In a quiet talk, Seishiro admits Himari has become someone he relies on. She nearly confesses something, then settles for "let's both come home safe."

## Scene 254 — Operation Extend; epilogue (core; many locations)
They fight the Mucus Emperor (Sheryl's name). Suol arrives with Ark Ship reinforcements, and the all-forces attack slows the core's regeneration. The Emperor's true form shows a face and embedded machinery, and Vasage reveals that humans implanted it. Long pre-battle quote set: sympathy for the Mucus, Bernie's "something to tell you after the battle" (Chris calls it bad luck), Kamina's fireworks, Da Cider's "core-ifying" pun, and Lamuness's "super, mega fired up." A Macross Attack opens the core, and the Shiden Sword finishes it. Vasage fuses with the core, the Mucus go dormant everywhere, and Himari sees the core's sad memories. Epilogue: the White Base unit returns to the Federation, and Amuro closes with "There's no greater joy than this!" Shiro leaves the army for Aina. Judau heads to Jupiter with Ple. Quattro, Kamille, Zechs and Bushido join the Preventers. Bernie and Chris reunite with Al. Heero and Setsuna slip away (Heero: "Your cooking… was good."). Kira and Athrun leave, and Shinn stays. The Keroro Platoon is home after "one day" and is stranded when the Warp Gate closes. Getter vanishes into the rift of time. The Ryu Knights return to Earthtia. In Doki Doki Space, Himari sneezes after Korin Land, and Lamuness goes home to entrance exams. Eiji returns to the Planet of the Seal. The Good Thunder Team takes a vacation and vanishes via the Beamler, which oddly reassures everyone. The Cyber Beast Force leaves with Koji. Sheryl and Ranka protest being sent home, and the Skull Squadron stays. The Eldran kids hold a festival, and Kamina, Simon and Yoko say goodbye ("change your world with your own hands"), with Mr. Shinoda praising Yoko. Viral leaves ahead. Daba declines the council chair to care for Olibee. Zero and Suzaku spar politely. Raven and Shadow leave, and Naomi and Rinon will settle things at the tournament. Moonbay tries to take the Ultrasaurus. Irvine goes freelance, Van and Ruuji stay in the Guardian Force (the "Liger Combo"), and Fiona assists Dr. D. Lloyd blew up the Lancelot. Suol orders Seishiro on leave and tells Himari to take him on a date, which becomes a double date with Domon and Rain across Japan. Ken skirt-flips Himari one last time, and Ryu Dolk leaves gifts for Yui and Mai. Goto tells Utsumi he's under arrest, but Utsumi slips away while Bud stays with the Griffon. SV2 goes home, and Kanuka heads to New York. Show returns to Byston Well with Cham. Chirico heads for Kummen, followed by Shakoh, Vanilla, Coconna and Gotho, with Roche watching. The Himari Sensor fades. Seishiro has forgotten what he meant to ask her, and she promises he'll remember on their leave. Exart launches.

## Character state at end of episode
- The Mucus are dormant and transforming. Vasage has merged with the former Genesis Core and lives on in the reborn galaxy.
- Connect Force is scaling down. Warp Gates will eventually be neutralized by the Planet of the Sages, and many units have gone home.
- Seishiro is on enforced leave: a planned Japan tour and double date with Himari, Domon and Rain. He has forgotten what he meant to ask Himari, and she intends to make him remember.
- The Himari Sensor is fading as the Mucus lose their "dark" presence.
- Utsumi is at large. Bud and the Griffon stay with Connect Force. The Votoms crews stay on, except Chirico, who is chasing Fyana in Kummen.
- The Good Thunder Team has vanished via the Beamler, Getter has vanished into the rift of time, and the Keroro Platoon is stuck at home.
