# Episode 21 — Connect wins the war against Goburiki, then turns toward the DG Cell threat and new Knightmares

Goburiki's army attacks Central Connect Station, and Zero takes overall command of Connect's allied fleets. Show defeats the Black Knight, who goes Hyper in a Zwarth swollen with hatred, after Jakoba Aon sends him the Billbine. Lamuness and Da Cider, backed by everyone's "hot-blooded power", finish off the Demon God Goburiki in his ultimate form, which frees the Monskars, and the Dark Dragon Soldiers surrender. Afterward, DG Cells start converging on the Earth Sphere and Ple defects from Neo Zeon to join Judau. On Mars, Lloyd, Cecile and Rakshata unveil the Lancelot Albion, the Guren S.E.I.T.E.N. and the Shinkiro, which get their first combat test against a Mucus raid.

## Scene 163 — The Dark Zwarth
Allied fleets (Gilgamesh, Federation and others) gather at Central Connect Station. Zero is given overall command and explains that Goburiki's jewels control the Monskars and Mucus. In private he tells C.C. that Goburiki has cornered Connect strategically: he sent Da Cider first as a disposable piece, and he could make the warp system go berserk. Zero's unmanned decoy fleet and the Macross Cannon hurt the first wave. At Hoihoi Castle, Don Harumage sends the Black Knight with the Dark Dragon elite. The Black Knight says he serves Goburiki by choice, out of pure hatred for Show, then goes Hyper. When Show is tempted to go Hyper too, Queen Ciela, Lady Elle and Jakoba Aon send him the Billbine. Show purifies the evil aura ("I don't kill people! I'll kill that hatred!") and the Zwarth vanishes from this world. Seishiro steadies Himari against the Black Knight's malice and trusts his father's Raiko Blade.

## Scene 165 — Goburiki, Ultimate Form
Macross Quarter's elite unit fights Goburiki's main body on the Ark Ship. Don Harumage is pompous and taunts every pilot. Da Cider demands the title "Super Cool Hero Who Slew Goburiki a Second Time", and Hebi Metako laughs that Harumage's bald head has turned red ("the octopus is boiled"). Zero reveals that Goburiki lost the bet the moment he showed himself, because his arrogance made him want to savor their despair. When everyone runs low on energy, all the hot-blooded heroes send their spirit to Lamuness and Da Cider. Lamuness says "I'm ULTIMATELY fired up right now!", Da Cider says "Blood! Pressure! RISING!", and they defeat Goburiki. The Monskars become Guardian Knights again, and the Dark Dragon Soldiers surrender. The space-time distortion fades, but fear of a future Mucus horde remains.

This is a near-duplicate of 165, a variant script. Only a few lines differ (Koji's split, Suzaku's line, Da Cider's "Let's do this!"), and they are translated identically.

## Scene 167 — Vanguard of the DG Cells
Quattro's analysis shows DG Cell sightings surging in the Earth Sphere and dropping elsewhere, as if they are converging on Earth. Domon's team is surrounded by Death Army at an asteroid. Ple arrives alone in a Qubeley Mk-II to help Judau, and Quattro distrusts her as an Enhanced Human. Kamille and Amuro sense no malice. Ple declares she has left Neo Zeon, and she agrees to a medical check only if Judau goes with her. Domon is convinced that the Devil Gundam must be destroyed. Neo Zeon's protest is a formality, and Ple is assigned to Connect Force.

## Scene 168 — DG Asteroid Approaching
A hollow asteroid heading for Earth hides a DG-fused Mucus Gate along with Mucus and Death Army. Domon notes that only killing the core, the Devil Gundam, will solve things. Keroro wants to use DG proliferation to mass-produce Gunpla. Amuro senses that something far worse is approaching.

## Scene 169 — A New Knightmare Frame
At the Mars Connect Station lab, Lloyd and Rakshata bicker (she calls him "the Earl of Pudding") and reveal the new machines. The Lancelot Albion with Energy Wings goes to Suzaku, the Guren S.E.I.T.E.N. (Cecile went overboard) to Kallen, and the Shinkiro to Zero. C.C. asks after her pizza-making robot, and Cecile's homemade pizza horrifies Lloyd, who is "saved" by a Mucus alarm. The trio tests the new machines in combat while C.C. supports them from the station. Suzaku warns that the power is dangerous, and Zero says it must be used rightly.

## Character state at end of episode
- Goburiki (Don Harumage) is defeated. The Monskars are Guardian Knights again, and the Dark Dragon Soldiers have surrendered. The Central Connect Cluster is secure for now.
- The Black Knight (Bern) and his Zwarth have vanished from this world after being defeated by Show.
- Show now pilots the Billbine, sent by Jakoba Aon, Queen Ciela and Lady Elle. The Dunbine was recovered.
- Da Cider has fully redeemed himself alongside Lamuness, and his rivalry and comradeship continue.
- DG Cells are converging on the Earth Sphere, and Domon is set on destroying the Devil Gundam.
- Ple has defected from Neo Zeon and joined Connect Force. She clings to Judau and is under medical care for her Enhanced Human drugs. Quattro remains wary of her.
- Suzaku has the Lancelot Albion, Kallen the Guren S.E.I.T.E.N., and Zero the Shinkiro. Lloyd, Cecile and Rakshata work together at the Mars Connect Station.
- Connect Force is again being dispatched across the solar system against DG-fused Mucus Gates.
