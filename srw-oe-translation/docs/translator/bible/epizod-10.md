# Episode 10 — Signs of the Evil God: Liger and Ganbaruger join up, Himari links with Vasage, and Solomon falls

Back in the Solar System, Connect Force follows Yui and Mai's prophetic dreams to a Mucus Gate under the mountains near Aozora Town, where Metal Knights and the Mucus are working together; Ken Taiga and the Ganba Team then formally join the force. In the cavern below, Vasage sends Himari a vision that reveals the gate's magma-feeding roots, and the force concludes that Mucus Gates are speeding up the Evil God Drago's revival. In an asteroid-belt mission Vasage warps in through the Exart's core and fights in sync with the Exart, which unlocks Emperion Glanz and Vario Reflect, and Dr. Kusanagi meets Himari and reveals that she is a descendant of the Sages. The Gundam thread moves on: the White Base team collects the NT-1 Alex at Side 6, beats off the Cyclops Team (Bernie is captured by Chris, and Char covers the others' retreat), and a Connect Force detachment stops Commander Killing's nuclear attack on the colony. The episode ends with the fall of Solomon, Dozle's last stand in the Big Zam, and Gihren's refusal of any ceasefire.

## Scene 82 — Signs of the Evil God
Narration: the force is back in the Solar System, the Federation-Zeon war has stalled, and the reappearing Metal Knights hint that Drago is returning. Guided by Yui and Mai's dream, Ken summons Liger in a forest near Aozora Town and finds Metal Knights fighting alongside the Mucus ("Ugly mug like that was made to serve Drago!"). The Ganba Team (Kotaro, Yosuke, Rikiya) arrives, then Connect Force and Jin. Himari senses a Mucus Gate underground, and Seishiro notes how sharp her senses are getting. Narration: a strike team forms, and Ken and the Ganba Team are attached to Connect Force from that day on.

## Scene 83 — The Mucus Gate Below
In the hangar the crew discusses how hard dormant gates are to find (Luca: a gate is like a seed that is hard to spot until it sprouts). Underground, a Giga Nekton appears and the gate regenerates absurdly fast. Himari goes silent while Vasage sends her a vision, then explains that the gate draws magma energy through its roots, and the force destroys it. Farewells follow: Tobei, Yaminorius ("Yesterday's enemy is today's friend") and Ms. Aiko see off the Ganba Team, and Ken leaves Yui, Mai and reporter Saeko Yagami to watch Earth. Ken believes negative emotions flowing through Mucus Gates are feeding Drago. In the hangar Himari describes feeling like the Mucus's bloodstream (thirst, hunger, throbbing pain); Seishiro believes her, is relieved she hasn't "awakened into a different person", and gets flustered when she asks if he likes her as she is.

## Scene 84 — Talking with the Envoy
Narration on Vasage the Envoy and the lost Sages who could speak with it. Himari tells the Quarter crew that Vasage feels similar to the Mucus, and Seishiro explains that the two may be related species. Sheryl asks whether Himari wants to know where she came from (she doesn't, much). At an isolated asteroid gate a Benthos-class Mucus appears and drops self-destructing escort units; Vasage warps in using the Exart's core as an anchor, and Himari relays Vasage's attacks onto Seishiro's trigger (new attacks: Emperion Glanz, Vario Reflect). At Connect Station Dr. Kusanagi meets Jeffrey and Suol, then surprises the pair in the hangar. She reveals that the Exart's controls were tuned to Himari, that Himari is a descendant of the Sages, and that she kept it secret for Himari's safety. Then she asks whether they have kissed yet ("No, we haven't yet!") and takes Himari off for "girl talk".

## Scene 85 — Operation: Seize the Alex
The White Base team is temporarily recalled to collect the NT-1 Alex at a secret Federation lab in neutral Side 6. Bernie and Al watch the port, and Bernie fishes for whether Chris mentioned him. The Cyclops Team (Steiner, Misha, Garcia, Andy) attacks with orders changed to destroy the Alex, and Chris launches under protest. Amuro and Shiro's team arrive, then Char (with Slender and Crown in a Gelgoog) covers the Cyclops retreat: Commander Killing plans to nuke the colony on his own authority, and the team is moved under Kycilia's command. Chris downs Bernie's Zaku and recognizes him as he surrenders. Char trades ideals with Amuro and Shiro ("Just as ideals alone cannot win a war…"). Aboard the White Base, Chris joins the team and Bright explains Char's unofficial deal: intel on the nuke in exchange for assigning Bernie to Connect Force. In sickbay Bernie admits that the "Al's big brother" story was a lie, and Chris teases that if he joins they'll be colleagues.

## Scene 86 — Stop the Nuclear Attack
Narration: the Orthros Team tipped off Connect, Bernie's testimony confirmed it, and Side 6 granted permission to fight. A Connect Force detachment stops the nuke-carrying Musai (Kamina: "If it blows, we'll deal with it then!"; Keroro frets over whether helping is canon; Sheryl tells Alto to trust the others). Chris says goodbye to Al and promises to bring Bernie back after the war. In his quarters Bernie agrees to fight with Connect Force, but not against Zeon. Narration: Killing is arrested, Connect gets the credit (the Orthros Team hid its role), and the White Base faces a major Federation operation.

## Scene 87 — Space Fortress Solomon
The Solar System breaks Solomon's defenses. Dozle abandons the fortress, entrusts his family (Zenna, Mineva) to M'Quve to take to Kycilia, orders evacuation flares, and sorties in the Big Zam toward Tianem's fleet. Conscon follows in a crippled Gwazine to cover the retreat ("This reminds me of Loum"). Canon lines: Amuro's "It's overwhelming…!", Dozle's "Once the Big Zam is mass-produced…" and "I will not let you! … As long as I'm here, you will not pass!" Kai and Sayla are uneasy about fighting a rearguard, and Sayla senses that the pilot is a Zabi. Conscon dies, Dozle falls, and the Zeon survivors surrender. On the bridge the crew doubts that Gihren will accept a ceasefire. Narration: Degwin seeks peace, but Gihren prepares all-out resistance.

## Character state at end of episode
- Ken Taiga (Liger) and the Ganba Team are now Connect Force members. Yui, Mai and Saeko Yagami stay on Earth to track Metal Knights, and Tobei, Yaminorius (Mr. Yamino) and Ms. Aiko guard Aozora Town.
- The force believes that Mucus Gates are accelerating Drago's revival by channeling negative emotions from across the universe. Connect has begun investigating.
- Himari can now link with Vasage directly (shared images, not words). Vasage can warp to the Exart via its core and fight in sync with it (Emperion Glanz, Vario Reflect). Dr. Kusanagi has confirmed that Himari is a descendant of the Sages.
- Dr. Kusanagi (head of the Mucus Research Institute and Development) has met Himari, approves of her, and is teasing the pair about romance. Himari now asks to be called just "Himari".
- Seishiro is openly fond of Himari but flustered by it. He says he has made peace with his father's death and takes pride in it.
- Chris (Lieutenant Christina Mackenzie) and the NT-1 Alex have joined the White Base team. Bernie (Corporal Wiseman) is a POW who will join Connect Force but refuses to fight Zeon. Both promised Al to return together.
- The Cyclops Team survivors are now under Kycilia via Char. Commander Killing is arrested. The Orthros Team is quietly feeding Connect intel.
- Solomon has fallen, and Dozle and Conscon are dead. Zenna and Mineva escaped with M'Quve. A Baoa Qu is the expected next target, and Gihren rejects a ceasefire.
