# Episode 12 — Guardians of the Colonies join up, a Mucus seed in the asteroid belt, and Rudolph's kidnapping leads to the Blade Liger

Episode 12 opens in the Earth Sphere: the Connect Force retakes an industrial colony that the Mechanization Empire had turned into a mobile suit/Mechanized Beast plant, and the two "Guardians of the Colonies", Setsuna (Gundam Exia) and Heero (Wing Gundam Zero, sent by Relena and Doctor J), formally join the force. After quiet character scenes (Heero's lone-wolf maintenance, Domon and Seishiro's sparring talk, Himari's lunch) and comic health checks at Connect Station on Mars, Vasage warns Himari of a newly sprouted Mucus Gate in the asteroid belt, which the force destroys. Zero's intel sends the force back to Planet Zi, where Prozen's plot to assassinate Emperor Rudolph is foiled by accident when Rosso and Viola (Desert Arcobaleno) kidnap the boy for ransom. Prozen declares Rudolph dead, blames the Republic and takes the throne; Rosso and Viola become Rudolph's protectors, hand him to Van's party, and are seemingly killed holding off Raven's new Geno Breaker. In a running fight toward Guygalos, Raven nearly destroys Van, but Fiona and Zeke's power evolves the Shield Liger into the Blade Liger, Shadow drags Raven away in fear, and a hidden "???" (Hiltz) watches with interest.

## Scene 100 — Guardians of the Colonies
Narration: runaway-Labor incidents across the colonies are a Mechanization Empire uprising, and unaffiliated Gundams nicknamed the "Guardians of the Colonies" have been saving colonies. In the White Base hangar the kids, Koji, Himari, Seishiro, Kei, Quattro, Amuro, Kamille, Chris, Bernie and Shiro reflect that a common threat is uniting Federation and Zeon (Gihren only retreated after losing the Solar Ray; Shiro: peace needs a first step; Van: just help people and everyone gets along). Jeffrey and Bright plan a two-pronged assault on an occupied industrial colony. Setsuna, already fighting there in Exia, proposes a joint front ("Gundam Meister Setsuna F. Seiei"); the force defeats Labors, mobile suits, Mechanized Beasts and the Dark Go-Saurer (Silcorn summon unlocked). Setsuna then asks to join the Connect Force early; Mirai sees warmth behind his stoicism.

## Scene 101 — Mop-Up Operation
Flashback "a few days earlier": Relena meets Heero, now his collaborator through Doctor J, and urges him to join the Connect Force ("I'll protect your back"). At the main spaceport, Heero's Wing Gundam Zero blasts away a swarm of Mechanized Beasts and he joins on the spot (Jeffrey: "No room for red tape on the front line"); the port and Dark Go-Saurer fall, and the Federation fleet takes over the mop-up. At Connect Station, Heero refuses help with his Gundam and calls himself a replaceable part (Karen and Shiro worry). In the training room Domon tells Seishiro his swordsmanship is tense because he envies his gifted comrades, and that a flawless person cannot evolve; Himari scolds them for missing lunch, then reveals it was a joke and brings bento ("good manager"). Narration: the threat recedes, information comes out after the ceasefire, and Connect gains public support.

## Scene 103 — The Mucus Nesting in the Asteroid Belt
At Connect Station (Mars), Dr. Kusanagi rushes a new Mucus detector and Suol personally checks crew health. Comic medical exams: Kamina and Van run from the scanner, Keroro refuses to give "Pekoponian" cell samples until Himari bribes him with Shige's machined-aluminum Gundam (Natsumi: "stupid frog", cavity scare). Himari's long exam was for the Himari Sensor; she then receives an image from Vasage of a new, just-sprouted Mucus Gate in the asteroid belt, which the force destroys. Suol orders the force back to Planet Zi on Zero's solid intel that the Guylos Empire is stirring; narration says the Federation sends more forces to defend the solar system.

## Scene 104 — Assassinate Rudolph
Fixed units (Rosso, Viola). Narration: Zero detects Imperial Regent Prozen's scheming, a Connect envoy team is sent, and Prozen moves to assassinate Rudolph first. Rosso and Viola, planning a ransom kidnapping, spot Prozen's special forces disguised in Republic Command Wolves, beat them and grab Rudolph ("Unhand me, you insolent cur!"). Prozen privately fumes that someone outmaneuvered him and blames the Republic for public consumption.

## Scene 105 — The Beast Reborn
Narration: Prozen announces Rudolph's death, takes the throne under the late emperor's will, and hunts the trio; Rosso and Viola, now trusting Rudolph, protect him while he buys their food. Van's party learns the news; Rudolph wanders up asking for food and Dr. D recognizes him. Fiona senses "the devil's spawn": Raven's new Geno Breaker, which destroys the Shield Liger's position with its charged particle cannon. Rosso and Viola arrive (Raven: "a petty thief, right?"), Raven destroys his own allied Zoids, and the bandits sacrifice themselves to cover the escape; Rudolph cries "Those two are my father and mother!" Raven lets Van go, wanting him at full strength; the main force joins Van and heads for the capital.

## Scene 107 — Valiant! The Geno Breaker
Narration: an emergency conference with President Louise; Schubaltz's units distrust Prozen. In the Great Canyon, Sleeper Zoids (including Gun Snipers) stall the force, and Raven joins in, clearing Sleepers with his cannon and saying he wants to fight the Death Saurer. Raven overpowers Van and knocks out Zeke and Fiona; Fiona's voice and Van's feelings ("I wanna keep traveling with you guys!") evolve the Shield Liger, and Dr. D names it the Blade Liger. Shadow, awed, pulls the Geno Breaker away against Raven's will; Dr. D: "It's a miracle!"; a hidden "???" (Hiltz, with Ambient) looks forward to Van growing stronger.

## Character state at end of episode
- Setsuna (Exia) and Heero (Wing Gundam Zero) have joined the Connect Force; Heero acts on Relena's and Doctor J's urging and is still a loner who sees himself as a replaceable part.
- Relena, daughter of the Vice Foreign Minister, now acts as a go-between for Heero and Connect.
- The Mechanization Empire's colony plant is destroyed; the public now sees a shared threat, and Connect has growing public support.
- Seishiro admits he envies his gifted comrades; Domon has offered to spar. Himari teases him and has the upper hand.
- Himari's Vasage link works as an early-warning system for Mucus Gates; medical tests found nothing abnormal.
- Prozen has declared Rudolph dead, blamed the Republic and taken the throne; his trump card is the Death Saurer.
- Rudolph is with the Connect Force, heading for Guygalos; Rosso and Viola are presumed dead after holding off Raven.
- Van's Shield Liger has evolved into the Blade Liger; Raven (Geno Breaker) was forced to retreat by Shadow and is eager for a rematch and to fight the Death Saurer.
- Hiltz and Ambient (still "???") are secretly watching Van.
