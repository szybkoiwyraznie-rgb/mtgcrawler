# Episode 19 — The Federation's assault on Axis fails, Degwin dies, Char takes the Zeong, and the 00 Raiser is won back from Zechs and Mr. Bushido

The One Year War / Neo Zeon storyline comes to a head. After Neo Zeon's Axis-drop operation (previous episode), the Federation, pushed by its government, launches an under-prepared assault on Axis; White Base is recalled to lead the N Field landing while the main fleet attacks the S Field. Gihren, now supreme commander of Zeon and Neo Zeon, lures the Federation into a trap with the Newtype Corps, sends his father Degwin to die aboard the Great Degwin as a morale symbol, and wins decisively. White Base's 08th MS Team seizes control points inside Axis against Ramba Ral, the Black Tri-Stars and Desert Rommel, but the Federation orders a full withdrawal. Kycilia, who learns of Degwin's death, gives Char (her "Casval") the Zeong to attack White Base; Connect Force arrives as a political buffer and Kycilia withdraws on her own judgment. Haman then sends Ple and Ple Two to probe the Macross Quarter. Kycilia releases Char from Zeon and announces she will seek peace on her own. In a separate thread, Kycilia's Orthros Team (Zechs, Mr. Bushido) hijacks the 00 Raiser transport; the dispute is settled by a duel, and Saji Crossroad joins Connect Force. Finally Connect Force moves to the Frontier system, where it captures an ISTO supply base so that N.U.N.S. can take ISTO's Warp Gate without a fight. Leads into Episode 20 (the 203rd Warp Gate, the formal Gundam-style duel with Zechs/Bushido/Char).

## Character state at end of episode
- Degwin Sodo Zabi is dead (killed aboard the Great Degwin); Gihren rules Zeon and used his death for morale.
- The Federation suffered a major defeat at Axis; Revil will take blame but can recover.
- Kycilia has split morally from Gihren, intends to seek peace independently, and has released Char; she knows Char is Casval Deikun.
- Char piloted the Zeong against White Base; Amuro, Kamille and Sayla sensed/saw him. His "Quattro" identity is now compromised to those close to him.
- Haman considers Connect Force a major future threat and plans to contain it politically; Neo Zeon and Zeon are deliberately showing different stances.
- Ple is attached to Judau and suffering headaches; Ple Two hates Judau/Connect and refuses to be compared to Ple. Athrun suspects they are clones.
- Zechs and Mr. Bushido (Orthros Team under Kycilia) respect Connect Force, share its goal, but distrust Connect's ties to the Federation; they lost the duel honourably.
- Setsuna now pilots the 00 Raiser with Saji Crossroad, who has formally joined Connect Force.
- ISTO's Warp Gate to the Frontier system is under joint ISTO/Connect control; ISTO is newly wary of N.U.N.S. power.
- Connect is secretly negotiating with Zeon (via Utsumi's company and Kurosaki) over compensation for its intervention.
