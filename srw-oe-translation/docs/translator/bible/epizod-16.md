# Episode 16 — Connect Force defends Earthtia's Mystic Shield, recruits Ruuji on Planet Zi, and rescues the doomed Barcoff Squad on Galeade

Connect Force splits to repel Don Harumage's simultaneous assault on Eldgear and the Earth Blade: the White Base unit beats back a mind-controlled Da Cider, while the Macross Quarter group defeats the Black Knight's Hyper Zwarth before it can destabilize the Mystic Shield. Harumage withdraws from Earthtia to build up forces for Goburiki's revival and resolves to tighten his control over the unreliable Da Cider. On Planet Zi, while hunting Mucus, the team rescues Ruuji Familon and his Murasame Liger; he joins Connect Force on Schubaltz's recommendation, observed by a mysterious Hiltz, while Fiona senses the true Death Saurer is yet to come. In the Astragius Galaxy, Chirico is recalled to the Barcoff Squad, a misfit unit Rochina and the deposed Pailsen use as test subjects for the "abnormal survivor" theory; their canyon raid on Galeade fails but all survive. Abandoned at a far-north base facing a -185 degree downburst and Balarant/ISTO forces, the squad is rescued by Connect Force arriving a day early, with Kochak's cold-proof PRL mix saving everyone.

## Scene 130 — Assault of the Goburiki Army (Eldgear)
Earthtia's early-warning network lets Connect Force intercept the Goburiki army; White Base defends Eldgear with White Dragon. Da Cider, brain-"boosted" by Harumage, leads the Dark Dragon Soldiers (who resent taking orders from a human) and riffs puns at everyone: "double zest" Double Zeta, Noa's "arrest" taken as stealing his heart, a warrior's pride "gathering dust" (Ryu Dolk), Mazinger's "castle of black iron" vs. zebra/Ingram. Running voice-actor jokes: Judau hates his voice, Sara finds it creepy, Shinobu likes it, and Da Cider steals Shinobu's "Let's do thiiis!". Defeated, he says he'll probably join "next chapter" and flees; the enemy fleet withdraws.
Translator notes: Hebi Metako's じゃん = "y'know"; meta jokes about chapters/EXP kept.

## Scene 131 — Aura Power Run Amok (Earth Blade; Eldgear core; Hoihoi Castle)
The Macross Quarter group defends the Earth Blade against the Black Knight, empowered by Harumage's sorcery; he goes Hyper (Hyper Zwarth), and the surge of negative energy destabilizes the Mystic Shield. Galden, Domon, Daba, Leccee, Paffy and Mr. Bushido (a fellow masked man) all confront him; Show refuses to go Hyper, masters his aura and breaks the Hyper form, and Bern flees. At Eldgear, White Dragon notes Harumage has left Earthtia's space; Galden adds two Dark Dragon divisions to its defense. At Hoihoi Castle, Harumage forgives the Black Knight (guilt keeps him pliant) but finds Da Cider too soft, threatening Punishment Robot No. 2 and stronger mind control, to Hebi Metako's alarm.

## Scene 132 — The Day of Departure (Planet Zi, Guylos frontier; Guardian Force base)
Connect Force waits on Zi to ferry a Guardian Force Zoid unit to Astragius, hunting Mucus meanwhile. Ruuji Familon defends his village alone in the Murasame Liger; Van (whom Ruuji reveres as the hero who beat the Death Saurer) and the team help, and Thomas gripes that nobody read the files. Lamuness senses a Guardian Knight on Zi. Seishiro invites Ruuji to join (Schubaltz recommended him); after consulting his village he leaves with the Guardian Force taking over its defense. A hidden Hiltz ("???") savours Ruuji's growth. At base, the Mucus Gate on Zi stays hidden (Rare Hertz may interfere), Prozen's whereabouts are unknown, and Fiona fears the real Death Saurer.

## Scene 133 — The Galeade Front (Base M7; Manid Canyon; comms room)
After the Tybus Crossing on Lomuss wipes out his 1,500-AT decoy unit, a gravely wounded Chirico is inexplicably sent to Base M7 on Galeade. Nor Barcoff forms the squad: Chirico, brutal Gary Godan, young Zaki ("kid"), and panicky engineer Kochak. Captain Rochina (Ministry of Intelligence) orders a night airdrop to take a canyon base without collapsing the canyon; Chirico, suddenly healed, joins. Kochak's clumsy landing alerts the enemy, the fortress gun brings down the canyon on the relief force, but the squad escapes intact. Rochina reveals to Pailsen (deposed after Sunsa, held by the Ministry) that the squad is an experiment in survival; Pailsen insists only Chirico is a true abnormal survivor.

## Scene 134 — Rescue on Galeade (far-north base; White Base; ops command)
Rochina tells Pailsen a -185 degree downburst is about to hit the base the squad is marching to, three days late through a blizzard. On White Base, Himari dreads the -85 degree cold; Gregore, Murza and Byman explain the squad is a penal unit left to buy time, and Zero notes the rendezvous is timed for after the disaster; the team leaves a day early anyway. Barcoff, an ex-meteorologist, discovers the danger in the empty base; Balarant and ISTO forces attack. Connect Force fights while Kochak (ex-AT developer) mixes PRL for -200; Gregore's team joins in. Everyone survives the downburst; Rochina reports to Pailsen that the squad's standing has changed and it will lead "that operation".

- The Black Knight (Bern) failed with Hyper Zwarth, retains Harumage's favour, and trains to command the Mucus.
- Harumage has left Earthtia to gather forces for Goburiki's revival; the Mystic Shield holds; Galden's two Dark Dragon divisions help guard Earthtia.
- Ruuji Familon (Murasame Liger) has joined Connect Force, looking up to Van; Hiltz is secretly watching him.
- Fiona senses the true Death Saurer has yet to appear; Prozen is missing; Zi's Mucus Gate is undiscovered.
- A Guardian Knight is somewhere on Planet Zi (sensed by Tama-Q).
- The Barcoff Squad (Barcoff, Godan, Kochak, Zaki, Chirico) has been picked up by Connect Force alongside Gregore, Murza and Byman; Kochak earned the squad's respect.
- Rochina and Pailsen are running "abnormal survivor" experiments on the squad; high command now plans a major operation centred on them.
- ISTO and the Balarant are actively pursuing Connect Force in Astragius.
