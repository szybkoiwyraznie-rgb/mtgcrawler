# Episode 26 — The Death Stinger razes New Helic City, the Ultrasaurus awakens, the Red Shoulders are hunted, and the search for the Mechanization Empire begins

On Planet Zi, Hiltz unveils the Death Stinger ("Harbinger of the End"), discards Raven after Van beats him, and nearly kills Shadow with a charged particle cannon. The Death Stinger tunnels to New Helic City and burns it, while President Louise evacuates to Wendeen Lake, where the Republic's hidden Ultrasaurus lies. With the Death Stinger blasting straight through the mountains, Moonbay coaxes the Zoid core awake, the Ultrasaurus dodges Hiltz's shot, and Hiltz withdraws for now. Elsewhere, Gregore's Red Shoulder squad is sent on a fake army mission and ambushed by their own side; Gregore and Byman are crippled, Chirico names Pailsen as the man behind it, and the three leave the Gilgamesh army for Connect Force. Finally, Connect Force tracks the Mechanization Empire to another galaxy through Mucus Gates, with Himari's sensing as the key.

## Scene 200 — Harbinger of the End
Underground, Prozen, now the Dark Kaiser fused with the Death Saurer's core, watches Hiltz fuse two ancient Zoid cores with Ambient's power. Raven leads Sleepers down Route 35 toward New Helic City; Van, Thomas, Irvine, Fiona and the Turner group intercept (comic bickering: Thomas insists Irvine is Guardian Force, Irvine denies it). Fiona begs Raven to stop being used; Van defeats him and Shadow hits its limit. Hiltz raises the Death Stinger, declares Raven a disposable pawn, and fires a charged particle cannon at him; Shadow shields Raven and falls silent. Hiltz burrows off toward the capital, and Raven is left screaming Shadow's name.

## Scene 201 — Fall of the Capital
Herman briefs Louise on three crater exits; Hiltz calls the palace, preaches survival of the strong, and surfaces in Block C23, the city center, outflanking the garrison. Zero persuades Louise to abandon the city for Wendeen Lake, hinting at a "trump card". White Base fights the Death Stinger (energy shield, extreme armor, self-repair) with heroes' rebuttals: Himari's faith in Vasage, Seishiro, Suzaku, Lamuness/Tama-Q ("mya"), Eiji, Koji, Alto, Ruuji, Kira. Hiltz guesses the Wendeen Lake plan, decides to "humor" it, and vanishes underground.

## Scene 202 — The Ultrasaurus Revived
Wendeen Lake, a decoy lake, is drained to reveal the Ultrasaurus, secretly restored by Republic and Empire engineers and armed with Dr. D's Gravity Cannon. Its core has never woken (under 50% odds); Van says it's up to them, and Herman grudgingly likes that. The Death Stinger blasts straight through the mountains, leaving 30 minutes instead of 3 hours. Herman's rallying cry ends in "A miracle!" (running gag with Irvine). Dr. D diverts Gravity Cannon power, and Moonbay retunes the core ("Zoids are alive"). After a limiter on the B10S device is found, the core awakens; Moonbay steers it out of the Death Stinger's shot. Hiltz withdraws, Herman takes command with Moonbay as helmsman, and Fiona senses a bigger threat behind Hiltz.

## Scene 205 — Brush with Death
After the Monad fiasco, Vice Minister Wockam falls, Pailsen vanishes, and Red Shoulder survivors are being erased. Gregore, Murza, Byman and Chirico are sent on a special op against an ISTO survey team on a Didilium planet; after they win, "friendly" units shoot the prisoners and gun down Gregore and Byman. Vanilla, Nor, Shako, Seishiro and Himari arrive and save them. Gregore is riddled with wounds and Byman loses his right arm, which is replaced by a prosthetic he jokes is perfect for strangling Pailsen. Chirico confirms Pailsen is behind it. Murza's silence implies his family was killed. The three formally leave the Gilgamesh army and join Connect Force.

## Scene 206 — Hunting the Mechanization Empire
Analysis of the Ark's Mucus Gate shows the Mechanization Empire regrouping in another galaxy; Connect sends a Warp Gate and the Macross Quarter hunts Mucus Gates with new sensors. Banter: the "Himari Sensor", Hayato explaining her brain links senses, and Viral as Himari's self-appointed guardian (Yoko teases her to choose between Seishiro and Viral). The Saurers and the Eldran kids fight Mucus and Mechanized Beasts and collect samples.

## Scene 207 — A Lead on the Mechanization Empire
White Base destroys another Mucus Gate on an asteroid, with Mechanized Beasts and Nekton. Himari overworks on sample analysis because the kids are counting on her, and Seishiro joins her. In the Saurers' command room, Takako and Yoji enjoy the data work, the kids wonder whether Kikaishin is back stronger, and Kenichi, "all fired up", is assigned to rest for later muscle work.

## Character state at end of episode
- Hiltz pilots the Death Stinger (stronger than the Death Saurer, can move underground) and serves the Dark Kaiser (Prozen fused with the Death Saurer core). He has withdrawn for now, and Mucus and Sleepers have gone quiet on Zi.
- Raven has been discarded by Hiltz; Shadow is gravely hurt and unresponsive.
- New Helic City is destroyed (citizens evacuated). The Ultrasaurus is active under Colonel Herman, with Moonbay as helmsman; the Gravity Cannon is untested and was drained for the startup.
- Fiona senses a threat beyond the Death Stinger behind Hiltz.
- Gregore (heavy wounds) and Byman (prosthetic right arm) are back in action quickly; with Murza they have left the Gilgamesh army for Connect Force. Murza's family is apparently dead.
- Pailsen is confirmed as the one erasing the Red Shoulders.
- Connect Force is closing in on the Mechanization Empire's base in another galaxy; Himari's sensing is central, and she is overworking (setup for her exhaustion in Ep. 27).
- Viral openly vows to protect Himari until they can return home.
