# Episode 14 — War Cloud: the Connect Force invades Pentagona, Show joins, Judau steals the ZZ

Episode 14 is the Connect Force's first open military intervention in ISTO territory: alongside Daba's rebels it takes the planet Mizun from the Poseidal Army (L-Gaim), fighting Nei Moh Han and Gavlet Gablae, who tries to wipe out the captured base with a Planet Bomber. Show Zama and Chum (thrown to Mizun at the end of Ep 13) meet the White Base and join. The side missions then advance three other lines: the Zoids Guardian Force gets Irvine's Lightning Saix and introduces Thomas Schubaltz (and his AI Beek), who joins the Connect Force and instantly falls for Fiona; at the Mars Connect Station, Viral leads unusually coordinated Mucus, an Invader space-rift opens, and the original Getter Team (Ryoma, Hayato, Benkei) returns from the Invader dimension; finally, in the Earth Sphere, Neo Zeon's child Newtype Ple attacks the White Base and Judau Ashta, a 14-year-old junk dealer, hijacks the new ZZ Gundam in Shangri-La and is recruited. The episode ends with Earth Sphere tension spiking as Gihren masses forces opposite A Baoa Qu, leading into Ep 15's Neo Zeon offensive.
