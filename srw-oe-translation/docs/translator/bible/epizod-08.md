# Episode 08 — Doki Doki Space: knights, heroes, a brainwashed rival, and the Red Shoulders

Episode 8 brings the Connect Force to the fantasy worlds of Doki Doki Space and then moves it on to the Astragius Galaxy. On Earthtia (Lord of Lords Ryu Knight), Adeu, Paffy, Hagu Hagu and Galden join the force after a Dark Dragon raid on the ancient city of Eldgear. The force then destroys a Dark Dragon pirate base, whose dying leader boasts of a mysterious new "master". On the Hara-Hara World side (NG Knight Lamune & 40), Lamuness, Tama-Q and the three Arara sisters join, and Da Cider, apparently brainwashed by the reviving demon god Goburiki, lures them into a trap with Monskar and Mucus. Natsumi Hinata (Sgt. Frog) is rescued from Da Cider's hostage scheme and reunited with Keroro. A side mission sends Simon and Moonbay rare-metal mining in a Dark Dragon nest. The episode closes in the Astragius Galaxy (Armored Trooper VOTOMS: Pailsen Files): Chirico and his Red Shoulder squad (Gregore, Byman, Murza) are shunted to the Connect Force by Pailsen, survive an ambush at the rendezvous, and are then called straight back to the hell of the Third Battle of Sunsa. Kusanagi also tells Himari, in confidence, about the hidden Central Connect Station. The episode picks up directly from Episode 7's departure for Earthtia, and the Sunsa ending leads into the Votoms material of Episode 9.

Note: Scene 68 (mission label "？？？？") and Scene 69 ("Rare Metal Mining") are two versions of the same mission with almost identical dialogue; they differ only in the item pickups (Scene 68: Rare Metal / Rare Metal G; Scene 69: assorted parts such as Kamina's Shades) and a few small line variants at the end. Translate shared lines identically.

## Scene 65 — Episode 8: Earthtia (Eldgear / central district; Pafuricia Castle)
- Present: Adeu Waltham, White Dragon, Nagy, Paffy Pafuricia, Hagu Hagu, Galden, Dark Dragon soldiers, Monica Lang, Ram Hoa, Jeffrey Wilder, Catherine Glass; battle quotes by Heero Yuy, Noa Izumi, Asuma Shinohara, Aiko Shimada, Akira Imamura, Jin Hyuga, Koji Hoshiyama, Asuka Tsukishiro, Tsutomu Kojima, Daisuke Sato, Maria Shiratori; later Kusanagi, Himari, Bright Noa, Seranan Pafuricia, Marlia Pafuricia.
- Synopsis: The narration explains that the Dark Dragon Emperor is dead, but remnants still raid Earthtia, and Adeu, the hero who struck the final blow, keeps traveling to train. Nagy and White Dragon summon him to the deserted ancient city of Eldgear: a sinister power behind the Dark Dragon space pirates is more than Galden's Dark Dragon squad can handle, so Adeu must join the Connect Force as Earthtia's representative. Adeu is thrilled ("I'm getting excited!"). The Mystic Shield is weakened over Eldgear to let the ship in. Dark Dragon remnants attack, and Adeu summons Ryu Knight Zephyr from his card. Paffy (with Hagu Hagu) arrives uninvited, furious that she was the only one kept in the dark, since she overheard the message to her father, and Nagy explains that Adeu cannot keep a secret. Galden arrives, teases Adeu ("getting rusty?"), and they Class Change. The Macross Quarter joins in. Galden teases Paffy that she is coming along to keep "bugs" away from Adeu, and Hagu Hagu chimes in "Suspicious, hugu…". Afterward, Kusanagi briefs Nagy, and Adeu admits that he understood little of the space-time talk but knows people need help: "Knight's Code, Rule Number One! A knight never abandons those in need!" Meanwhile, Bright meets King Seranan and Queen Marlia at Pafuricia Castle and learns that Galden has located the pirates' hideout.
- Translator notes: Bright, adventurous fantasy tone. Adeu: earnest, hot-blooded and a little dim. Paffy: a princess with polite, slightly prim speech (ですわ), secretly in love with Adeu. Galden: a cool former rival who is now an ally. Hagu Hagu ends lines with ハグ ("hugu"); keep the tic consistent across episodes. Adeu's 騎士道大原則 catchphrase: keep "Knight's Code, Rule Number One!" consistent with Episode 7. Battle-quote gags: Noa says girls hate reptiles, Asuma reassures her that reptiles don't walk on two legs, and that makes it worse; the Raijin-Oh kids want to "catch one alive". Heero's line is a terse mission report ("Target acquired. Engaging."). "Adeu's card" and "Ryu Knight card" are labels for card images.
- New names: 邪竜族皇帝 → Dark Dragon Emperor (high). 邪竜兵 → Dark Dragon soldier(s) (high). 大賢者 → Great Sage (Nagy's title; high). ソリッド軍団 → the Solid Corps (Earthtia's non-Ryu forces; low). クラスチェンジ → Class Change (Ryu Knight term; high). パフリシア王国 / パフリシア城 → Kingdom of Pafuricia / Pafuricia Castle (follows the glossary spelling; high). リュー使い → Ryu user(s) / Ryu wielder(s) (medium).

## Scene 67 — Da Cider Returns (Kingdom of Arara / castle town and Arara Castle; trap site in Hara-Hara World; White Base / Hangar and interior)

- Synopsis: The narration says that King Yokkora III has agreed to lend his heroes to Connect because Monskar and Mucus sightings suggest that Goburiki is returning. Himari, Ranka and Sheryl play tourist (photos at the castle), and Lamuness bursts out of the castle fleeing Milk's lecture, stopping long enough to call Himari "cute!". Sheryl predicts he'll grow up a womanizer, and says he's still better than a buttoned-up type like Kusanagi. A letter from Da Cider arrives: "I'm at the Dragon Palace having a blast, come join me", plus a P.S. to bring the King Squasher, which is obviously a trap. Da Cider makes a terrible pun on "Connect"; Himari snorts and Kusanagi doesn't get it, so Da Cider declares Himari a good guy who will be his bride (Metako: "No, she'll be the maid!"). Lamuness admits he's excited to travel with "all those cute girls" and Milk tries to throw him off the ship. Natsumi is welcomed by Yoko and hands Keroro an upgraded gadget from Kululu that turns his Gunpla into a working mobile suit.
- Translator notes: Broad comedy, the zaniest scene so far. Da Cider is a narcissist braggart who breaks the fourth wall; give him bombastic, silly English. Wordplay that needs re-creation: (1) コネクトは８回しか来ない…コネクトはコネェ９度 is a pun reading "Connect" as kone(e)-ku-do ("won't come nine times"); invent an English "Connect" pun with the same groaner energy (e.g. "Connect? Con-NEXT time!" or "Connect? More like Con-DON'T-ect!"), keeping Kusanagi's blank silence and Himari's snort. (2) そうだろ、そうだろ、さしすせそ～だろ～ riffs on the kana row sa-shi-su-se-so; render it as a similar sing-song ("Right, right, right as rain, right on, right-o!"). (3) 蛇だけにヘビーな事態 puns hebi (snake) on "heavy"; try "a hiss-terical situation" or "things are getting a little snake-y". (4) His canonical self-intro 敵にすれば手強い！友達にすれば楽しい！彼氏にしたら超ハッピー！ ("Tough as an enemy! Fun as a friend! Super-happy as a boyfriend!") must stay identical whenever it recurs. (5) The Judau/Da Cider "stop copying me" gag is a voice-actor in-joke (same seiyuu); Metako: "You sound exactly alike!" Hebi Metako says じゃん at the end of lines and calls Da Cider ダーリン ("Darling"). Tama-Q ends lines with ミャ ("myaa"). Keroro's であります tic and 吾輩 (glossary voice card); ボケガエル = "stupid frog" (glossary). Natsumi is his fierce "guardian" in the house. 竜宮城 = the Dragon Palace from the Urashima Taro tale ("living it up at the Dragon Palace"). スットコドッコイども = "you nincompoops". グンバツ = jive inversion of 抜群, so make it slangy ("primo brains and looks"). Alto: "Why do I feel so humiliated? He hypes me up just to use me as a stepping stone!" Lamuness: girl-crazy but a genuine hero; Milk: jealous.
- New names: レスカ → Lesca (Cafe au Lait's nickname, from lemon squash; medium). 竜宮城 → the Dragon Palace (folklore; high). 熱血ゲージ → Hot-Blood Gauge (Lamune's power meter; medium). 戦闘隊長 → Battle Captain (Da Cider's self-styled title; medium). 変身アイテム (ギロロからもらった) → transformation gadget (descriptive).

Moonbay has already negotiated her cut. Kamina touts Simon as "Simon the Digger", who dug up Gurren Lagann's face, so Simon goes along as chief miner. Galden's line about reforming the Dark Dragons matters for his arc (he is half Dark Dragon in canon). Item pickups in Pomoc lines are game item names; check the item glossary if one exists.

Pailsen, sensing a Ministry of Intelligence spy, transfers Gregore, Byman, Murza and Chirico to the Connect Force with no destination given ("absolute obedience is the Red Shoulder rule"); Chirico asks only who issued the order. ## Scene 71 — Battle of Sunsa (Planet Sunsa)
- Present: Gregore, Murza, Byman, Chirico, Red Shoulder trooper.
- Synopsis: The narration explains that the hundred-year Gilgamesh–Balarant war is stalemated, but both sides are escalating ahead of ceasefire talks, and the Third Battle of Sunsa is launched with the Red Shoulders at its core. Gregore curses being sent to the front after the brief taste of Connect food. With the lead units wiped out, Chirico leads a dash through a land battleship's barrage to the foot of a hill. The epilogue narration: in February AC 7213, Sunsa becomes hell; after days of fighting, the Red Shoulders crush the Balarant forces, and the planet's atmosphere becomes unbreathable, which ends Sunsa's history.
- Translator notes: Grim and fatalistic, in keeping with the Votoms prologue (Sunsa is Chirico's hellish past in the series). The narration should read like a war chronicle. Mission note: fixed sortie units.
- New names: 第３次サンサ攻略戦 → Third Battle of Sunsa (medium). 地上戦艦 → land battleship (high).

## Character state at end of episode
- Adeu, Paffy, Hagu Hagu and Galden have joined the Connect Force as Earthtia's representatives. Paffy came against orders out of love for Adeu, and Galden teases her about it.
- A new, unknown master controls the Dark Dragon pirates; the old Dark Dragon Emperor is confirmed dead. Galden wants the Dark Dragons reformed, not wiped out.
- Lamuness, Tama-Q, Milk, Cocoa and Cafe au Lait (Lesca) have joined the force. Lamuness is girl-crazy, which makes Milk jealous.
- Da Cider (with Hebi Metako) is under Goburiki's control (or at least acting like it) and still at large; he wants to beat Lamuness and has "proposed" to Himari. Goburiki is reviving and can use Mucus Gates.
- Natsumi Hinata has been rescued and is aboard the White Base with Keroro, friends with Yoko. She remembers nothing about who kidnapped her. Keroro now pilots a weaponized Gunpla.
- Kusanagi has told Himari, and only her among the juniors, that Connect's secret headquarters sits at the Central Connect Cluster hub. She must keep it secret.
- Suol has promised Himari a chance to "talk" with Vasage about the warp strain.
- Chirico, Gregore, Byman and Murza served briefly in the Connect Force but are back with the Red Shoulders on Sunsa. Pailsen moved them to shield Chirico from the Ministry of Intelligence, and someone inside the army tried to have them killed at the rendezvous.
- Sunsa's atmosphere has been destroyed by the Red Shoulder campaign.
