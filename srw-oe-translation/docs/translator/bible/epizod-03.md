# Episode 03 — From the Kurils to Mars: Celestial Gundams, Keroro, pirates, and Grados at the Mars Base

White Base, now Connect Force's flagship, defends a Federation "observatory" in the neutral Kuril Islands from Garma Zabi, Char and M'Quve, only for Heero and Setsuna to drop in and destroy it as a secret Cyber-Newtype lab that the Invaders had already taken over. The Cyber Beast Force formally joins Connect Force, and Suol admits he let the Federation "fool" him to smoke out its corrupt faction, while Gihren and Kycilia decide to court both the colony forces and Connect. In a colony, SV2 and Exart chase rogue Labors and meet Sgt. Keroro, flung from Pekopon, whose "gero gero" resonance call turns out to trigger HOS Labors. En route to Mars, Connect Force raids a pirate asteroid manned by ex-Zeon deserters, and Seishiro wonders how to save such men. At Mars, the Federation ground base is attacked by Grados SPTs (Gosterro); defector Eiji Asuka defends it with Skull Squadron, Exart and the Macross Quarter, and Eiji, the Macross Quarter and Skull Squadron join Connect Force at Mars Station.

## Scene 25 — Gundam Intervention (Kuril Islands observatory; Zeon cruiser; White Base)
White Base, the Getter Team, Mazinger Z and Exart arrive at a silent Federation observatory on an uninhabited Kuril island; the Cyber Beast Force joins them (Masato flirts with Himari, Himari hates the cold). Garma, who wants to expose the Federation's treaty violation, is told by Char that White Base now belongs to Connect Force; Char privately uses Garma's friendship ("Victory and glory to you!") and muses that the universe is no longer just Zeon vs. Federation. M'Quve arrives with the Black Tri-Stars on Kycilia's orders, believing the site is a Newtype lab; Garma wants the Zeon Cross. Many units banter (Keroro's "Red Comet? I eat Red Fox udon for breakfast" noodle joke, Noa's "my Alphonse is my personal custom unit too", Kamina's "don't dodge"). Zeon retreats, then two Gundams (Exia, Wing Zero) drop like ballistic missiles and destroy the facility.
- Translator notes: Char's private asides in parentheses; Garma calls Kycilia 姉上 "my sister". Keroro/Kululu instant-noodle brand gag (緑のたぬき / 黒い豚カレー) localized as brand banter. M'Quve used an H-bomb (Odessa). 一寸の虫にも五分の魂 → "even a worm will turn".

## Scene 26 — A Black Shadow Creeps Closer (observatory; White Base hangar and bridge; Kycilia's HQ)
Setsuna and Heero say Connect is being used by the Federation and that the site was a Cyber-Newtype lab running human experiments; Alan blames a military faction and arms makers. Go senses the place is now ruled by something else, and Invaders and a Metal Beast burst out; everyone, including the two Gundams, wipes them out before the Gundams leave. The Cyber Beast Force joins Connect Force; Seishiro calls Suol "a second father" who prefers "Chief". In the hangar Himari vents about the day; Seishiro says such days will be common now. Suol reveals the Gundams belong to unnamed "colony forces" who may join Connect Force on condition of non-interference, and that he deliberately played along with the Federation to expose its bad apples ("sly old fox"). Gihren tells Kycilia the mission was a strategic victory and approves sending a unit to work with Connect to buy time.
- Translator notes: タヌキ → "sly old fox". 大男総身に知恵が回りかね → "the bigger they are, the harder they fall". Hiryu's quoted line kept in “ ”.

## Scene 27 — The Green Invader (colony; White Base hangar)
Sakaki and Shige arrive at a colony; Connect assigns SV2 and Exart to secure unmanned Labors, since even non-HOS Labors are going rogue via a virus spreading through external links. Keroro, lost in the Universal Century in his Keroro Robo Mk-II, tries his "gero gero" resonance call to reach his platoon, and nearby Labors go berserk; Ota tries to arrest him. Seishiro rescues him, Amuro arrives, and Keroro geeks out over the RX-78-2; repeating the call summons more rogue Labors (Bud and the Griffon also join the fight). Keroro, a Gunpla fan, stays aboard; narration explains that low-frequency resonance (from wind on layered structures) triggers HOS Labors, a new countermeasure program is written, and Keroro is kept away from Labor zones.
- Translator notes: Keroro's であります rendered with sparing "sir"; ＜主人公名＞殿 → "Mr. ＜主人公名＞, sir". おやっさん → "Chief" (Sakaki).

## Scene 28 / Scene 29 — Raid on the Pirate Base (White Base bridge; pirate asteroid)
Bound for the Mars Warp Gate, Bright asks whether to raid a pirate base near their course; everyone votes yes. White Base enters the asteroid's port and the whole roster fights the pirates (many short battle quips from kids' teams, Lamuness, Rosso, Chris, Judau, Kira). Afterwards Seishiro is troubled that the pirates were ex-Zeon soldiers; Sanders explains deserters have nowhere to go, Alan and Ryo point to syndicates that exploit them. Himari stays behind to study Warp Gates and Mars Station with her raised database access. Scene 29 is a branch variant of Scene 28 (translated identically; adds label "Voyage to Mars").

## Scene 30 / Scenes 31-32 — The Mars Base Attack (White Base bridge; Mars ground base; Mars Station; Briefing Room)
Narration: Connect's solar-system HQ is a station in the Mars sector with a giant Warp Gate to other universes. On the bridge Himari borrows Fraw's terminal; Seishiro was teaching Shiro his family's sword style; Connect's guide beam speeds the trip. Seishiro explains Warp Gates artificially mimic the Mucus, can lead to other universes, cannot choose destinations, and need Ark Ships (shown with the Warp Ring). An SOS: Gosterro's SPTs are attacking the Federation ground base on Mars; Eiji defends it, Skull Squadron (Alto, Michel, Luca) arrives, then Connect Force and finally the Macross Quarter drive Gosterro off. At the station Eiji explains Grados plans to colonize Earth and that he is half Earthling; Suol introduces Captain Jeffrey (Macross Quarter and Skull Squadron join for the Frontier system) and accepts Eiji. Scenes 31 and 32 are near-identical branch variants of Scene 30 (only window breaks and a few battle lines differ).
- Translator notes: Gosterro's crude "Earth monkeys" taunts; Luca calls Alto "Captain Alto" here (Alto is Skull leader). Michel tests Alto by suggesting pursuit.

## Character state at end of episode
- Connect Force now includes White Base, the Cyber Beast Force, Getter Team, Mazinger Z, SV2, Keroro, Eiji (Layzner), the Macross Quarter and Skull Squadron.
- Heero and Setsuna belong to unnamed "colony forces" that may join Connect Force on a limited basis if left free to act.
- Connect and the Cyber Beast Force know the Federation ran a secret Cyber-Newtype lab; Suol used the incident to expose a corrupt military faction.
- Invaders had secretly taken over that lab; the Getter Team fears the Invader infiltration is widespread.
- Gihren and Kycilia plan to court the colony forces and send a Zeon unit to cooperate with Connect to buy time; Kycilia runs Newtype research.
- Char is quietly using Garma and preparing to act on his own as the war becomes multipolar.
- Keroro is stranded in this universe; his resonance voice explained the HOS Labor rampages, and he is kept away from Labors.
- Eiji Asuka (half Earthling) has defected from Grados, which plans to conquer Earth; Gosterro escaped vowing revenge, and the Grados vanguard's mothership must be destroyed quickly.
- Himari is studying Warp Gates and Mars Station and is thrilled at the prospect of warping.
- Seishiro taught Shiro his family sword style and worries about how to save men driven to piracy.
