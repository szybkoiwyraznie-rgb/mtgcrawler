# Episode 05 — Viral and the Mucus assault the Warp Gate, Vasage is revealed, Connect Force warps to the Frontier system, while the Zoids, VOTOMS, Lamune, G Gundam, Ryu Knight and Code Geass casts begin their own stories

Just before the warp to the Frontier system, Connect Station and the Warp Gate on Mars are attacked by Mucus led by the Beastman Viral, who was flung into this world by his fight with Kamina and now commands the Mucus by "the law of the beast." Connect's top secret Vasage, a living creature who makes the Warp Gate efficient, appears; Himari can hear both Vasage and the Mucus. Setsuna's Exia arrives early as the colonies' envoy, and Vasage wipes out most of the enemy with a blast of his warp energy, forcing Viral to retreat but delaying the warp by days. Connect Force finally sets off from the solar system with a casual speech from Suol. The second half is a set of prologue missions on other worlds: Van meets Fiona, Zeke, Moonbay and Irvine on Planet Zi; Chirico survives the Red Shoulders' "cannibalism" on Odon; Lamuness and Da Cider rescue Natsumi in Doki Doki Space; Domon hunts DG Cells with smuggler Gotho; Galden takes his Dark Dragon unit to space; and Suzaku, Cecile and Lloyd land on Planet Zi and join Karl Schubaltz.

## Scene 40 — Warp Gate
In the Connect Station lounge, Simon frets about warping while Kamina and Himari are thrilled; Kamina keeps calling everyone "Team Gurren," then "Team Dai-Gurren," to Seishiro's silent dismay, and Seishiro admits he is starting to enjoy the team's energy. Himari hears a voice before an unknown creature arrives: Seishiro reveals it is Vasage, a classified Connect ally, and Suol explains Vasage greatly cuts the time and energy of each warp. Mucus attack from the asteroid belt and Himari again senses them before the sensors. Viral appears leading the Mucus; he says humans pollute the world and must be purged, claims the Mucus bear an old grudge against humanity, and agrees to "hear out" whoever proves their strength as warriors. He trades barbs with many units (Da Cider's "go Viral" pun falls flat), then heads for the Ark Ship, which seems to be his target because of Vasage; Exart and Gurren Lagann pursue.

## Scene 41 — Viral the Beastman
At the Macross Quarter, Connect Force holds off endless Mucus waves until Setsuna's Exia arrives far ahead of schedule as the colonies' envoy, "per the pact"; Suol tells Seishiro not to pry. Viral reveals Lordgenome gave him an undying body and that Enkidu regenerates by draining the Mucus's life force. Himari hears Vasage, who fires the energy stored for the warp and erases most of the enemy; Viral concedes defeat and withdraws, earning grudging respect as a pack leader. In a corridor, Seishiro sees through Himari's worry about hearing Vasage and the Mucus, explains that Vasage is asleep and that no one at Connect has ever spoken with him, and reminds her he is her partner; she promptly asks for help with her report. Days later Exia goes to guard Connect's Earth branch, Yoko becomes the Earth Defense Class's homeroom teacher in space, and the fleet docks with the Ark Ship and warps.

## Scene 42 — The Boy from Planet Zi
On Planet Zi, Van finds Fiona and the Organoid Zeke in ruins; Fiona remembers only her name. Moonbay (a transporter) and her bodyguard Irvine meet them, and Irvine wants Zeke. Viola's bandits attack; Zeke fuses with a scrapped Shield Liger and Van pilots it for the first time, driving them off. Irvine and Moonbay warn that Zeke would bring bandits to Van's village; Moonbay offers to hire Van as a bodyguard, Fiona insists on coming, and Van goes to say goodbye to his sister.

## Scene 43 — Roots of Ambition
A VOTOMS prologue: in the Astragius Galaxy's Hundred Years' War, Chirico is transferred to the Red Shoulders, training on Planet Odon under Pailsen. Pailsen calls Chirico an "abnormal survivor," one in 25 billion; the skeptical Major Inge Riemann orders Gregore, Murza and Byman to kill him, but guns miss or jam. Riemann tries to blow up the whole section; the four escape in ATs, Chirico blinding the control room, and a riot breaks out. Pailsen halts the attack on Chirico, and Riemann plans to dispose of all four at Sunsa; an Intelligence Ministry spy has leaked unit data. A VOTOMS-style "next time" preview closes the scene.

## Scene 44 — Hero Lamuness's New Journey
Bored at home in Japan, Lamune is visited by Da Cider, who lures him back with a tip that a beautiful woman needs rescuing. In Doki Doki Space they find Natsumi trapped, and Monskars appear even though Goburiki was defeated; Natsumi frees herself with her Power Suit and finishes one off. Milk and Lesqua arrive in the Hulk Gunship, furious; Da Cider tricks Lamuness into staying behind and flies off with Natsumi to claim the "reward." Cocoa suspects the whole thing was a trap, likely by a revived Goburiki, while Milk lectures Lamuness.

## Scene 45 — The Terror of the DG Cells
Domon, chasing DG Cells across space on a Connect job, rides with the smuggler Bouleuse Gotho, who refuses murder and drugs and calls him "Domon, pal." On an asteroid, the Death Army and a Gundam Head attack; Domon summons God Gundam and wipes them out while two AT pilots guard the way out. Gotho, now a witness, is tied to Connect; Domon hires him personally to gather DG Cell intelligence, paid by results, and Gotho would like Domon to hunt pirates in return.

## Scene 46 — Remnants of the Dark Dragon Clan
After the war on Earthtia, Dark Dragon remnants keep raiding; Galden, a Ryu Knight of Dark Dragon blood, forms a unit of reformed Dark Dragon soldiers and leaves for Doki Doki Space to stop Dark Dragon pirates as atonement. He fights a pirate ship alone, Class Changes Steru and forces their surrender with "Submit to my command," then regrets the vulgar words. His aide explains that the Dark Dragon Realm teaches its people to obey only strength and suggests the fastest path to peace is for Galden to become master of the Realm; Galden says nothing.

## Scene 47 — The White Knight
On Planet Zi, where aliens are secretly contacting Guylos and Connect is approaching the Helic Republic, Suzaku wakes with Cecile and the Lancelot after a "dimensional warp" (he was chasing Lelouch). Bandits attack a village and chase Lloyd; Suzaku fights them and Major Karl Schubaltz joins in. Suzaku spares the bandits' cockpits and Karl respects him for it. Since they cannot go home, Karl offers to take them into his unit, citing sinister moves inside Guylos; Suzaku accepts, and Lloyd's jab about Suzaku siding with Britannia shows his complicated past.

## Character state at end of episode
- Connect Force (White Base, Macross Quarter and allies) has warped from the solar system toward the Frontier system; the Warp Gate is guarded by colony and Federation forces, and Exia guards Connect's Earth branch.
- Vasage is a living creature and Connect's top secret ally who controls warps; he exhausted himself and slept for days. No one at Connect has ever spoken with him, but Himari can hear him and the Mucus.
- Viral is in this world, commands the Mucus by strength, has an undying body from Lordgenome, wants his helmet back from Kamina, and retreated swearing revenge; he seems to target Vasage.
- Seishiro tells Himari he is her partner; she leans on him (even for reports). Yoko is now the Earth Defense Class's homeroom teacher in space.
- Van, Fiona and Zeke travel with Moonbay (Van as her hired bodyguard) and Irvine, who still covets Zeke; Viola's bandits know about Zeke.
- Chirico, Gregore, Byman and Murza are marked men in the Red Shoulders; Pailsen protects Chirico as an abnormal survivor, and Riemann plans to get rid of the other three at Sunsa.
- Da Cider has run off with Natsumi; Lamuness is with the Arara sisters and Tama-Q, and Monskars suggest Goburiki may be back.
- Domon works for Connect against the DG Cells and has hired smuggler Bouleuse Gotho as an informant.
- Galden leads a Dark Dragon unit in Doki Doki Space and holds captured pirates; he is torn over ruling his kin by force.
- Suzaku, Cecile and Lloyd are in Karl Schubaltz's Guylos unit on Planet Zi; Karl trusts Suzaku completely.
