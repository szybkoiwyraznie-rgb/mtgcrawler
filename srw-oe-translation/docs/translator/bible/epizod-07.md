# Episode 07 — Zero's gambit saves New Helic City; the force heads for Doki Doki Space

Episode 7 is the climax of the first Planet Zi arc (Zoids: Chaotic Century) and the debut of the Code Geass cast. With Emperor Zepplin II dying, Marshal Prozen launches an all-out invasion of the Helic Republic; Zero, already a Connect agent embedded with the Republic, plans a feigned abandonment of Mt. Ossa Fortress and then blows it up together with the volcano, wrecking several Imperial divisions in a single day. Suzaku Kururugi, stranded on Zi and serving under Major Karl Schubaltz, fights the Connect Force and discovers that Zero is on this planet too. Van defeats Raven, whose Saber Tiger dies under him, and the war ends when the boy prince Rudolph, newly crowned after his grandfather's death, orders a ceasefire on a planet-wide broadcast. Side missions follow: Van's group escorts a covert ruins survey through Imperial territory (Desert Arcobaleno bandits, the first Mucus on Zi, a mysterious watcher who is Hiltz, Fiona's and Himari's shared vision of the Death Saurer), and Van's party joins the Connect Force. Zero finds Kallen and Rakshata and folds them into the force, and the force then warps to Doki Doki Space, where newly assigned agent Domon Kasshu leads a purge of DG Cells and a Mucus Gate hidden in an asteroid. The episode picks up from Episode 6, where Louise asked Van's group for help, and ends with the force sailing for Earthtia (the Lamune & 40 / Ryu Knight worlds) in Episode 8.

Note: Scenes 56, 59, 63 and 64 are not new content. They are alternate/branch copies of lines from Scenes 55, 58 and 62 (the game re-uses them for branching or for which units were deployed). Translate them identically to the matching lines in the main scenes.

## Scene 55 — Episode 7: Mt. Ossa Fortress (New Helic City / Presidential Office; Mt. Ossa Fortress, heights near the fortress)
- Present: Louise Teresa Camford, Dr. D, Gunther Prozen, Zero, C.C., Bright Noa, Albatro Null Eiji Asuka, Seishiro Kusanagi, Himari Yaegaki, Rob Herman, Gai, Sayla Mass, Kai Shiden, Koji Kabuto, Amuro Ray, Kamina, Ryo Shiba, Alan Igor, Suzaku Kururugi, Lloyd Asplund, Cecile Croomy, Karl Lichten Schubaltz, Fraw Bow, Mirai Yashima, Oscar Dublin, Marker Clan, Kenichi Minezaki, Shinobu Asaoka, Ken Taiga, Domon Kasshu, Isao Ota, Mikiyasu Shinshi, Leena Toros.
- Synopsis: An opening narration sets the stakes: Emperor Zepplin II is dying, Prozen holds real power, and an Imperial landing force hidden inside a giant fake iceberg is creeping toward the capital under cover of "jamming snow". Louise oversees the evacuation with Dr. D, then calls Prozen, who demands unconditional surrender ("surrender or be wiped out") with a four-hour deadline, noon Republic Standard Time. Zero steps in and promises they will not lose. At the war council, Bright reports that the ISTO warp-out zone is secured, and Dr. D warns that Prozen is chasing a sealed, ancient Zoidian power. Zero and C.C. are formally introduced: Zero joined Connect "as the Black Knights" to find a way home, has been quietly exposing corrupt Connect staff, and invites anyone to shoot him in the back if he turns evil. The heroes size him up: Amuro senses he is lying about something but is not bad, Kamina feels he is carrying something heavy, and Dr. D teases C.C. for her oddly old-lady calm. Zero lays out a plan with a suspiciously low casualty estimate that writes off the fortress itself; Herman objects, and Louise backs Zero. On the Imperial side, Suzaku, Lloyd and Cecile receive a captured Gustav as a mobile HQ. Lloyd quietly saves the HQ from Cecile's sandwiches, and Schubaltz reveals that he hopes not to use Suzaku at all and senses that things are going too smoothly. The mission itself is a holding action on the key heights: the White Base buys time while the Republic's garrison withdraws, then retreats in good order. Schubaltz reads the retreat as a planned move and urges Prozen to stop at the second line and postpone the sea landing.
- Translator notes: The briefing is serious political drama with light comic relief (Himari: "too advanced for me", "lots of people like that around here"; Lloyd and Cecile's sandwich gag, where his aside "(Phew… that was close. The whole HQ was almost wiped out…)" means her cooking is lethal, a running Code Geass joke). Zero's motto 弱者を助け、悪を否定する ("We help the weak and reject evil") is Zero's established Black Knights line; translate it as close to the official dub as you can ("The Black Knights stand with the weak and reject evil" or similar) and keep it identical when it recurs in Scene 57. 正義の味方 = "allies of justice" (dub phrasing). C.C. introduces herself as "C.C." with the reading シーツー in parentheses; drop the reading in English. Ota's 撃たれたい奴から前に出て来い is classic Ota trigger-happiness. Leena's "Offense is the best defense!" Schubaltz is honorable and friendly toward Suzaku. Prozen is a cold villain. Louise and Herman are close (she slips from "Rob" to "Colonel Herman", a hint of their romance in the anime).
- New names: ジャミングスノー → jamming snow (descriptive; medium). 共和国標準時 → Republic Standard Time (high). 第１装甲師団 → First Armored Division (high). ツェッペリンⅡ世 → Emperor Zepplin II (Zoids dub spelling Zepplin; medium). ルドルフ殿下 → Prince Rudolph (dub; high). アイアンコングＳＳ → Iron Kong SS (Schubaltz custom; keep SS; medium). 白兜 (Scene 57) → see there.

## Scene 57 — Episode 7: Schubaltz's Offensive (First Armored Division HQ; hills outside New Helic City)
- Present: Karl Lichten Schubaltz, Suzaku Kururugi, Lloyd Asplund, Cecile Croomy, Imperial officers, Irvine, Van Flyheight, Fiona Elisi Linette, Moonbay, Zeke, Alan Igor, Sara Yuki, Ryo Shiba, Shinobu Fujiwara, Masato Shikibu, Rob Herman, Raven (mentioned), Zero, C.C.; reaction lines from Naomi Fluegel, Ruuji Familon, Noa Izumi, Asuma Shinohara, Hero Lamuness, Tama-Q, Shiro Amada, Chirico Cuvie, Kusanagi, Himari.
- Synopsis: The narration explains that the Republic has abandoned the trap-laden fortress. Schubaltz notices that the main force has destroyed almost no real enemy Zoids, smells a trap, and orders the divisions back, though he expects glory-hungry generals to ignore him. Irvine grumbles about fighting for the Republic while Van and Fiona insist Prozen must be stopped; Irvine caves ("Saying it like that, a man can't back down!"), and the group complains that Dr. D has posted them on the most important route. The Cyber Beast Force (Dancouga) arrives as "backup", which annoys Irvine, and Moonbay asks to be protected because she's "just the supply girl". Suzaku and Schubaltz sortie as the elite strike, and Herman calls in a red Saber Tiger shredding the line and asking for Van by name. Van leaves to face Raven while the others hold. In the battle quotes, Noa is unafraid of the gorilla (Iron Kong) because "big gorillas are nice to delicate girls" and gripes that she isn't pretty enough for mercy; Lamuness is proud that Schubaltz senses his "Hero power"; Suzaku begs Moonbay to withdraw, and Chirico deadpans that the army never taught him to surrender early. Kusanagi notes that Suzaku moves like a trained swordsman. At Zero's appointed hour, Mt. Ossa volcano erupts and the fortress explodes, burying divisions, freezing Zoid combat systems, and setting up air raids that wipe out the supply dumps. Schubaltz admits he has been completely outplayed, and Lloyd remarks that it looks like Zero's work. Zero and C.C. appear in the Gawain, having destroyed Schubaltz's rear guard. Zero spares Schubaltz to test whether he is Prozen's man, offers him safe withdrawal, and tells a furious Suzaku that they will one day be comrades on this world. Schubaltz accepts and asks Suzaku about Zero ("a famous terrorist"). Alone, Zero tells C.C. that the Connect Force holds the key to getting home and that the White Base has gone to the coast: "checkmate".
- Translator notes: Tense military chess with bursts of comedy (Irvine and Dr. D, Moonbay's money talk: "I'll be billing you for every resupply", "hazard pay"). Suzaku and Zero are bitter enemies (Code Geass R1/R2): Suzaku calls him a terrorist who manipulates good people, and Zero keeps calm superiority. Keep Zero's chess metaphor ("checkmate") and his formal register. Zero says the volcano idea came from "a renowned retired Republic officer" (implicitly Dr. D). C.C. calls Lancelot 白兜. Note that Zero switches to 俺 when alone with C.C. ("the key to our way home"), which is Lelouch's private voice, so make it more casual. ゼロォォ! is Suzaku's anime-style scream.
- New names: 白兜 → "the white knight" or "the white helmet" (C.C.'s nickname for the Lancelot; there is no fixed dub term, so I suggest "the white knight" (low)). マウントオッサ火山 → Mt. Ossa volcano (high). ガウェイン is in the glossary (Gawain).

## Scene 58 — Episode 7: Battle for the Capital (Helic Bay coast / Prozen's iceberg base; Republic HQ; New Helic City; Presidential Office)
- Present: Gunther Prozen, Raven, Shadow, Bright Noa, Mirai, Oscar, Fraw, Marker, Rob Herman, Kusanagi, Himari, Van, Zeke, Fiona, Louise, Rudolph Gerhalt Zepplin III, Zero, C.C., Dr. D, Irvine, Moonbay, Shinobu Fujiwara, Ryo Shiba; battle quotes by Ruuji, Kamina, Yoko, Simon, Noa, Asuma, Judau, Kotaro Kirigakure, Rikiya Ryuzaki, Yosuke Kazamatsuri, Killy, Shingo, Remy, Goro, Yoji, Kenichi, Taro, Adeu, Ken Taiga, Domon, Kira, Amuro, Sayla.
- Synopsis: Prozen's landing force is stalled by the Connect Force at the shoreline; Raven ran wild alone on the flank, so Prozen recalls him to deal with the Connect Force ("lone wolves are useless in organized warfare"). Raven toys with the heroes until Van arrives, and the two of them duel ("crybaby Van"). The Saber Tiger reaches its limit; Raven refuses to let it retreat, and the Zoid dies while Fiona cries out. Raven leaves with Shadow, vowing to finish Van next time, and Prozen decides to "hurry the plan" now that the Republic has an Organoid user. When the volcano blows, Prozen coolly writes off several divisions as a cheap way to purge incompetent officers. Louise calls to demand a ceasefire; Prozen refuses and threatens to burn the city with his own troops, but Prince Rudolph interrupts on all channels. The Emperor died at 11 a.m., and Rudolph, as heir, orders the attack stopped. Prozen retreats. Afterward, Herman jokes that "they lost on their own", Moonbay wonders how much to bill the President, Van calls Herman "old man Herman", Himari corrects him to "big brother Herman", and Herman insists on "Colonel". Zero denies engineering the Emperor's death, though C.C. admits he arranged for Rudolph's speech to be broadcast. Fearing Prozen will target the boy emperor, they decide to send Van's group into the Empire as covert "special envoys", with a ruins tour for Zeke and Fiona that Dr. D obviously wants for himself.
- Translator notes: Tragic beat for Raven's Saber Tiger (Raven abuses his Zoid, which mirrors Chaotic Century, where Raven's Saber Tiger is destroyed and he later gets the Geno Saurer). Van's theme: Zoids are partners, not tools. Comic battle quotes: Noa barking "Grrr… woof!" at the tiger on Asuma's advice to stare it down; Yoji quoting a karate autobiography; Remy and Killy mocking Prozen for commanding from his ship; Adeu's knightly code 騎士道大原則ひと～つ！ ("Knight's Code, Rule Number One!") is a Ryu Knight catchphrase that must stay consistent; Domon invokes the Shuffle Alliance. Rudolph's broadcast should sound dignified but youthful. The ハーマンのおっさん/お兄さん gag: Herman is touchy about being called old.
- New names: ルドルフ・ゲアハルト・ツェッペリンⅢ世 → Rudolph Gerhalt Zepplin III (glossary entry is empty; Zoids wiki/dub spelling; medium). ヘリック湾 → Helic Bay (high). シャッフル同盟 → Shuffle Alliance (official G Gundam; high). 騎士道大原則 → Knight's Code (Ryu Knight; medium). ゴウタイガー → Go-Tiger (Ganbaruger combining mech mentioned by Kotaro; low). キングエレファン → King Elephan (Rikiya's mech; low).

## Scene 60 — Search for Zoid Eve (Imperial wasteland toward the Garil Plateau; Garil Ruins; canyon)
- Present: Van, Fiona, Irvine, Moonbay, Dr. D, Kusanagi, Himari, Viola, Rosso, Hiltz (as "???"), Ambient (as "???"), Zeke.
- Synopsis: The narration covers the ceasefire. Prozen plays the loyal regent, hunts bandits and lets the generals take the blame, and the ISTO military alliance is scrapped, though it is all set-up for a new plot. Van's group, as Republic envoys, tours ancient ruins in the Gustav with Exart flying escort. Dr. D explains that Rare Hertz scrambles sensors and has secretly coated everyone's machines; he has also re-tuned Irvine's Command Wolf, and the "perfect tuning" Irvine was proud of turns out to be Dr. D's work. Fiona and Himari have become friends (cake and tea). Kusanagi orders Himari to practice landing on the moving Gustav; she stalls, then hears something, and when he blurts "Himari Sensor!" she reflexively does a "Bzzzzt!" sound effect, then scolds him for setting up the gag so seriously. The Desert Arcobaleno (Viola in a Redler, Rosso in a Red Horn) ambushes them for Zeke. Kusanagi spots Rosso as ex-Imperial military, and Rosso retreats in good humor ("Don't die, kid! You beat the Desert Arcobaleno!"). Sleeper Zoids infected by Mucus then attack, watched by an unseen "???" who admires "the beauty of the strong". At the Garil Ruins, Fiona touches a stone tablet fragment and sees the Death Saurer; Himari, touching Fiona, glimpses it striding through a burning city. Kusanagi snaps at Himari's speculation that the Death Saurer might absorb Mucus, and she admits the Mucus voices really frighten her. Later he apologizes, and the two reaffirm that they trust each other with their lives. After more fruitless ruins, Fiona asks to join the Connect Force, Van, Irvine, Moonbay and Dr. D agree, and Kusanagi arranges it.
- Translator notes: Warm road-trip comedy that turns eerie. The "Himari Sensor" is a running gag, and the term must match the glossary ("Himari Sensor"); her ブブゥゥゥンッ is an antenna/sensor noise ("Bzzzzt!" or "Bwoooong!"). 先輩も結構、天然ですよね = "You're pretty spacey yourself, you know." The Kusanagi–Himari mentor/partner bond deepens. Rosso and Viola are colorful rogues, not evil. In Chaotic Century they are ex-Imperial soldiers who become bandits. Hiltz and Ambient are unnamed ("???") here, so keep "???" as the speaker label and give no hints. The Death Saurer vision foreshadows the arc climax. Moonbay's "Expecting too much is the worst thing you can do" earns a rebuke from Van.
- New names: none (Garil Ruins/Plateau, Rare Hertz, Desert Arcobaleno, Redler and Red Horn are in the glossary). 石碑の欠片 → tablet fragment (descriptive).

## Character state at end of episode
- The Helic Republic and the Guylos Empire are in a ceasefire. Rudolph Gerhalt Zepplin III is the new young emperor and favors peace and distance from ISTO.
- Prozen is outwardly loyal to Rudolph and accommodating toward Connect, but is secretly planning to revive the Death Saurer ("the plan") and probably to eliminate Rudolph. Zero, Bright and the others suspect this.
- Zero (Lelouch) is a Connect agent and the Republic's de facto chief strategist. He has not yet formally joined the Connect Force and says he will later. Only C.C. (and the player) see him unmasked.
- Suzaku serves Schubaltz in the Empire, knows Zero is on Zi, and regards him as a terrorist. Zero claims they will "fight side by side" one day. Schubaltz respects Zero, is not Prozen's creature, and is now in command of the Imperial army.
- Kallen and Rakshata have joined the Connect Force. Kallen reveres Zero and does not know (as far as this world is concerned) that he is Lelouch.
- Van, Fiona, Zeke, Irvine, Moonbay (paid courier) and Dr. D have joined the Connect Force; they are still searching for Zoid Eve and Fiona's memories.
- Raven has lost his Saber Tiger, holds a grudge against Van, and is still with Prozen.
- Hiltz (unnamed "???") and Ambient are secretly observing Van's group and are linked to the Mucus-infected Sleeper Zoids. Nobody knows who they are.
- Fiona and Himari share psychic sensitivity (the Death Saurer vision); Himari now openly admits that the Mucus voices frighten her. Kusanagi and Himari have agreed to be honest with each other.
- Rosso and Viola (Desert Arcobaleno) have been defeated but respect Van; they are not enemies to the death.
- Domon Kasshu, a newlywed who left home to hunt DG Cells, has joined the Connect Force. The DG Cell source in Doki Doki Space (a Mucus Gate) is destroyed, but more gates may exist.
- The force is en route to Earthtia (Doki Doki Space); Adeu (Ryu Knight) is already with the force and protective of Earthtia.
