# Episode 22 — Connect Force destroys the Devil Gundam, the Keroro Platoon is reunited, the Dankets join, and a strike team stops the colony laser

DG-infected Mucus Gates are surrounding the Earth Sphere, and Connect Force finds the core: a giant Mucus Gate hidden in an asteroid that houses the Devil Gundam. Macross Quarter breaks through, White Base destroys the gate, and Domon and Turner's team takes on the Devil Gundam as it bursts out of the asteroid. Domon is pushed to the brink by its evolving regeneration until his comrades rally him, and he unlocks the Sekiha Tenkyo God Finger to destroy it. On Earth, the rest of the Keroro Platoon (Tamama, Giroro, Kululu, Dororo) and Angol Mois arrive to rescue Keroro and Natsumi, but their warp device is absorbed by a Mucus Gate, so they stay and join Connect Force. The Dankets help the Eldran teams destroy a Mucus Gate under Japan and enroll in Ms. Yoko's class, while Gihren moves Axis toward Earth and a small Turner-led unit stops Conscon's attempt to fire the Solar Ray.

## Scene 170 — Showdown with the DG Cells R1
Himari detects a giant Mucus Gate in the asteroid belt, and Domon senses the Devil Gundam there. Cathy splits the attack force into three units: one clears the approach, one hits the giant gate and one destroys the Devil Gundam. Macross Quarter must break through waves of Death Army and Mucus to reach the asteroid within 10 turns. The enemy pulls back to regroup ("the calm before the storm"), and Jeffrey moves to phase two.

## Scene 172 — Showdown with the DG Cells R2
White Base leads the attack on the asteroid, where a giant Mucus Gate grew underground before breaking through the bedrock. Macross Quarter holds off reinforcements in the rear but lets a few through. If the 12-turn limit runs out, the whole area turns into Devil Gundam. On success, the enemies flee, energy spikes inside the asteroid, and Domon says "he" is coming out. White Base falls back, and Domon and Turner take over as the final unit.

## Scene 173 — Devil Gundam - Final Round
The Devil Gundam emerges, heading for Earth. Domon summons God Gundam ("Come fooorth! GUNDAAAAAM!!") and fights it with Giga Nektons and Gundam Heads around it. The Gundam Heads fire every 3 turns (a special trap). The Devil Gundam's regeneration keeps evolving, and Domon nearly breaks. Seishiro, Kamina, Simon and the Eldran kids remind him that his fists led them all this way. Domon declares he wants to protect everyone, unlocks the Sekiha Tenkyo God Finger and destroys the Devil Gundam. In the lounge, Domon explains that the move came from making Master Asia's Sekiha Tenkyoken his own. Himari wants a secret art for the Exart, and Domon drags everyone, including a reluctant gamer Lamuness, to intensive training. The search for other giant Mucus Gates hidden in the solar system continues.

## Scene 177 — God Keron Is Here!
Kululu's "Thataway-Thisaway Gate" brings Tamama, Giroro, Kululu, Dororo and Mois into a forest on Earth, among Mucus. Aboard White Base, Keroro is caught trying to sneak into the Guntank (Irvine teases him and Natsumi yells at him for skipping cleaning duty). Keroro scouts with the Zoid team, the platoon reunites and forms God Keron ("Ultimate Union! God Keron!"), and Mois one-shots a Giga Nekton with her hammer. At the station, Kululu reveals that the warp device was absorbed by a Mucus Gate, and that he hacked Connect's data bank in three minutes. Seishiro asks him for security advice instead. The platoon joins Connect Force until a new device can be built, and Irvine narrates that "Sgt. Keroro's dream of returning home was crushed".

## Scene 178 — Eldran's Heroes Assemble!
The Eldran teams attack a giant Mucus Gate under Japan. Takako theorizes that Japan's volcanic energy feeds the gates, which makes them regenerate faster. A Giga Nekton ambushes Raijin-Oh, and the Dankets save them in Perfect Daiteioh and announce that they are joining Connect Force. Hiryu is aloof and Momotaro chafes at him. Afterward, the Dankets are enrolled in Ms. Yoko's fifth-grade class. Yoko explains that she dreams of being a teacher, Tsutomu blushes as her assistant, and Momotaro is horrified to learn that there will be extra lessons.

## Scene 179 — Colony Laser Reactivated
Legitimate Zeon gains colonial support. Gihren lets dissidents emigrate and launches an operation to drop Axis on Earth, which Kycilia condemns but cannot stop. Intel says Side 3's Solar Ray is being reactivated, likely to hit Connect Station, so Turner leads a small detached unit through the trade lanes while the main force heads for Axis. Conscon, still bitter over Solomon and Dozle, defends the control ship Gwazine with the Black Tri-Stars, who rejected the peace. Gaia calls Quattro "Char" and Bernie faces his old idols. Connect Force downs the Gwazine within 8 turns and races off to rejoin the main force.

## Character state at end of episode
- The Devil Gundam is destroyed, and the DG Cell threat is over, though Domon vows to keep training in case it returns. God Gundam has the Sekiha Tenkyo God Finger.
- Several giant Mucus Gates may still be hidden in the solar system. A large-scale search is underway, and the gates on Earth (especially under Japan) draw on the planet's energy.
- The full Keroro Platoon (Keroro, Tamama, Giroro, Kululu, Dororo) and Angol Mois are stranded and have joined Connect Force until Kululu can rebuild a warp device. God Keron can use Hellmageddon 1/1M.
- Kululu has hacked Connect's data bank and may advise on security. Tamama calls Keroro "Mr. Sergeant" and Natsumi "Natchy", Kululu calls Keroro "Sarge", and Mois calls him "Uncle".
- The Dankets (Momotaro, Kakeru, Mai, Hiryu) have joined Connect Force and attend Ms. Yoko's class with the other Eldran kids.
- Himari and Seishiro want to develop a secret art for the Exart's Raiko Blade, and Domon is training everyone.
- Gihren is moving Axis toward Earth, and Connect Force's main body is heading to stop it. Kycilia condemned the operation but has not acted.
- The Solar Ray firing was stopped. Conscon and the Black Tri-Stars (Gaia, Ortega, Mash) were defeated and retreated, and Gaia grudgingly admits Connect Force's strength.
