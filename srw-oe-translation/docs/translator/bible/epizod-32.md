# Episode 32 — Into the Recoral Galaxy: the first spear strikes the Mucus Fortress

Operation Extend is under way. Connect Force warps into the Recoral Galaxy, the Mucus homeland, where humanity has built a huge bridgehead of Warp Gates. The first target is the Commune, the star system where the Sages and Vasage's kind live. Chief Suol Douglas and Dr. Kusanagi explain the stakes: the whole galaxy is effectively the inside of the Mucus's body, and outsiders can only warp in because Vasage is in contact with the Commune, which "unlocks the door from the inside". A fortress-class Mucus, a fully grown Mucus Gate that seeds other universes, blocks the route. Former enemies (Le Cain's Grados fleet, the Balarant fleet, Pentagona's regulars) now fight beside Connect Force, which underlines the "humanity united" theme. The episode has two story missions (fleet breakthrough, then the Fortress core with its space-expanding shockwave trap) and two side missions (Turner rescuing a Pentagona unit; Ultrasaurus escorting the slow Ark Ship). It sets up Episode 33 (arrival at the Commune and the Sages' judgment) and starts the long "final push" arc that ends in Episode 34. Tone: confident and rousing before the final battle, with light comic relief from Keroro's platoon, Remy and Moonbay.

- Synopsis: The narration recaps Operation Extend: Warp Gates are in place, the bridgehead is secure, and the first objective is the Commune, the system of the Sages and Vasage's kind. A fortress-class Mucus stands on the route. The Ark Ship docks at Station 4. Himari mentions that Vasage felt a pang of homesickness during the warp ("Missing home must be the same everywhere in the universe!"). At the bridgehead Suol briefs everyone. Zero notes that the warp-out point was chosen by Himari and Vasage. Kamina sums it up as "so it's going to plan, right?!" and Boota squeals. He praises them grudgingly ("no unit is more reliable as a friend"). First battle: break through the defensive line. Afterwards Bright's White Base charges the fortress while the Macross Quarter switches the Macross Cannon to long-range mode to draw its attention. Narration closes: this is humanity's first long spear thrown at the Mucus, and also its last.
- Translator notes: The tone is epic and upbeat. The fleet briefing must sound military-crisp. Le Cain, once a bitter enemy (Layzner), is now an ally: keep his arrogance, but make the praise sincere. Battle-quote highlights: the Keroro platoon gag (Keroro claims his racing heart is "high morale"; Kululu calls his bluff with his heart rate; Giroro snaps "Focus!"); Jin calls his shaking hands 武者震い, so use "the shakes before a fight", "battle jitters"; Killy wants "a long vacation" (this pays off in Ep 34, where GoShogun gets a vacation and vanishes). ## Scene 243 — Episode 32: The Mucus Fortress (Recoral Galaxy / Mucus Fortress)
- Present: Mirai Yashima, Himari Yaegaki, Bright Noa, Oscar Dublin, Marker Clan, Fraw Bow, Seishiro Kusanagi, Jin Hyuga, Lamuness, Shingo Hojo, Remy Shimada, Adeu Waltham, Kotaro Kirigakure, Zero, C.C., Sgt. Keroro, Chirico Cuvie, Tama-Q, Ruuji Familon, Kei, Gai, Go, Ryoma Nagare, Hayato Jin, Benkei Kuruma, Shiro Amada.
- Synopsis: The fortress turns out to be a single gigantic, overgrown Mucus Gate packed with Mucus, a terminal that sends Mucus units and Gates to other universes. Himari reveals that there are countless fortresses like it in the galaxy, so only destroying this one's core will stop the flow. Mid-battle the gate unleashes a burst that flings every unit far away, though the crews feel no impact. Seishiro deduces that space itself was pushed out (sudden spatial expansion, an application of warp energy) and that nothing can block it short of sealing off space. Remy jokes about being "shoved away by a shy fellow". Himari spots the recharge window, and they push in between bursts, which also do damage (special trap: every 3 turns, 2000 damage to all enemies of the gate). The core falls, the fortress collapses under its own weight, and the leaderless Mucus scatter. Zero says not to pursue. Bright leaves mop-up to other fleets and sets course for the Commune.
- Translator notes: The tone is tense, then triumphant. Keep "Mucus Gate" consistent. Zero frets that this spatial ability may affect "the plan", and C.C. needles him ("worry about later after you beat it"). Their usual banter: she is dry and he is irritated. Kotaro's "That's cheating!" gag is childish indignation. Lamuness's catchphrase オレは今、モーレツに～ ("I'm fired up like crazy right now!") recurs in Ep 34, so keep it consistent. Chirico's line in the source has a typo (火力をさせて, probably 集中させて), so translate it as "concentrate fire". The trap text in the victory condition is a UI string: keep the ＜特殊トラップ＞ token.
- New names: none.

## Character state at end of episode
- Connect Force is inside the Recoral Galaxy and has broken the fortress guarding the Commune. Former enemies (Grados under Le Cain, Balarant, Pentagona regulars) are loyal allies in Operation Extend.
- Humanity now knows that countless fortress-class Mucus Gates exist and that they can expand space as a weapon.
- Himari and Vasage are essential: they choose warp-out points and are the "key" to the galaxy's barrier. Himari still relays Vasage's feelings (homesickness).
- The Ark Ship lags behind with the Ultrasaurus (Herman, Moonbay, Schubaltz). Its field is needed for the final approach.
- Zero is already thinking ahead to "the plan" and how the Mucus spatial ability affects it.
- Turner (Kyao, Daba and co.) works as a roaming fire brigade between sectors.
- Alto, Sheryl and Ranka: affectionate rivalry. Both girls worry openly about Alto.
- Nobody yet knows the true origin of the Mucus or what the Commune will demand.
