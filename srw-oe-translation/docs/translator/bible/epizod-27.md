# Episode 27 — Connect Force storms Mechanization Castle and destroys Kikaishin; in Pentagona, Olibee is made Posaydal's puppet commander and Gavlet Gablae defects

Two halves. First, the Go-Saurer / Eldran storyline climaxes: Connect Force has located the Mechanization Empire's home base (thanks largely to Himari's Mucus-sensing, which has left her exhausted), the Macross Quarter breaks the outer Mucus/Mechanized Beast defence line, and the assault group on the Turner fights through a giant Mucus Gate to Kikaishin himself. Nearly every pilot gets a "heart vs. machine" exchange with Kikaishin, the four Eldran robots finish him with the Eldran Special, and the Mechanization Empire is destroyed. Kikaishin's parting reveal matters for the main plot: he learned of the Recoral Galaxy (Mucus homeland, a Connect secret) from Connect data stolen during the Shinohara HOS incident, and Vasage visibly warns him off. Narration frames this as proof that hostile factions could swallow the Recoral Galaxy, so Connect must speed up peace with ISTO. Second half, the Heavy Metal L-Gaim storyline: Posaydal installs Daba's adoptive sister Quwasan Olibee as supreme commander of the Thirteen; Connect Force skirmishes with Nei Moh Han, Shinn reacts painfully to talk of sisters, Olibee broadcasts a propaganda speech, and Zero's plan (a decoy raid on ISTO's real HQ, a Balarant land battleship on Gastogal) succeeds with surprise help from Gavlet Gablae, who has defected out of love for Olibee and reveals the Bio-Relation System. Daba then tells him Olibee is his fiancee. Leads directly into Episode 28, the assault on the capital Sveet.

## Scene 209 — Ep. 27: Terror! The Wrath of Kikaishin (Mechanization Castle, space)
- Present: Kikaishin, Seishiro, Himari, the Saurers (Kenichi, Erika, Shinobu Asaoka, Hiromi Tachibana, Goro Ishida, Taro Shirogane, Yoji Hiyama, Takako, Saneyasu Mushanokoji, Yuka Mizuhara, Kumiko Tanabe), Mirawoo Kyao, Patlabor cast, Raijin-Oh/Ganbaruger/Daiteioh kids, and battle-dialogue from almost the whole roster; Vasage.
- Synopsis: The Turner group approaches; Himari detects a giant Mucus Gate in the castle core, which Kikaishin will use to invade other universes. Kikaishin appears (as "???" first) and welcomes them. First phase: the Mucus Gate keeps regenerating on castle energy (Patlabor comic relief: Noa whining, Ota demanding more ammo, Kanuka lecturing him) until a concentrated push stops the energy flow. Kikaishin emerges in person, swearing revenge for his past defeat by the Saurers. A long run of one-on-one exchanges: Kikaishin preaches the "Order of Steel" (mechanize everything; hearts are flaws); each pilot rebuts in character (Seishiro/Himari: an imperfect heart is why people grow; Fiona: Zoids are alive and have hearts; Domon: love machines and they gain a soul; Lamuness: Tama-Q is a machine with a heart; Amuro, Kamille, Kira, Shinn, Daba, Alto, GoShogun's "Kenta", Noa: "Labors are our friends!", Chirico, Ken: "Liger is Bio-Armor, not a machine", Raijin-Oh kids mock his 64-million-year losing streak against Eldran, Kotaro "I've been a dog before, so being a machine doesn't scare me", Yosuke admits he is scared but refuses to lose his heart, Dancouga team "order? not us", Adeu's chivalry, Ruuji, Kamina "first Beast-guy, now Machine-guy?", Zero, Masaki's "Demon God, Evil God, now a Machine God - gods are on clearance", Setsuna, Show/Chum, Heero, Keroro's meta gag). Mid-battle reveal: Kikaishin is grateful to Connect for letting him learn of the Recoral Galaxy, the Mucus homeland, by stealing Connect data during the Shinohara HOS incident; his aim is to conquer it and spread the Order of Steel to every universe. Vasage manifests and warns him not to touch the Mucus core; Kikaishin dismisses it. He revives mechanized Labors (still in police colours, to Ota's outrage) and mobile suits. Final phase: Kikaishin draws all castle energy into himself; Maria and Asuka realise this is the chance, Tsutomu sends attack points, and the four Eldran robots combine power. Kenichi's big line: "That's why we're... the hottest-blooded and the strongest!" Kikaishin is destroyed. Kenichi is starving; Taro challenges him to an eating contest. Help: God Raijin-Oh, Great Ganbaruger, King Go-Saurer and Perfect Daiteioh can now use the Eldran Special. Narration: the Mechanization Empire is gone, but Kikaishin proved other factions could absorb the Mucus and seize the Recoral Galaxy; Connect must secure cooperation with ISTO and eliminate hostile forces without delay.
- Translator notes: Hot-blooded super-robot climax; sincere, speech-heavy. Keep "Order of Steel" (glossary) consistent in every Kikaishin line. Kikaishin switches pronoun 我 to 余 with the Eldran kids; both are a regal "I" in English. Masaki's joke needs the "god" meaning, so say "Machine God" there (or "Kikaishin, the Machine God"). The 64 million years is a Raijin-Oh/Eldran series joke; keep the number. Kotaro the dog: callback to a Ganbaruger episode in which Kotaro was turned into a dog (uncertain which one); it comes back in Ep. 31. Keroro's gag: villains who come back as "Mecha-[name]" after a defeat (a tokusatsu trope); a localization like "the whole 'Mecha-Somebody returns' bit only works for a beaten villain coming back super-powered" fits. "Kenta" is GoShogun's boy hero who believes machines are friends, and the Go Flasher is GoShogun's beam. Kenichi's 熱血最強 = the series title Nekketsu Saikyo; make it a punchy title-drop ("hot-blooded and strongest" or similar) and keep it identical where it repeats (Scene 210). Hostility: Kikaishin vs. everyone; the Saurers treat him as their personal nemesis. The Recoral Galaxy is still classified; Himari remarks on it.
- New names: ケン太 → Kenta (GoShogun's Kenta Sanada; high). ロボライガー → Robo-Liger (Ken's coinage for a mechanized Liger; medium). バイオアーマー → Bio-Armor (Jushin Liger term; high). 騎士道大原則 → Grand Rule of Chivalry (Adeu's catchphrase "Grand Rule of Chivalry, number one!"; medium). 篠原ＨＯＳの事件 → the Shinohara HOS incident (glossary HOS; high).

## Scene 211 — Quwasan Olibee (Connect Station / Pentagona World; Connect Station / Briefing Room; battle space; Connect Station / Lounge; Planet Gastogal / Capital Sveet)
