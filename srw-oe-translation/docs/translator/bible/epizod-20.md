# Episode 20 — Seishiro recovers his father's original Raiko Blade, a Gundam Fight ends the feud with Zechs, Bushido and Char, and Da Cider comes back to his senses

A key episode for the original storyline and several rivalries. Connect reopens the long-sealed 203rd Warp Gate, through which Balarant once caused a Mucus catastrophe; Connect Force finds the far-side planet eaten hollow by Mucus and covered in tens of thousands of dormant Mucus Gates. Dr. Kusanagi (Seishiro's mother) reveals a secret aim: recovering the original Raiko Blade of Seishiro's late father. Himari senses it inside a giant Benthos Carrier, the very Mucus that killed him; Seishiro defeats it as "a warrior, not an avenger", reclaims the blade and recalls his father's technique, the Shiden Sword. In the Earth Sphere, Kycilia's faction declares independence as "Legitimate Zeon" and settles the question of leadership with Connect by a formal Gundam Fight (Setsuna vs Bushido, Heero vs Zechs, Amuro vs Char, Domon refereeing); an Invader/Mucus attack interrupts, everyone fights together, and Quattro returns to Connect Force, reconciling with Sayla. Amuro and Quattro receive the Nu Gundam and Sazabi at Von Braun and fight off Neo Zeon (Rakan, Ple Two). Finally Da Cider, controlled by Goburiki, attacks Central Connect Station and is brought back to his senses by Lamuness; Goburiki's full invasion (Episode 21) is imminent. Continues from Episode 19's Axis aftermath.
