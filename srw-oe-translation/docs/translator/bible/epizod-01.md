# Episode 01 — Connect starts recruiting allies: Patlabor's rogue-Labor mystery, Odessa, the Alex launch and a drill with the Earth Defense Class

Connect "moves to direct action": Suol reveals that Connect, unable to mass-produce anything matching Exart (its core unit cannot be copied), will team up with outside organizations that share its goals, and that Himari is to be trained as Exart's co-pilot. The episode is a sampler of storylines. Patlabor: a joint JSDF/SV2 exercise is wrecked by unmanned rogue Labors, beginning the HOS (Patlabor: The Movie) mystery, which ends with Division 2 of SV2 seconded to Connect. Mobile Suit Gundam: White Base fights the Black Tri-Stars and Amuro stops M'Quve's H-bomb at Odessa. 08th MS Team / War in the Pocket: Shiro's team guards the Alex shuttle launch against the Cyclops Team, then rescues a Medea. Matchless Raijin-Oh: Exart trains with the Earth Defense Class at Hinobori Academy, and Raijin-Oh later helps SV2. The thread linking them is the string of unmanned Labor rampages, Connect's mysterious influence (rumors that it trades with outer space), and Connect gathering allied teams. Episode 02 continues on Earth with the rampage investigation and the Eldran teams.

## Scene 3 — (no title) (Connect Station / Earth Sphere; JSDF Fuji Training Area)
- Present: Suol Douglas, Seishiro, Himari, Noa Izumi, Asuma Shinohara, Isao Ota, Kiichi Goto, Mikiyasu Shinshi, Hiromi Yamazaki, Kanuka Clancy, JSDF soldiers.
- Synopsis: Briefing: Himari has pilot aptitude and will share control of Exart so Seishiro can command; Connect will cooperate with like-minded organizations. Suol teases Seishiro not to "make a move" on the cute girl, then laughs at how literal he is and says loosening up would make him a great leader; Seishiro: "...I'll work on it." Patlabor intro narration. Exart drops onto the Fuji range without a parachute in strong wind while Himari panics; her orders only said "practice makes perfect". Seishiro: "I'm trusting you with my back." She resolves to do it. JSDF soldiers grumble that Japan's neutrality limits their tech and they must rely on the Cyber Beast Force and Saotome Lab's super robots. SV2 Division 2 arrives with their usual banter (Ota hates networks, Shinshi nags about the budget, Goto leaves everything to the kids, Asuma: "A two-seater, man and woman? We can't lose, Noa"). The exercise turns live: the unmanned HAL-X10 target Labors fire real ammunition and cannot be accessed remotely. Goto can't act without orders; Seishiro takes responsibility on Connect's behalf and orders all to engage. The JSDF tells SV2 to withdraw; Goto "withdraws", adding that any obstacles on the way out may be removed by force (a sly loophole). Battle; Himari is overwhelmed ("My head's spinning!"). Afterwards Goto thanks "Mr. Connect" for handling the aftermath; Kanuka, Asuma, Ota and Shinshi speculate that Connect secretly trades with aliens and sells alien-tech weapons. Narration: no claim of responsibility; incident kept from the public; the Ministry of Defense must cooperate with other organizations.
- Translator notes: Comic banter (Suol's teasing; SV2 bickering) around a tense incident. Goto's loophole order is a classic Goto move; keep its dry irony. Noa calls her Ingram "Alphonse"; she worries Shige will scold her for scratching it. Running Patlabor gags: Shinshi's stomach/wife, Ota's trigger-happiness ("I'll make Swiss cheese of it!"). "習うより慣れろ" = "Practice makes perfect / Learn by doing". Kanuka's suspicion of Connect starts here. Reference to the HAL-X10 rampage at the JSDF exercise loosely echoes Patlabor: The Movie's opening (Labor rampage at a JSDF test).
- New names: 自衛隊 → JSDF (Japan Self-Defense Forces) (standard; high). 富士演習場 → Fuji Training Area (standard; high). 防衛省 → Ministry of Defense (high). 警視庁 → Tokyo Metropolitan Police Department (high). 特科車両二課 パトロールレイバー中隊 → Special Vehicles Section 2, Patrol Labor Company (official SV2 expansion; high). パトレイバー → Patlabor (high). 早乙女研 → Saotome Lab (short of glossary Saotome Research Institute; high). シゲ / シゲさん (斯波繁夫) → Shige (nickname; high).

## Scene 5 — Stop the H-Bomb at Odessa (Federation Operations HQ; White Base / Bridge; Zeon Base / Odessa; Zanzibar)
- Present: General Revil, M'Quve, Bright, Amuro, Kai, Hayato, Sayla, Fraw, Oscar, Marker.
- Synopsis: M'Quve threatens Revil: withdraw or he uses a hydrogen bomb, Antarctic Treaty be damned. Revil refuses to set a precedent of yielding to threats. Bright briefs: the fuse location can be found from public treaty data once the missile type is known; Kai wants to run. White Base attacks Zeon forces retreating to the spaceport. M'Quve fires the missile; Bright orders Amuro to cut the fuse with his beam saber at a single spot over the mountains. Amuro succeeds: no nuclear reaction. "Mission complete. Gundam, returning." Narration: Federation wins Odessa through sheer numbers; mobile suits mark a new era. On the Zanzibar, M'Quve: with the resources already sent to Kycilia, "Zeon can fight for another ten years!"
- Translator notes: Tense, then triumphant. Canon: Mobile Suit Gundam ep. 25 "Odessa Day". M'Quve's final line must use the glossary form "Zeon can fight for another ten years!". Mission title label reads 第１話「オデッサ２水爆を阻止せよ」 → Episode 1 "Odessa 2: Stop the H-Bomb" (the "2" = second Odessa mission).
- New names: 水爆 / 水爆ミサイル → H-bomb / H-bomb missile (standard; high). 信管 → fuse (high). レビル将軍 is in glossary (General Revil).

Jin drags Koji off to play soccer; Seishiro suspects the Chief arranged this to show him the kids' spirit, and Himari agrees Suol is a schemer.
- Translator notes: Light, cheerful. Kids talk casually; Maria is the bossy class president type. Tutorial on push-off (terrain) attacks; Raijin Flash should match the attack name used elsewhere. Hint of Seishiro's unknown past (his childhood).
- New names: 陽昇学園５年３組 → Hinobori Academy Class 5-3 (canon; high). ライジンフラッシュ → Raijin Flash (attack name; medium). 防衛隊 → Defense Force (the Eldran-era Earth defense organization led by General Takeda; medium).

## Scene 7 — Launch the Alex (Federation Base / Launch Control Room; mountain launch site; Federation Base / Hangar)
- Present: Shiro Amada, Christina Mackenzie, Terry Sanders Jr., Karen Joshua, Eledore Massis, Michel Ninorich, Hardy Steiner, Mikhail Kaminsky, Gabriel Ramirez Garcia, Andy Strauss, Federation officer.
- Synopsis: Narration: RX-78NT-1 "Alex", a Newtype-use Gundam, is to be shuttled to Libot colony in Side 6, guarded by the 08th MS Team. - Translator notes: Tense battle with warm banter. ## Character state at end of episode
- Himari is now officially Exart's co-pilot in training; she is terrified but committed after Seishiro says he trusts her with his back.
- Suol has started recruiting other organizations; SV2 Division 2 (with Shige, Sakaki and Kanuka) is now seconded to Connect and heading to space.
- Asuma is in trouble for the Hachioji raid; the government is covering up the HOS defect as a "free upgrade". Nobody knows who planted the rampage trigger.
- Kanuka and SV2 are suspicious of Connect (rumors it trades with aliens/outer space); Goto sees the secondment as an opportunity.
- Shaft's Utsumi, Kurosaki and Bud are secretly watching the Ingrams in action and relish the challenge.
- Shige never installed HOS on the Ingrams, so they are safe; Goto knew and kept it from Asuma.
- White Base has won at Odessa, beaten the Black Tri-Stars and stopped the H-bomb; M'Quve escaped to space.
- The 08th MS Team protected the Alex launch from the Cyclops Team and is heading to Jaburo on a Medea; Karen's Ground Gundam has a GM head.
- The Cyclops Team survived and failed to stop the Alex; it is on its way to Side 6.
- The Earth Defense Class (Raijin-Oh) knows Connect from TV ads and is friendly; Connect has not met the other Eldran teams in battle yet.
- Seishiro has a past he doesn't talk about (his childhood); Himari thinks Suol is a schemer.
