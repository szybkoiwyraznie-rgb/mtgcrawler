# Episode 29 — The Gravity Cannon fells the Death Stinger on Zi; Connect Force moves to the Planet of the Seal, where Cowen and Stinger reveal they want Himari

Dr. D explains the Gravity Cannon's Planetalicite shell (internal gravity, black hole effect, one-way super-gravity). Connect Force pins the Death Stinger with the Delta Formation Blockade and the Ultrasaurus hits it; its Zoid Core stops, but Hiltz has Ambient revive it, and it attacks the Ultrasaurus from underground before finally being destroyed, Hiltz promising humanity will vanish once Zoid Eve awakens. The main group leaves Zi to Herman and heads for the Planet of the Seal system, where Invaders are using Mucus Gates; Roan, now leading the Grados Expeditionary Force, pledges full cooperation. In a mixed Invader/Mucus battle, Cowen and Stinger emerge from a wormhole, say they learned of the system via ancient ruins linked to Mars, admit they lured Connect Force here and invite Himari to "become one" with them, since the road to the Recoral Galaxy stays closed to them. On the Planet of the Seal a giant worm Invader that wiped out a Grados unit is destroyed.

## Scene 223 — Gravity Cannon (Ultrasaurus, C.F. Ground Command; Zi battlefield)
Dr. D lectures on the Gravity Cannon and Planetalicite; everyone pretends to follow (Thomas bluffs and passes to Van; Moonbay admits she didn't get it; Dr. D feels dread). Moonbay calls the Ultrasaurus "my Ultrasaurus", Herman silently objects. Hiltz mocks the small force, shows off Sleeper Zoid control and a charged particle cannon charge; heroes answer with teamwork (Keroro repaying kindness, Daiteioh kids refusing the "child soldiers" jab). Van, Irvine and Thomas perform the Delta Formation Blockade; the Gravity Cannon fires, the Zoid Core is confirmed stopped, and Thomas celebrates to Fiona.

## Scene 224 — The Death Stinger's Onslaught (Ultrasaurus; Zi)
Fiona and Zeke sense something; Hiltz summons Ambient and the Death Stinger revives, burrows and rams the Ultrasaurus (Dr. D is hurt but refuses the infirmary). Battle quotes: Getter team, Koji, Dancouga (Masato's crayfish gag), Heero's "I won't kill anymore", Setsuna snapping the stinger, Leena's "nobody likes a guy who won't take a hint", Zero's "all that flourishes must fall". Hiltz briefly slips into a mocking, childish register. The Death Stinger is destroyed; Hiltz, dying, says humanity will vanish with Zoid Eve. Narration: Fiona's unease lingers, Herman's Ultrasaurus keeps sweeping, and the main group leaves Zi.

## Scene 226 — An Invitation from the Void (Connect Station; hangar; Macross Quarter in the Seal system)
Suol briefs: Invaders are using Mucus Gates in the Seal system, left thinly defended after the Grados peace. Roan reports fleets arriving; Grados has left ISTO yet backs Balarant against the Mucus; Zero thanks Grados and foresees cooperation with ISTO. Viral warns Himari and the Vasage to be careful; Seishiro agrees they are Connect's key. The Getter team and others theorize the Invaders now prioritize absorbing the Mucus and the Recoral Galaxy (Hayato: "less than a grain of sand in a desert"). After a battle with Invader-controlled Mucus (Seishiro forbids Himari from probing them), a wormhole opens: Cowen and Stinger appear, reveal they watched the Grados war, lured Connect Force here, and call for Himari; they beat Ryoma and leave through the wormhole, promising a "hellish stage".

## Scene 227 — The Devil in the Crater (Planet of the Seal; White Base)
A Grados unit with battleships has vanished; White Base finds only a huge shaft. A giant worm Invader (Devourer Type) erupts. Alan warns they must burn every cell; Go-Saurer kids squirm (Kenichi insists he isn't scared of an overgrown earthworm). The worm and the remaining Invaders are destroyed; Go urges wiping out the rest before they multiply.

- The Death Stinger is destroyed; Hiltz's fate is left ambiguous, and he still speaks of Zoid Eve.
- Fiona remains uneasy; Sleeper Zoid threats persist on Zi under Herman and the Ultrasaurus.
- Dr. D was injured in the Ultrasaurus attack but kept working.
- Roan commands the Grados Expeditionary Force and cooperates with Connect; Grados acts as a bridge to ISTO.

- Seishiro has forbidden Himari from probing Mucus minds; Viral and Hayato also warned her.
- Invaders remain on the Planet of the Seal; Connect Force continues the sweep.
