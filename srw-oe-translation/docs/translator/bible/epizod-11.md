# Episode 11 — Connect Force retakes the Ark, the Saurers face Kikaishin again, and the Solar Ray is stopped short of firing

Connect Force raids the Ark in Tokyo Bay. It has become a Mechanization Empire factory quietly building copies of SDF military Labors, and it is guarding a Mucus Gate. Division 2 and the ATs open the way past a captured Type-Zero, the Exart team destroys the Gate, and the Saurers wreck the production plant, where Kikaishin reveals he survived. Back in the colonies, Division 2 hunts Labor-armed extremists. Shaft cuts a deal with Connect, Bud and the Griffon officially join the force, and Kanuka gets her own Type-Zero. With Kycilia's tip-off, Connect Force goes after the Solar Ray, and Quattro and Kamille knock out its receiver panel. The Degwin–Revil talks then produce a ceasefire, but Gihren keeps Zeon's army intact and plays for time.

## Scene 89 — Operation Retake the Ark
After the fall of Solomon, Japan's government finally lets Connect Force into the Ark once a Mucus Gate reading turns up inside. Goto, Shinshi and Asuma point out that the rogue SDF-type Labors have no serial numbers, which suggests the Ark is producing them for the Mechanization Empire. Kanuka warns that the Empire could slowly swap out Labors all over society, the way Kenichi was once mechanized without noticing. The advance team (Division 2, Chirico, Bud, Lamuness, Keroro and Natsumi) fights through an ambush of military Labors, self-destructing Chaldea guard robots and Mechanized Beasts, then beats a captured, unmanned Type-Zero. Noa dislikes it because its face "looks like a bad guy's", and Asuma tells her to trust "wits and courage". They hold the gate until the follow-up teams pass.

## Scenes 90–91 — (variant scripts of the Ark briefing and battle; same content)

## Scene 92 — The Door That Opens in Darkness
The Exart leads Getter, Team Gurren, the Ganbaruger kids, Ken Taiga, Judau and Bernie against the Mucus Gate deep in the Ark, which the Ark keeps supplying with energy. Giga Nektons pour in. Seishiro tells Himari that worrying about the other teams shows she is getting used to combat. The Gate is silenced, and Seishiro trusts the Saurers to finish the plant.

## Scenes 93–94 — Den of the Mechanization Empire
In the undersea block, the Saurers and their allies (Domon, Shinn, Show, Koji) attack the plant. Kikaishin appears as a projection. He explains that only one of his bodies was destroyed, that he exists wherever the Empire does, and that he has absorbed the Mucus so he can spread the "Order of Steel" across universes. He sends Dark Go-Saurer (a King Go-Saurer copy) and a new Labor against them. Kinta rallies the kids with the power of teamwork. The plant is destroyed, but Kikaishin says it can be rebuilt, that his true body is outside the solar system, and that this invasion "holds special meaning" for him. Afterward, Goto announces the next job (Shinshi gets one night at home) and a new unit for Kanuka. Seishiro practices tight-space swordwork with a shinai in his cabin and almost hits Himari. The Saurers decide to join Connect Force, and Connect builds Harukaze Elementary a temporary school so King Go-Saurer can leave.

## Scene 96 — SV2 and a New Ally
In the colonies, cheap military Labors have reached extremists. Division 2, with Kanuka now in Type-Zero, raids a group hiding in a colony's outer wall that also has mobile suits and bomb-rigged guard robots. Utsumi and Kurosaki secretly funded the group and leaked it to Connect as a "gift". Bud arrives in the Griffon and declares he has joined Connect Force, which Goto confirms is official through Shaft's backing. Ota is outraged. At the station, Utsumi plays the "stolen experimental machine" line, Noa insists Bud will attend the force's school (he runs off), and Goto has Noa keep minding him. Kurosaki explains that Shaft wants combat know-how and expansion, including SSS (Shaft Security System) Labor units for colony security, and Kanuka exposes SSS as Shaft's shady private army. The hunters and the hunted now share a ship.

## Scene 97 — Operation Stop the Solar Ray
The Federation fleet heads from Solomon toward A Baoa Qu. Suol and Jeffrey brief the force on the Solar Ray, a sealed colony turned into a laser. Connect wants to prevent tit-for-tat WMD use and has also protested the Federation's use of the Solar System. Gihren tells Kycilia he will fire along the Gel Dolba line before Degwin's Great Degwin can reach Revil, since peace now would mean defeat in all but name. The Macross Quarter holds off a Zeon fleet and a Big Zam so White Base can go ahead.

## Scenes 98–99 — The Pulse of Zeta
Acting on Kycilia's intel, White Base attacks the Gwazine-class control ship. A failure branch shows the canonical disaster: Amuro's "light of hatred", the Solar Ray firing, and Degwin and Revil dying. In the main path, the firing signal can't be sent because Quattro and Kamille, on Commodore Blex's special orders, have destroyed the Solar Ray's receiver panel. The pair join Connect Force and cover White Base's escape. Aboard ship, Quattro stays guarded, Yoko and Remy appraise the new men, and Keroro wants to inspect the Hyaku Shiki and Zeta. In a corridor, Amuro insists he "knows" Quattro, who answers that he is Quattro Bajeena, nothing more, and asks him not to pry with Newtype senses. Kamille and Amuro become friends on first-name terms. The Degwin–Revil talks yield a ceasefire, but Gihren refuses to disband Zeon's army, citing popular support. The Federation holds A Baoa Qu, Federation radicals are weak after Jamitov's arrest, and Gihren buys time for a secret plan.

## Character state at end of episode
- The Ark is retaken with minimal damage. Kikaishin is alive, has absorbed Mucus technology, and is building his power outside the solar system. He sees the solar-system invasion as "special".
- The Saurers have officially joined Connect Force, and Harukaze Elementary has a Connect-built temporary school.
- Kanuka now pilots Type-Zero. Division 2 works in the colonies as Connect Force's Labor-crime unit.
- Bud and the Griffon are officially in Connect Force, backed by Shaft. Noa is effectively his guardian and wants him in school. Utsumi and Kurosaki are now "colleagues", and SSS is a looming shadow.
- Quattro Bajeena and Kamille Bidan (Blex's special unit) are under Connect Force command. Amuro suspects Quattro is Char. Amuro and Kamille are friends.
- Zeon and the Federation are in a ceasefire. A Baoa Qu is in Federation hands, but Zeon's forces are intact, and Gihren is stalling for a hidden plan. Degwin and Revil are alive.
- Kycilia is quietly working against Gihren by leaking the Solar Ray's control-ship intel to Connect.
- Seishiro is drilling tight-quarters swordwork and mentoring Himari, who is now steady enough in combat to worry about other teams.
