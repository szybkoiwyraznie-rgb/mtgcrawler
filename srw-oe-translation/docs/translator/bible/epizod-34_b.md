# Episode 34 (group 255) — Operation Extend, alternate version: the same finale with a variant epilogue

Group 255 replays the final battle and the epilogue of group 254. About 84% of its lines are identical and reuse the group 254 English. The differences sit in the epilogue scenes. Seishiro and Himari visit Doki Doki Space after a Korin Land detour where he opened the hatch at minus 50 degrees for a coffee. Suol fakes an "ice planet" posting before ordering Seishiro on leave and assigning Himari to take him on a date. The ending changes too: Seishiro remembers that Himari once started to tell him something in the hangar, and she promises to tell him during their Japan trip.

## Scene 255 — Operation Extend (variant); epilogue (core; many locations)
The battle against the Mucus Emperor and Vasage's fusion with the core play out as in 254. Changed epilogue beats: Kamille notes the Preventers' first mission saw no combat. Keroro dreams of God Keron heroics and of riding more mecha, but Kululu admits nobody can run the mini Warp Gate's core unit without Vasage. Adeu and Galden reunite with the Ryu users after an all-night victory party. In Doki Doki Space, Himari sneezes after Korin Land (Da Cider: "Korin 'Brrr'-land"), the cast speculates that the Planet of the Sages is a tropical paradise, Lamuness leaves for his exams, and Milk blows her nose into Cocoa's handkerchief. Eiji thanks Connect and heads home to Anna on the Planet of the Seal. The Good Thunder Team leave for a month at a Connect Travel resort aboard GoShogun, vanish via the Beamler, and Sara suggests the Beamler leads worlds toward peace. Sheryl and Ranka protest being sent home, and Suol admits that Connect's top brass are fans too. Viral warns Kamina off, and Kamina answers "Just who the hell do you think we are?!" Suol jokes about an ice-planet posting, then sends Seishiro on leave with a "date" mission, which becomes the double date with Domon and Rain. Domon calls it training in showing someone a good time. In the Exart, the Himari Sensor fades, and Seishiro compares it with losing his childhood bug-hunting instincts. He recalls that Himari began to say something in the hangar. She defers it to the trip ("stew on it until then"), and an enemy alert cuts the talk short.

## Character state at end of episode
- Same as 34_a, except that Seishiro now knows Himari has something to tell him. She has promised to tell him during the Japan trip.
- Seishiro learned that he grew up bug-hunting in the mountains of Japan before going to space with his father and Suol (as in 34_a).
- Suol has assigned Seishiro a "date" mission. Domon frames the trip as training.
- Sheryl and Ranka have gone home but may be recalled if fans riot.
