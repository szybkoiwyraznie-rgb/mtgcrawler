# Episode 02 — The Mucus appear: Connect Force takes shape as Mazinger Z, Getter, Dancouga, SV2, Gurren Lagann, the Eldran kids and White Base come aboard

The episode where Connect's real enemy surfaces. In an underground cavern near Mt. Fuji, revived Mechanized Beasts and Invaders pour out of a "Mucus Gate", accompanied by the alien Mucus; Himari senses them before the sensors do (first hint of the "Himari Sensor"). Koji Kabuto (Mazinger Edition Z) and the Getter Team (Getter Robo Armageddon: Go, Kei, Gai) join Connect Force, and Simon, Kamina, Yoko and Boota of Gurren Lagann drill through from another world. Seishiro is forced to reveal the Mucus to everyone present. Patlabor continues with Bud's flying Griffon (Shaft) ambushing SV2 at a JSDF range. Dancouga's Cyber Beast Force trains with Exart and SV2 at Connect Station and teaches Seishiro about fighting spirit. Mobile Suit Gundam / 08th MS Team: Jaburo is attacked by Char, Ginias Sahalin's Apsalus III attacks Jaburo (with Ramba Ral, alive in this setting), and White Base becomes the 13th Independent Unit, ordered by Revil to escape to space and join Connect Force; Exart arrives to stop Char's pursuit and Tokwan's Bigro. Finally the Eldran teams decide that the Earth Defense Class (Raijin-Oh) and Gurren Lagann will go to space with Connect Force. Episode 03 follows White Base and Connect Force in space.

## Scene 13 — The Threat Lurking Underground (Hinobori Academy / Class 5-3 classroom; underground cavern; Connect / Transport Plane; Connect / Transport Plane Hangar)

- Synopsis: The Saurers visit Class 5-3 to agree on joint training twice a week; Kenichi and Jin would rather play soccer, the meeting descends into chaos. An alarm: Mechanized Beasts have appeared, though the Saurers destroyed the Mechanization Empire. The Getter Team (searching for Invader remnants underground) is already fighting. Exart arrives with SV2 Division 2 (Ota: "Weren't we supposed to be handling rogue Labors?!"). Himari suddenly senses "something coming" with no sensor reading, then spaces out mid-battle; strange new enemies appear and Seishiro recognizes them. Mazinger Z bursts in ("Hold it right there!"), Koji announces he joins Connect on behalf of the Photon Power Research Institute. Invaders appear; then Gurren Lagann drills up through the floor of the cavern. Kamina thinks Simon's drill broke through to a new world. Yoko introduces them (from Littner Village, fighting Gunmen); nobody here knows those names. Kamina's grand introduction; Himari: "So cool!". Kamina to a frightened Simon: "Believe in me, who believes in you!" Simon senses enemies behind a wall and drills through for a surprise attack, finding the plant-like structure spawning enemies; the Getter Team follows with Getter-2. Gai shares video with everyone ("Looks like we've been declared comrades." Kamina: "Men don't need words."). After the battle, Seishiro explains: Connect calls the alien species "Mucus"; the Mucus Gate is a warp gate they use; humans cannot pass, but bodies that can disassemble/reassemble (Mechanized Beasts, Invaders) can; the Mechanization Empire may be reviving somewhere in space. Himari protests she's a Connect employee and knew nothing; witnesses must sign an NDA. At the school, Yoko explains Viral and the Beastmen; Seishiro guesses they came from a planet in another universe and offers Connect protection and papers. Yoko trusts him; Seishiro is flustered and Himari teases "You're blushing!" On the transport home Himari apologizes for losing focus; Seishiro says her sixth sense found the gate and may be a talent to polish. In the hangar Koji meets the Getter Team; taciturn Go says just "...Nice to meet you."
- Translator notes: Tone: kids' comedy -> action -> mystery reveal. Kamina's speeches must have swagger; his canonical intro (Gurren Lagann ep. 1-2): "Team Gurren, whose infamy echoes through Giha Village! The indomitable demon leader with a man's soul burning on his back! The great Kamina, that's me!" and "Believe in me, who believes in you!" (ep. 1). Simon calls Kamina 兄貴 "Bro" (glossary アニキ = Bro). Boota only says "Buu!"-type noises. Takako is nicknamed 教授 "Professor" by Erika (canon). First use of the terms Mucus and Mucus Gate in dialogue; use glossary forms. Himari's mid-battle trance and "sixth sense" are foreshadowing (Himari Sensor). Kei teases Go for "liking" the newcomers.
- New names: 光子力研究所 → Photon Power Research Institute (literal; medium; check SRW V/X English). リットナー村 → Littner Village (official English; high). 教授 (Takako's nickname) → Professor (canon nickname; high). ゲッター２ → Getter-2 (official; high). 地下 (command) → Underground (UI command; medium). 守秘契約 → nondisclosure agreement (high). エルドラン・チーム → Eldran teams (medium).

## Scene 16 — Roar of the Wild (Earth Orbit / Connect Station; Connect Station / Hangar; Connect Station)
- Present: Himari, Seishiro, Noa, Kanuka, Ota, Asuma, Shinshi, Hiromi Yamazaki, Goto, Shinobu Fujiwara, Sara Yuki, Ryo Shiba, Masato Shikibu, Alan Igor, Suol.
- Synopsis: Narration: the Cyber Beast Force, which defeated the Muge Zorbados Empire, cooperates with Connect and will soon join Connect Force. SV2's first time in space (Hiromi: Earth looks all connected; Goto: "Never thought I'd see it in my lifetime." Asuma: "We're just local civil servants."). Kanuka warns Noa that space-modified Labors can only move on decks, not fight like mobile suits. The Cyber Beast Force arrives trading insults with SV2: Sara mocks SV2's stack of written apologies; Masato calls Ota "old man"; Shinobu suggests he direct traffic ("Don't knock traffic duty!"). Alan restrains them. Suol arrives (meetings with Commander Igor and General Takeda), welcomes the fighting as bonding. The exercise uses Mucus holograms set stronger than real data. Shinobu needles Seishiro as "the greenest one"; Ryo observes that Seishiro loses focus when emotional, while they peak when angry. Seishiro can't damage the targets and calls guts "illogical"; the team demonstrates Final Dancouga, which exceeds the machine's theoretical potential through sheer spirit (Will), and says they sense hidden power in Exart. In step 2 Exart is nearly wrecked; Shinobu offers to let him bail out for Himari's sake; Seishiro refuses and pushes through ("Let's show them what we've got!" Himari: "Adrenaline strength!"). Afterwards the teams bond. Himari teases Seishiro for showing anger and urges him to be more hot-blooded; Ryo: with a firm core, anger won't lead you astray; Shinobu: cool and hot-blooded at once would be the strongest. Shinobu's catchphrase やってやるぜ does not appear here. Mission title echoes the Dancouga theme of wild beasts.
- New names: ムゲ・ゾルバドス帝国 → Muge Zorbados Empire (official; high). イゴール長官 → Commander Igor (Alan's father; medium). ファイナルダンクーガ → Final Dancouga (official; high). 気力 → Will (SRW stat; high). 第１３独立部隊 → 13th Independent Unit (medium; Bandai English varies: "13th Independent Corps/Squadron").

## Character state at end of episode
- The Mucus are now known to Connect Force members (all witnesses signed NDAs); Himari learned of them in the field and feels left out of Connect's secrets.
- Himari can sense the Mucus before sensors do; she lost focus in a trance-like moment and is embarrassed. Seishiro sees it as a talent.
- Koji Kabuto and the Getter Team (Go, Kei, Gai) have joined Connect Force; Go is taciturn and approves of Team Gurren's instincts.
- The Mechanization Empire seems revived somewhere in space and appears to control Mucus and Mucus Gates; the Labors with Mechanized Beasts were not simply mechanized (tie to the Labor rampages is suspected).
- Team Gurren (Simon, Kamina, Yoko, Boota) are from another world, under Connect's protection; Kamina has "drafted" the Eldran kids into Team Gurren. Yoko trusts Seishiro.
- Gurren Lagann and the Earth Defense Class will go to space with Connect Force; the Saurers, Ganba Team and Dankets stay to defend Earth.
- SV2 Division 2 is in space at Connect Station; Bud's Griffon (Shaft) escaped and wants a rematch with Noa; nobody knows who owns it, but Kanuka suspects an organized backer.
- The Cyber Beast Force respects Seishiro after the drill but has gone back to base; they will formally join Connect Force later. Seishiro is reflecting on being "cool yet hot-blooded".
- White Base is the 13th Independent Unit under Connect Force jurisdiction, with Bright in command; the 08th MS Team is aboard but cannot fight in space.
- Char honored Connect's ceasefire and apologized for Tokwan; he knows White Base was Revil's decoy. Ramba Ral and Hamon survived and retreated.
- Ginias Sahalin is dead; Aina has deserted Zeon with loyal retainers; Shiro still loves her and believes she is alive.
- Revil openly supports Connect and believes humanity must end the war to face a common enemy.
