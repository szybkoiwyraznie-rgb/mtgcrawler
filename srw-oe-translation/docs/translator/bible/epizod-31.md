# Episode 31 — Shin Getter ends the Invaders at their wormhole fortress, and Connect Force readies for the Recoral Galaxy

Invaders strike Central Connect Station right after the giant Warp Ring opens, and a wormhole delivers a huge asteroid fortress fused with a Mucus Gate. White Base's group destroys the regenerating Gate; then Cowen and Stinger reveal themselves in giant form, preaching that all life should "become one" through Getter Rays, and nearly every pilot rejects them in personal terms. As Cowen absorbs every Invader's energy to blow away the whole area, Go has everyone send their will to Shin Getter, and its strike annihilates Cowen and Stinger. Operation Extend is delayed and the fleet is hurt, but morale soars. Side missions follow: a Mucus sweep inside the classified old Warp Gates, a trial by combat that wins the Dark Dragon Realm's army, and an Ultrasaurus launch defended against reawakened Sleeper Zoids.

## Scene 236 — Death Match!! The Invader Fortress! (Central Connect Station)
A giant asteroid with a Mucus Gate emerges from a wormhole; Mirai calls it an Invader fortress and Wilder orders it destroyed while Turner and the Getter Robo race over. White Base's team crushes the Gate twice despite its regeneration (Devour-type Invader trap), and Bright hands the area to Kyao's Turner. The Getter Teams sense Cowen and Stinger nearby; Stinger revives the Gate ("Now let's start round twooo!"). Go's team blasts it with every Getter Ray they have, forcing the pair out in giant form.

## Scene 238 — Flash!! At the End of Evolution! (Invader fortress)
A small elite team (Shin Getter, the old Getter Team, Turner and others) takes on Cowen & Stinger, who offer fusion as humanity's only salvation from the Mucus. Pilot after pilot rejects them: Amuro (you hurried evolution), Kamille and Judau (identity, loved ones in the heart), Kira, Athrun (finite life), Alto (difference lets us love), Viral (Invaders lack dignity of life), Kirico, Setsuna (Aeolia sought understanding, not absorption), Keroro ("crossing the line AND the crosswalk"), Lamuness and Tama-Q, Van and Zeke, Ruuji, the GoShogun trio (Remy keeps her secrets). Cowen drains every Invader's energy to destroy the area; Go asks everyone to send their will to Shin Getter, Kei steadies him at Benkei's urging ("I know, Dad!"), and the strike erases Cowen and Stinger ("Our race is eternaaaal!"). Suol thanks everyone, admits real losses and delays, and recalls Connect Force; narration notes the morale boost before the Recoral Galaxy.

## Scene 239 — Sweeping the Warp Gate (Central Connect Station)
Other fleets warp first while Connect Force must clear Mucus hiding in the classified old-model Warp Gates, since only it may enter them. Amm scolds Kyao, now captain of the Turner, who frets about his dream life on Pentagona. Seishiro splits the force into small teams; Himari senses Mucus hidden in every gap. Banter includes Remy mocking men's allergy to "cleaning" and Ota's "I am PERFECTLY calm!"; the block is cleared and the search moves on.

## Scene 240 — Warriors of the Dark Dragon Realm (Dark Dragon Realm, Doki Doki Space)
Galden leads a detachment to meet a powerful Dark Dragon clan whose forces could reinforce Operation Extend. Their envoy demands a trial by combat to prove Connect Force worthy; Galden accepts and Adeu calls it the warrior's way. Keroro frets about not being able to look them in the eye, and the kids worry about losing. Connect Force wins, Galden praises Adeu's growth, and the clan pledges its army and follows the Turner to Connect Station.

## Scene 241 — The Ultrasaurus Sets Sail (Planet Zi spaceport)
The Ultrasaurus is assigned a special role in Operation Extend and is to be shipped to the Recoral Galaxy on a super-heavy transport; Herman keeps its use a surprise. Sleeper Zoids, likely woken by the launch energy spike, attack the spaceport, and Connect Force holds the defense line. Van wants to recover the freed Zoid cores, but Herman leaves the cleanup to the Guardian Force; Fiona, Thomas and Irvine reassure him. Moonbay heads for the spaceport ("Aye-aye, sir!").

## Character state at end of episode
- Cowen and Stinger are destroyed; the Invader threat is over.
- Go has proved Shin Getter can channel everyone's will; he is exhausted but fine, and the Getter Teams are confident against the Mucus.
- Operation Extend is delayed and the fleet took real losses, but morale is high.
- Central Connect Station's old Warp Gates have been swept of Mucus.
- The Dark Dragon Realm clan has joined Operation Extend under Galden's mediation.
- The Ultrasaurus is headed for space and the Recoral Galaxy with a still-secret role.
- Kyao is captain of the Turner and still dreams of a quiet life on Pentagona.
