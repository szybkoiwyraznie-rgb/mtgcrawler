# Episode 15 — Rise of Neo Zeon: Axis hits A Baoa Qu, the ceasefire collapses, Kira, Athrun and Shinn join, and Connect Force returns to Doki Doki Space

Neo Zeon, led by regent Haman Karn on behalf of Mineva Zabi and working hand in glove with Gihren, uses a fake colony drop to pin down the Macross Quarter, then rams the asteroid Axis into A Baoa Qu, smashing the massed Federation fleet; Zeon ends the ceasefire. The Turner unit and the reassigned White Base escape Rakan and the cloned Cyber-Newtype Ple Two. On Earth, White Base defeats Desert Rommel and Ramba Ral in Africa, and Judau is shaken by veterans fighting a lost war. Kira Yamato joins on behalf of the colony powers during a defense against M'Quve's renewed colony drop, and Athrun and Shinn join after an Invader purge at a resource plant. Connect Force then leaves the solar system for Doki Doki Space, where the Black Knight commands Mucus with Don Harumage's jewel and a Goburiki-worshipping Dark Dragon faction moves on the Mystic Shield.

## Scene 122 — Rise of Neo Zeon (Connect Station / Earth Branch; Neo Zeon flagship Sadalahn)
Narration covers Axis's history, Haman as regent, and Kycilia's faction leaking a Neo Zeon colony-drop plan to Connect. The crew (Seishiro, Alan, Shinobu, Kei, Gai, Jeffrey) discuss Gihren's aims, the Federation hawks ("主戦派" = hawks), and White Base being recalled to A Baoa Qu; the Turner task force (Kyao, Daba, Leccee) stands by. On the Sadalahn, Gihren and Haman reveal that the colony drop is bait to pin down Connect Force. The Macross Quarter sinks the Endra-class control ship (Haman scuttles it), Haman debates spacenoid rights with Jeffrey, then covers the retreat in her Qubeley, taking on Heero, Setsuna, Kira, Daba, the Cyber Beast Force, Getter, Noa ("That Haman lady is scaaary!"), Domon and Masaki's cats. Haman orders Axis to course 235, toward A Baoa Qu.
Translator notes: Haman cold and regal ("Lady Haman"; "vulgarian" tone); Gihren addressed as "Supreme Commander". Noa's reflexive-apology gag. Cats: "meow"/"nyot".

## Scene 123 — Axis Impact (White Base; Federation HQ; Sadalahn; Turner unit)
White Base finds Axis abandoned and on a collision course with A Baoa Qu; Quattro deflects Sayla's question about knowing Haman ("undercover work"). Admiral Tianem orders a chaotic evacuation under satellite missiles, mass-driver rocks and minefields; General Revil sends a relief fleet and reassigns White Base to Connect. Suol reports Zeon ended the ceasefire just before impact. The Turner (Kyao) and the Exart meet Rakan's Endra; White Base breaks through to a designated area pursued by Ple Two, whom Amuro, Kamille and Judau sense is like Ple but not her ("Don't call me that! I'm Ple Two!"). Narration: the Federation lost many veteran units; Neo Zeon and Zeon set up a new absolute defense line around Axis.
Translator notes: Ple Two snarling, "You make me sick!"; Rakan bluff ("Bow down before this Zaku III!" / Judau "Get outta the waaay!" - "Not a chance!"). Mission note uses ＠6...＠0 colour tags.

## Scene 124 — Battle of the Burning Sands (Rommel's secret base; White Base / bridge; African desert)
Zeon guerrillas rise across Earth; in Africa, Desert Rommel and Ramba Ral (the Blue Giant) meet at Rommel's hidden underground hangar and plan to ambush White Base, rallying to Mineva ("Sieg Zeon!"). The 08th Team (Shiro, Terry, Michel, Eledore, Karen) and White Base land into a Minovsky-shrouded ambush; Ral's Zanzibar arrives with Hamon, Acous and Cozun, and Ral recognises Quattro's moves as an ex-Zeon ace. Rommel refuses to stop; Judau calls him reckless and Rommel answers "I waited. I waited, and I prepared. You call that reckless?!" (ZZ homage). Afterwards Judau rails at adults bound by such things; Quattro notes word of the Mucus war is spreading as the media blackout fails.
Translator notes: Ral calls Amuro/Judau "boy"; Acous/Cozun call him "Lord Ral". Rommel is "Commander Rommel" (中佐). Mission note: NT-1 Alex sorties as ＠9GR＠0 (token kept).

## Scene 125 — Wings Called Freedom (derelict colony; Connect Station / Earth Sphere)
The Federation trades a promise of conciliation for colony powers' military aid; Connect guards a derelict colony against M'Quve (Tokwan's Bigro) and Neo Zeon's Rakan and Ple Two. Kira Yamato arrives in Strike Freedom as the colony powers' representative ("Strike Freedom, Kira Yamato, launching!"). Kira argues with M'Quve and Rakan ("A man has his ambitions"); Killy and Remy try to coax Ple Two with parfaits and baths and wound her pride. M'Quve withdraws, claiming his second goal (spreading the Federation thin) achieved. Kira formally joins, says his organisation long supported Connect and that a friend will join soon.
Translator notes: M'Quve "you see"; Killy calls himself "your big brother here". Hiromi (Go-Saurer) is a boy.

## Scene 126 — Destiny (resource plant in a debris field)
Athrun and Shinn, scouting ahead, find a resource plant overrun by Invaders; Athrun praises Shinn's caution ("You've grown, Shinn") but has them draw the Invaders away to protect survivors for 3 turns. Connect Force arrives with Kira; Athrun recognises Strike Freedom. After the purge Gai calls Connect "less an elite unit, more a band of misfits"; Kira reassures Shinn. Narration: Connect Force must leave the solar system as other universes need it.
Translator notes: Location header "＃The SEED Gundams" kept with ＃.

## Scene 127 — Return of the Goburiki Army (Arara Castle; outside the capital)
In Doki Doki Space, Yokkora III welcomes Connect Force; Milk demands dinner (running gag "Food! I need food!"). Monskar attack; Chum calls Harumage "Don Baldmage" and Lamuness corrects her; Show recalls Harumage summoned him. The Black Knight appears commanding Mucus via a jewel from Don Harumage, and is confronted by Show, Seishiro, Himari, Zero, Lamuness ("Right now, I am SERIOUSLY ticked off!"), Adeu ("Knight's Code, Rule Number One!"), Mr. Bushido and the Daiteioh kids. Hidden Mucus strike at the castle; Adeu and Paffy arrive from Pafuricia and unlock 'Fire Crash Down'. Bern escapes into another dimension; Pafuricia will send reinforcements to Arara.
Translator notes: Group 128 is a duplicate branch of the battle lines with ⏎ at line ends; text copied from 127. Tama-Q "mya".

## Scene 129 — Those Who Seek the Demon God's Return (vast cavern under Earthtia; Pafuricia Castle)
Following leaked intel, the Turner group (Daba, Lilis, Kyao, Amm, Leccee) and Galden enter a vast cavern and are ambushed by Dark Dragon Soldiers who now worship Goburiki as their lord. Galden scorns them for dishonouring the Clan and delivers his "glory beyond the path of hardship" speech (three duplicate branches). A survivor flees to a main force. At Pafuricia Castle, Cocoa finds the soldiers under light mind control from Harumage; news comes that a Dark Dragon fleet is heading for Eldgear and the Earth Blade to destroy the Mystic Shield and open Earthtia to the Dark Dragon Realm. Paffy leaves with Connect Force.
Translator notes: Lesqua: "if he loses, there'll be hell to pay"; Adeu: "She'll make one scary queen someday". Keroro "sir".

## Character state at end of episode
- The Zeon-Federation ceasefire is over; Axis has destroyed A Baoa Qu and much of the Federation's veteran fleet; Zeon and Neo Zeon now hold a defense line around Axis.
- Haman acts as Mineva's regent in league with Gihren but despises Kycilia's ties to Connect and big business.
- Quattro is evasive about knowing Haman; Sayla has noticed.
- Ple Two (Ple's clone) is a conditioned Cyber-Newtype fighting under Rakan; Amuro, Kamille and Judau sense her link to Ple; she hates being called Ple.
- White Base is reassigned to Connect Force; the Rommel and Ral units are beaten in Africa and the Federation regains the upper hand there.
- Judau is troubled by humans fighting hopeless wars against each other.
- Kira Yamato (Strike Freedom), Athrun Zala and Shinn Asuka have joined Connect Force; Athrun treats Shinn as a maturing protege.
- The Black Knight (Bern) uses Mucus via Don Harumage's jewel and keeps escaping through another dimension; Da Cider is still under Harumage's control.
- A Goburiki-worshipping Dark Dragon force is organising; Connect Force heads to defend Eldgear and the Earth Blade (Mystic Shield).
