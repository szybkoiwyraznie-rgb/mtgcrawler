# Episode 30 — The Death Saurer rises and falls at Eve Polis, and the factions unite for Operation Extend

Connect Force regroups on Zi as enemy numbers surge; Hiltz returns and Fiona is spirited away by Dark Kaiser (Prozen fused with the Death Saurer core) to open the gate of Eve Polis in the Valley of Rare Hertz. Zoid Eve awakens, but Hiltz betrays Dark Kaiser, has him absorbed as a mere "eggshell", and fuses with the Original Death Saurer himself. Raven turns on his former masters and fights beside Van; the Gravity Cannon fails, so Van has the Blade Liger fired from it, Raven breaks the energy shield, and the Death Saurer is destroyed. Connect Force then sweeps the last Invaders in the Seal system and guards a universe-wide conference at a new Connect Station in Balarant space, where Suol steers the factions into Operation Extend. As the giant Warp Ring links to the Recoral Galaxy, Invaders led by Cowen and Stinger ambush the gathering fleets.

## Scene 228 — Ancient Memories (Ultrasaurus; wasteland; Eve Polis)
Herman, Karl and Zero report that Sleeper Zoids and Mucus keep rising despite the Death Stinger's fall; Himari can't read the Mucus clearly and Fiona has a bad feeling. Hiltz transmits, alive, and Fiona glows and vanishes after recalling her past; Zeke leads Van toward the Valley of Rare Hertz while Moonbay drives the Ultrasaurus ("think of it as a little diet"). Dark Kaiser reveals himself to Fiona as Prozen reborn inside the Death Saurer core and forces her to say the word that opens Eve Polis ("Fiona"). Fiona recounts Zoid Eve's history and resolves to shut it down, but balks at killing every Zoid; Dark Kaiser leaves to claim his true body. The Death Stinger blocks Van, Irvine and Thomas, and Bright's force breaks through; Hiltz is beaten, fuses with Ambient, then is enveloped by the Death Stinger's core and withdraws to Eve Polis as Zoid Eve glows.

## Scene 229 — Above Eve Polis (Macross Quarter)
Narration recaps Prozen's survival and plot. The Macross Quarter holds the skies against Mucus and Zoids while the Zoid units fight below (Keroro, Raijin-Oh, Go-Saurer, GoShogun, Getter and others banter). Whale Kings arrive: Emperor Rudolph personally commits the Guylos fleet to joint operations, and as a Death Saurer-like energy surge erupts, he urges everyone to trust Van.

## Scene 230 — The Beast of Destruction, Death Saurer (Eve Polis; Ultrasaurus)
Raven confronts Dark Kaiser, swearing revenge for being Prozen's and Hiltz's plaything, and is swarmed by Mucus until Van arrives; Shadow carries Fiona to Van, and Van and Raven agree to team up ("I'll take you on anytime after this"). Hiltz arrives, reveals Dark Kaiser was only a shell guarding the core ("Bye-bye, Prozen"), and fuses with the Original Death Saurer, which opens a Mucus Gate and devours its own allies. Raven explains its new armor and charged particle converter; Fiona begs Van to destroy Zoid Eve, but Van and Raven refuse and fight on. The Gravity Cannon's super-gravity is repelled; Van has Karl fire the Blade Liger from the Gravity Cannon while Raven and Shadow breach the energy shield, and the Death Saurer is destroyed. Comedy coda: Moonbay's "my Ultrasaurus" (Herman's silent objection), Thomas's "I'm super, mega moved right now!" stealing Lamuness's catchphrase, and "Your love is MEGALOMAX!"

## Scene 232 — Invader Sweep (Seal system)
Narration: Connect and ISTO negotiate a peace treaty and the counteroffensive framework; force allocation for an assault involving millions of ships stalls. Cowen and Stinger have vanished; Connect Force heads for the Astragius Galaxy to support a new Warp Gate and Connect Station in Balarant territory.

## Scene 234 — Consensus (Connect Station / Grand Assembly Hall)
Suol chairs a universe-wide conference (new Pentagona, reformed Grados, Balarant and others), warning of the calm before the storm. The crew watches the broadcast; Keroro's bid to "represent the Keronians" to skip work is shot down by Kururu and Natsumi. Mucus Gates erupt on a nearby planet and Connect Force crushes them (Remy's husband-hunting gag). Himari brings Seishiro a homemade bento. Despite harsh criticism of ISTO, Suol brokers consensus, helped by the outbreak and Connect Force's command performance; the plan is named Operation Extend.

## Scene 235 — Warp Ring Activation (Central Connect Station)
Hundreds of Ark Ships gather; Zero and Seishiro explain Connect's mass-produced cruisers and unmanned drones for the outer perimeter. As the giant Warp Ring links to the Recoral Galaxy, Invaders strike and devour a Gilgamesh ship. Connect Force repels the vanguard; Cowen and Stinger appear, inviting everyone to "become one", and a second wormhole disgorges something huge elsewhere. The Getter Team heads out to settle things while Wilder holds the line.

## Character state at end of episode
- Hiltz and the Original Death Saurer are destroyed; Dark Kaiser/Prozen was absorbed and is gone.
- Zoid Eve was not destroyed; Zi's Zoids live on, and the Guardian Force handles the remaining sweep.
- Raven fought alongside Van and still insists he alone will beat Van; Shadow helped rescue Fiona.
- Fiona has regained memories of her past as Elisi Linette and is safe with Van.
- Emperor Rudolph personally led the Guylos fleet in joint operations with Connect Force.
- The factions agreed to Operation Extend; Suol's standing as chair is high.
- Cowen and Stinger have resurfaced at the Warp Ring; the Getter Team is moving to confront them and a giant object.
