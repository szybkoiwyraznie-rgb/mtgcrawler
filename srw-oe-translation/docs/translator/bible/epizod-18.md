# Episode 18 — The Planet of the Seal is liberated, and Chirico falls into the Uoodo trap

Connect Force launches the liberation of the Planet of the Seal: the Macross Quarter, joined by Ozma Lee and his new VF-25S, seizes the orbital Station Satellite while White Base descends on the Grados Government House. Le Cain stages the battle as a planetwide broadcast and offers to withdraw if he personally loses; Connect accepts, defeats Gosterro and then Le Cain's Zakarl, and Le Cain keeps his word, pulling ISTO out with Roan Dimitrich, now his adjutant. Connect worries the withdrawal is a calculated move to let the liberated planet fracture, then helps mop up rogue Grados/Balarant gangs before leaving a small security force. In the Astragius Galaxy, the Balarant and Gilgamesh sign a truce; Chirico is transferred out, witnesses the Prototype (Fyana) stolen from Asteroid Lido, is left to die, captured and tortured by Rochina, and allowed to "escape" to Uoodo. There he joins Gotho, Vanilla, Coconna and Ru Shako as a battling pilot, survives a set-up and an army purge of the town, and is extracted by Connect Force via Gotho's secret Connect contacts.

## Scene 143 — Overture to Liberation (Macross Quarter briefing; Orbit / Station Satellite)
Narration: Connect's private warp gate gives it a route ISTO cannot match, so it must win fast before ISTO reinforcements arrive; the resistance Eiji once fought with may be dying. Jeffrey, Zero and Eiji outline phase one (take the Station Satellite) and phase two (take the Grados Government House); Eiji says most Gradosians opposed the invasion and will surrender without Le Cain, and Zero has staffed the provisional-government support group to prevent revenge atrocities. Ozma, fresh from the Frontier system, skipped the briefing to tune his VF-25S (and, Luca notes, to drink); he lectures Alto on people skills and rejoins the Skull Squadron. In orbit Ozma deliberately disables rather than destroys a Grados ship, White Base dives for the surface, and the Macross Quarter destroys an ISTO Ray-class ship before boarding teams take the Satellite.

## Scene 144 — Overture to Liberation (battle lines)
Duplicate of the Scene 143 mid-battle quotes (Ozma, Michel, Goshogun team, Alto), without the window-clear marks.

## Scene 145 — Le Cain's Challenge (Grados Political Prison; Grados Government House; Ruined City District)
In prison, Anna Stephanie is visited by Roan, now Le Cain's advisor in Grados uniform, who urges her to become Gradosian and help erase the planet's "inferior" culture; she is devastated. Le Cain forbids his fleet from retaking the Satellite, names Roan his right hand, and broadcasts the battle as a stage to prove resistance is futile; Eiji confronts him, and Gosterro is sent in first and beaten. Le Cain then declares he will withdraw if he loses to his guard alone; Connect accepts (Bright, Zero, Seishiro), defeats the Zakarl, and Le Cain, calmed by Roan, keeps his word and withdraws via the Station Satellite while insisting Grados will stay with ISTO. Zero and Seishiro suspect the staged "honorable" defeat is groundwork for a later return if the freed planet descends into feuding between collaborators and resisters. Eiji reunites with Anna, admits Roan must have his reasons, and leaves to keep fighting with Connect, promising to return; narration notes Roan talked resentful units into the retreat and Connect leaves only a small security force.

## Scene 146 — Valley of Greed (White Base / Bridge; a valley on the Seal)
A new security force built around the thin resistance needs help, so Connect Force stays on to support it. Zero briefs on armed groups of Grados and Balarant deserters working with the local mafia; Seishiro stresses that final peace must be won by the locals. Eiji leads a strike in a valley; the gang calls in reinforcements as Zero predicted and is wiped out, with the security force taking custody of prisoners.

## Scene 147 — Asteroid Lido (Asteroid Lido; Gilgamesh Intelligence medical facility)
After Monad, the Balarant and Gilgamesh close a truce; Chirico is suddenly transferred out of Connect Force and sent, uninformed, on a raid of Asteroid Lido. His unit attacks friendly forces guarding a secret base; inside, Chirico finds the PS capsule with a woman in cold sleep, which his captain has hauled to the ship. The mothership then destroys the asteroid to silence everyone; the Hundred Years' War ends in a truce. Rochina interrogates the rescued Chirico about where the cargo went, then lets him "escape" as a trap, and the exhausted Chirico drifts to Uoodo.

## Scene 148 — Uoodo (Gotho's hideout; battling arena; Intelligence Special Office)
Narration paints Uoodo as a lawless war-ruined town where mafia-run battling is the main business. Gotho introduces Chirico to Ru Shako, Vanilla and Coconna; Vanilla has painted his AT's shoulder red, and Chirico corrects the shade and side, naming the official unit (Melkian Strategic Armored Trooper Corps, Special Mission Unit X-1). The first "battle royale" is a set-up by killers, likely with the security police; Chirico admits he is the target, and Shako stays to fight beside him, sensing a warrior's kinship. The army moves in and purges the town, and Gotho promises to call in a contact. Rochina reveals the army was his bait to flush out the Prototype's thieves, orders Chirico kept under watch and protected, and muses it is time to return him to Connect Force.

## Scene 149 — Escape (Intelligence Special Office; Gotho's hideout; Uoodo Spaceport)
A vehicle, likely carrying the Prototype, slipped the cordon; Rochina plans to trace it via the Didilium it needs and cares only about what it will mean for Chirico. Gotho reveals he has secretly dealt with Connect for years and that naming Chirico got an instant rescue; Chirico reveals he was seconded to Connect Force before the truce. At the spaceport a rogue army unit holds the pad with a derelict battleship; Vanilla steals an AT Fly ("Airman Second Class Vanilla, reporting for duty!"), and Seishiro, Himari, Domon and a landing team arrive to secure a zone for White Base, fighting to avoid cockpit hits, which impresses Shako. Everyone boards White Base as Uoodo burns; Chirico's group joins Connect Force, and oddly Intelligence never asks for Chirico back.

## Character state at end of episode
- The Planet of the Seal is free under a provisional government; Connect left a small security force, and tensions between collaborators and resisters threaten stability.
- Le Cain has withdrawn to the Grados system via the Station Satellite but still backs ISTO; Roan Dimitrich is his trusted adjutant.
- Anna Stephanie is free, hurt by Roan's choice; Eiji has promised to return to her and David.
- Gosterro was beaten but survives.
- Ozma Lee and his VF-25S have rejoined the Skull Squadron.
- The Balarant and Gilgamesh are in a truce; Uoodo has been destroyed in the fighting.
- Chirico, Ru Shako, Vanilla, Coconna and Gotho have joined Connect Force; Gotho has long been a secret Connect informant.
- Rochina deliberately let Chirico go and is tracing the stolen Prototype (Fyana) via Didilium; Chirico has seen her in the PS capsule.
