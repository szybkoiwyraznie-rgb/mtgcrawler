# Episode 09 — The Sunsa intervention: Chirico's death and return, a Giga Nekton, and Kusanagi's past

Episode 9 finishes the Sunsa arc of Armored Trooper VOTOMS: Pailsen Files and then sends the Connect Force back to the Earth Sphere. Acting on a Ministry of Intelligence tip, the force fights its way past an ISTO (Posaydal Army) blockade above Sunsa. On the ground, Inge Riemann ambushes Gregore's squad to "prove" that Chirico can die, and Chirico is killed, or seems to be. He revives aboard the White Base just in time to help destroy a grown Mucus Gate that ISTO had been nurturing, the first Giga Nekton. Nei Moh Han of the Thirteen withdraws with ISTO's Mucus researchers. Suol then reveals Connect's dark history: Balarant once stole a Warp Gate and sent its Mucus Core out of control, and Kusanagi tells, in a playable flashback, how his father died covering the Ark Ship's escape with Vasage's help. The second half is a run of side missions and "chapter 3" teasers: Ken Taiga's return (Jushin Liger) against a Metal Knight; Noa's grudge match with Bud's Griffon at Shinohara Heavy Industries (Patlabor), which sets up the occupied "Ark" in Tokyo Bay; Zechs's duel with Heero in Antarctica, after which Kycilia's secret Orthros Team (Char, Zechs, Mr. Bushido) makes contact with the force; the Eldran kids fighting Mechanized Beasts and hijacked Labors; and Quattro and Kamille preventing the 30 Bunch gas attack, which leads to Jamitov's arrest. It follows Episode 8's Sunsa cliffhanger and leads into the Earth Sphere material of Episode 10.

Note: Scene 75 is a fragment of alternate battle lines from Scene 74 (Shiro/Kuro/Masaki and Dr. Kusanagi's "The Raiko Blade… evolved?"). Scene 78 is a one-line variant of Bud's parting shot in Scene 77. Scenes 76 and 77 end with fourth-wall "Chapter 3" preview segments; translate them as punchy next-episode trailers. Scene 81 (30 Bunch) is set slightly earlier than Scene 79 (Jamitov's arrest is already past news in Scene 79), so do not "fix" the apparent order.

## Scene 73 — Episode 9: Death Match with Riemann (Planet Sunsa, rear area)
- Present: Gregore Garosh, Murza Melym, Byman Hagard, Chirico Cuvie, Inge Riemann.
- Synopsis: The narration explains that, as Sunsa winds down, Gregore's squad is posted alone on patrol where the Connect Force will land. Riemann arrives in his custom machine with elite troops: "Gregore Squad, you die here." Byman quips that it's the Red Shoulders' traditional "cannibal drill". Riemann reveals the logic: Pailsen believes Chirico is an abnormal survivor who cannot die, and Riemann refuses to believe it and will prove him mortal with his own hands; the others "know too much" and are dogs to be put down. Chirico realizes that everything was Pailsen's design. In the climax, Riemann screams "How can you not die?! Any human must die!" and he and Chirico seem to kill each other. Gregore finds Chirico riddled with holes and with no pulse. Then an ISTO mixed force with land battleships appears, and the narration says the out-of-ammo squad can only wait for the Connect Force.
- Translator notes: Tragic and intense, the emotional peak of the Votoms material. Riemann's 死なぬはずがあるか！死なぬはずが！ / 必ず死ぬはずだ、人間ならば！ is a near-quote of Pailsen Files ep. 4–5, so render it as an obsessive, repeated scream ("He can't not die! He can't!" / "Any human being has to die!"). Gregore addresses Riemann with sarcastic politeness (司令官殿 → "Commander, sir"). Byman's dark humor. Chirico: few words. Fixed sortie units.
- New names: 共食い訓練 → cannibal drill (Red Shoulder's internal killing exercises; medium). リーマン司令 → Commander Riemann (high).

## Scene 74 — Episode 9: Seed of the Mucus (White Base / bridge, hangar, sickbay; surface of Sunsa; Connect Station / Astragius Galaxy; flashback: Ark Ship escape; Connect Station / private room)

- Synopsis: The White Base descends, and Himari senses Mucus. Gregore's squad is recovered and Chirico is rushed to sickbay, where Karen fails to restart his heart and gives up. A Balarant officer claims the Mucus Gate for ISTO and, when beaten, blows the rock above it, saying he would rather unleash it than hand it over. A gate larger than any before emerges: ISTO had been watching it grow, and it summons the first Giga Nekton. In sickbay, as the squad mourns ("Still feels like a dream"), Chirico opens his eyes, says "Danger's coming… I have to fight", and sorties, to everyone's disbelief. Gregore: "Chirico's immortal, that's all." Battle quotes compare the Mucus to the DG Cells (Domon) and the Invaders (the Getter teams), and Shingo turns it into "gardening tips" (Remy: "Since when do you garden?"). Afterward, Nei Moh Han of the Thirteen appears, recovers ISTO's pilots and Mucus survey team, scorns cooperation with "a mere Connect", and leaves with a classy exit. Chirico faints after the battle. At the station, Suol explains that controlling Mucus artificially is dangerous: Warp Gates use the Mucus Core's properties, which only Vasage made possible, and Balarant once stole a gate and triggered a runaway that erased a star system. - Translator notes: The emotional core of the episode: Chirico's resurrection is eerie, and the flashback is tragic and heroic. In the flashback, Kusanagi is a child: use childlike speech ("Mom", "Dad"), and the Captain's 剣の稽古は怠るなよ ("Don't slack on your sword practice!") should echo later. Suol calls the Captain by the surname token (＜主人公姓＞隊長), so keep the token. 賢者の卵 = "the Sage's Egg" (glossary); Dr. Kusanagi says Vasage will put the Egg first. ワープ断面 relates to the glossary term 時空断面 ("space-time cross-section"), so use "warp cross-section" or "cross-section" consistently. ライコウブレード (no dot here) = glossary "Raiko Blade". Shiro and Kuro (Masaki's cats) end lines with ニャ; the glossary maps the "Kuro" speaker label to "Guylos Imperial Officer", which looks like a glossary error, so check it, but in this scene クロ is Masaki's familiar Kuro. Domon's 真っ赤に燃える拳 nods to "This hand of mine is burning red!" (glossary). Hayato Jin warns that the Mucus regenerate like Invaders.
- New names: ギガ・ネクトン → glossary (Giga Nekton). 試作モジュール → prototype module (high). ワープ断面 → warp cross-section (medium). バララントの介入 → the Balarant incident (descriptive). ＩＳＴＯの混成部隊 → ISTO mixed force (descriptive).

## Character state at end of episode
- Chirico was confirmed dead and then revived himself. The squad and the White Base crew now half-believe he is "immortal". Riemann is dead (mutual kill). Pailsen believes in the abnormal-survivor theory, and a Ministry of Intelligence spy is exposing the Red Shoulders' secrets.
- ISTO (Balarant, the Posaydal Army) is actively studying and cultivating Mucus Gates for military use. Nei Moh Han of the Thirteen has met the force and recovered ISTO's Mucus researchers.
- Connect and ISTO are in open hostility; Connect leans on Gilgamesh as a backer.
- The players now know why Kusanagi fights the Mucus: his father died buying time for the Ark Ship escape after Balarant's warp-gate disaster. He says he feels no hatred. Kamina, Shinobu and others openly back him.
- Vasage watches over Himari but is not yet ready to "talk".
- Ken Taiga and Liger are back in action alongside the Saurers; the Drago threat may be reviving in another universe via Mucus Gates. Ken intends to join the Connect Force; Mai and Yui are angling to come too.
- Noa failed to capture Bud and the Griffon and is consumed by frustration. Shaft (Utsumi, Kurosaki) is clearly behind the Griffon. The Ark in Tokyo Bay is occupied by rogue Labors, with Type-Zero among them.
- Kycilia's covert Orthros Team (Char, Zechs, Mr. Bushido) is cooperating conditionally with the Connect Force but may still clash with the Federation. Char has met Amuro and Sayla face to face; Sayla stays aboard the White Base.
- Zechs owes Heero a rematch; Mr. Bushido intends to duel Setsuna once his new machine is finished.
- Jamitov has been arrested and his elite force disbanded after the foiled 30 Bunch gassing; Blex and Quattro are working with Kycilia's people toward peace. Kamille has met Setsuna and sees a kindred spirit.
- Gihren holds Zeon's military power and is suspected of holding a trump card (foreshadowing the Solar Ray).
