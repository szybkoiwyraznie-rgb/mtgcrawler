# Episode 13 — The Young King Returns: Prozen awakens the Death Saurer and Van brings it down

Episode 13 closes the Zoids (Chaotic Century) Guylos civil-war arc. Escorting Prince Rudolph back to the imperial capital Guygalos, the Connect Force discovers Prozen has seized the army, jailed Karl Schubaltz and declared Rudolph dead. Zero's counter-plot frees Schubaltz (via Suzaku) while the main force storms the capital; Prozen unleashes the resurrected Death Saurer, burns his own city, and confesses to killing Van's father Dan Flyheight. With Dr. D's and Fiona's analysis, Zero's 0.001-second plan and Suzaku's Lancelot, Van's Blade Liger slices through a charged particle blast and destroys the Death Saurer's core. Suzaku formally joins the Connect Force (keeping an eye on Zero). Epilogue: Rudolph is crowned, the war on Zi ends, the Guardian Force is founded.
The episode then branches into four short fixed-unit side missions that set up later arcs: Show Zama of Dunbine is summoned by Don Harumage (Lamune & 40) and duels the Black Knight (Bern Bunnings) before being flung into the Pentagona world (leads into Ep 14); Kira and Athrun foil a Neo Zeon asteroid drop led by Rakan Dahkaran (foreshadows Neo Zeon / Gihren's strategy, Ep 15); Daba is rescued by returning Leccee and Amm and plans to retake Mizun (Ep 14); Quattro's detached team seizes Jamitov's nuclear stockpile (fallout of the Jamitov arrest/30 Bunch incident). Finally the Guardian Force (Karl Schubaltz, Rosso, Viola) meets the boy Ruuji Familon, and Zero recruits Schubaltz, Rosso and Viola into the Connect Force.
