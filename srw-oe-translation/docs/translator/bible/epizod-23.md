# Episode 23 — Connect Force stops the Axis drop, defeats Haman and rescues Ple Two, then turns to a rising Mucus tide

Gihren accelerates Axis early, forcing Connect Force into a battle on his terms while the Federation readies a nuclear strike. The Macross Quarter defeats Ginias's Apsalus III and Gihren's flagship Gwazine, while White Base fights Haman's fleet. Inside Axis, Judau and Ple free Ple Two from the Quin Mantha, and Judau defeats Haman, who admits defeat and withdraws. Angol Mois's Hellmageddon 1/1 turns the empty Axis to dust, and a ceasefire brokered by Kycilia's Legitimate Zeon ends the war in the Earth Sphere. Mucus Gates then surface across the solar system, and Himari senses that a huge Mucus swarm is converging on Connect Station.

## Scene 181 — Gihren's Trap
Connect Force plans a pincer on Axis, but Axis makes its final burn far earlier than predicted, switching its target from Jaburo to an easier impact point. The Federation fleet is held back and Luna II prepares a nuclear strike, which Zero wants to pre-empt. He hints at an "ace up his sleeve" to be revealed at the end. Gihren commands from the Gwazine, betting on Connect Force's habit of letting Zeon soldiers escape. A dying Ginias makes a last stand in the Apsalus III and falls; Gihren broadcasts a rallying speech, argues with Quattro, Kamille, Zechs, Heero, Setsuna, Mr. Bushido and Daba, then abandons the Gwazine and leaves the rest to Haman.

## Scene 182 — Gihren's Trap (variant)
A partial duplicate of 181 (Gihren/Haman briefing and Gihren vs. Kamille) with window-clear tokens. Translated identically.

## Scene 183 — Haman's Resolve
White Base, approaching Axis on inertial navigation, is intercepted by Haman's Sadalahn. Haman guesses Connect Force can stop Axis and refuses to stand down. She offers to take Char back and invites Judau to join her; both refuse, and Ple sides with Judau. Rakan is beaten back, a Big Zam is thrown in, and Haman abandons her flagship and retreats into Axis in her Qubeley, baiting Judau. Zero tells C.C. that he must defeat Haman and make Neo Zeon withdraw before shattering Axis, so that she does not die a martyr.

## Scene 184 — The Earth Sphere United
Inside Axis, Ple Two attacks in the Quin Mantha. The kids, Kamille, Judau and Ple reach her, and the disabled machine lets Judau pull her out unconscious (Zeta gains Waverider Charge). Haman duels everyone, trades philosophy, and loses to Judau. In a ZZ-style farewell she admits defeat ("glad I came back… to meet a strong child") and withdraws. Ple Two, revealed as a Ple Numbers clone, will recover with Connect's treatment but probably never fly again, which Himari thinks is for the best. Mois destroys Axis, and the war ends in a ceasefire.

## Scene 185 — Joint Front
Mucus Gates surface everywhere. Revil, Kycilia and Garma split the cleanup with Connect Force, which takes the asteroid belt. Garma, who has learned Quattro's identity from Kycilia, warmly reconciles with him and teases him about the sunglasses. Connect Force destroys a giant gate that had grown slowly underground. Afterward Himari zones out in the hangar, and Seishiro sends her to rest, worried that she pushes herself too hard.

## Scene 186 — Mucus Gate Sweep
White Base clears a gate hidden in an asteroid field where radar is poor. The scene is mostly banter: Lamuness complains, Kamina vows to shield his underlings and Simon offers to help, and Keroro tries to skip out until Natsumi threatens to ban him from the hangar. Bright orders the crew to rest in shifts.

## Scene 187 — Ominous Signs
Seishiro searches HQ's records on his father (Captain Kusanagi, the Exart's previous pilot) but finds nothing new, then teaches Koji and Masato to send their own data. Masato reveals that he defied his rich father to enter the Space Force Academy, and Koji says he and Shiro were raised by their grandfather. A small, young Mucus Gate under Mars is cleared, but Himari is uneasy. Back at the station, Vasage tells her a massive Mucus swarm is converging from all directions, and Suol starts gathering forces.

## Character state at end of episode
- The war in the Earth Sphere is over. Axis was destroyed by Hellmageddon 1/1, and Kycilia's Legitimate Zeon mediated a ceasefire.
- Gihren escaped the sinking Gwazine. Ginias was shot down in the Apsalus III, and his fate is left unstated.
- Haman was defeated by Judau, admitted defeat and withdrew alive with Neo Zeon. Rakan retreated.
- Ple Two (a Ple Numbers clone) has been rescued and is in White Base's sickbay with Judau and Ple. She calls Judau "big brother" and will likely never pilot again.
- Garma knows that Quattro is Char and calls him a friend. The Zeon factions now cooperate against the Mucus.
- Zero's "ace up his sleeve" was Mois's Hellmageddon.
- Mucus Gates are activating across the solar system, and a record-sized Mucus swarm is converging on Connect Station near Mars.
- Himari is fatigued and has a sense of foreboding linked to Vasage. Seishiro is looking for a more efficient way to fight the Mucus and still digging into his father's past.
