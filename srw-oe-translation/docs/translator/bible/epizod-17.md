# Episode 17 — The fall of Monad, and the road to the Planet of the Seal

The Gilgamesh launch a 1.2-million-ship offensive to retake the Quent-built fortress planet Monad, with the Barcoff Squad carrying the key at the heart of the Ministry of Intelligence's ISS unit. Teleported deep into Balarant lines, the squad learns of the "abnormal survivor" theory and that they are Pailsen's test subjects, before Connect Force breaks in to rescue them. Monad's core runs wild and the planet is destroyed with over 100 million dead; White Base holds the port to take on refugees while Eiji fends off Gosterro, and Pailsen reveals he engineered the disaster through the Pailsen Files fed to Vice Minister Wockam. Connect then sends a giant Warp Ring-borne warp gate into the Grados colonial system, takes a Grados asteroid base, repels an ISTO raid on the gate, and purges DG Cell-fused Mucus from the abandoned fourth planet. On Zi, a detachment helps rescue Zoid Battle warrior Leena Toros, who frees herself, and she and Naomi Fluegel are recommended to Connect Force.

## Scene 135 — The Battle for Monad (Ministry private room; White Base; Monad port)
Rochina briefs Pailsen: the ISS's 300 troops escort the Barcoff Squad, who carry the key to the energy-extraction controller, and victory is "98% certain". Pailsen deduces Vice Minister Wockam is using the operation to grab power over secret peace talks, and warns that unforeseen things happen around an abnormal survivor. On White Base, Himari reports Vasage is unusually agitated; Kamille and Amuro sense the squad is cut off at Monad's core. Connect asks the Gilgamesh to pull surplus forces back under the pretext of watching ISTO, then dives in anyway; the Macross Quarter takes the port and the Turner team prepares to go in.

## Scene 136 — Abnormal Survivor (inside Monad's structural shell)
After seizing the control section, the squad is flung through a strange space into a Balarant-held section. Panic and infighting erupt: Godan accuses Barcoff of running, Kochak exposes Godan as "Shirasco the Reaper" who survived by betraying others, and Godan calls Kochak a spy for the brass. Chirico explains Pailsen's abnormal survivor theory (one gene in 25 billion), and Barcoff concludes the squad was assembled and tested to see if they are the same; Zaki begs them to stop, sensing danger in the subject. Connect Force's broadcast urging the Balarant to retreat gives them hope, the Turner arrives, and then the core goes critical and the Balarant are ordered off the planet.

## Scene 137 — Abnormal Survivor (alternate route)
A shorter variant of the end of Scene 136 (the Turner's arrival, the enemy pulling back, the core's runaway), reusing the same lines.

## Scene 138 — Rescue (Monad port; Gilgamesh HQ; Gilgamesh base; White Base)
White Base takes on wounded and surrendering soldiers from both sides while a fixed rear guard (Exart, Getter, Galden, Knightmares, the Barcoff Squad and Gregore's team) holds the port; the Turner and large units leave first. Gosterro arrives with a private shuttle, slaughters desperate Balarant troops and duels Eiji; Bright authorizes firing on armed ATs storming the port. White Base lifts off with one surrendering Balarant pilot carried in the Exart's hand, and Monad is destroyed; Wockam falls from power under martial law. Pailsen, revealed to have planted spies and readied his escape, tells Rochina the Pailsen Files lured Wockam into an impossible operation, insists Chirico is alive, and Rochina offers to serve him. Later the squad debates whether they truly are survivors, and Seishiro tells a tearful Himari that Connect must carry this stain while keeping their hearts true.

## Scene 139 — Planet of the Seal (Central Connect Station; Grados asteroid base)
Suol launches a test of the giant Warp Ring, which sends a whole warp gate (90% complete) to the Grados-colonized system of the Planet of the Seal, Eiji's home universe, with coordinates found via the Mars ruins. Connect Force warps in with the Ark Ship and catches a Grados asteroid base completely off guard; it surrenders. On the planet, Eiji confronts Le Cain, the golden-SPT pilot who is both Grados supreme commander and ruler of the colony. The narration closes Chapter 4 and announces Chapter 5.

## Scene 140 — Defend the Warp Gate (Connect Station, Grados space)
SV2 and Sakaki learn the warp gate uses an extra-large, rare Mucus Core as a module, a Connect secret, and Kanuka notes that Himari's bond with Vasage makes her a target. An ISTO special forces team of ATs and SPTs slips past the security net to hit the Core Module, and Connect Force wipes it out inside the facility. Further skirmishes give Connect a firm beachhead toward the Planet of the Seal, and show that ISTO's forces now coordinate more closely.

## Scene 141 — The Evolving DG Cells (Connect Station; fourth planet)
Eiji describes the Seal, once blue like Earth, now bombed and ruled through a Gradosian caste system; Zero plans an accelerator-beam relay station at the fourth planet first. ISTO has abandoned that barren planet to the Mucus and to DG Cells that arrived through a Mucus Gate, which C.C. and Hayato call colonial cost-cutting. Domon leads the attack on Mucus fused with DG Cells, Himari senses a buried Mucus Gate and the team smokes it out and destroys it. With the relay station set up, Connect prepares to strike before ISTO's slow reinforcements arrive.

## Scene 142 — The Bandits' Misfortune (Guardian Force base; bandit hideout, Zi)
A Connect detachment training a Zoid garrison on Zi is asked by Schubaltz to rescue Leena Toros, an unassigned pro Zoid Battle warrior kidnapped by ex-military bandits. Naomi Fluegel, her mentor, admits responsibility and guides them; the camouflaged hideout has minefields, long-range guns and Weasel-unit Gun Snipers, and Naomi's sniping opens a path for Van's Liger. Leena wrecks the bandits' battleship from inside, then "punishes" the surrendering bandits with deliberately missed shots. Back at base it emerges Naomi promised to recommend her if she beat the bandits alone; Schubaltz recommends both, with Naomi to keep Leena in line, to her horror.

## Character state at end of episode
- Monad is destroyed with over 100 million dead; Gilgamesh now leans heavily on Connect, while the Balarant draw closer to ISTO.
- Wockam has fallen; Pailsen has escaped with Rochina's support and is preparing a "new file" on Chirico, whom he believes survived.
- The Barcoff Squad (Barcoff, Godan, Kochak, Zaki, Chirico) is with Connect Force; Kochak is cocky about being an "abnormal survivor", and Zaki fears the subject; Godan is "Shirasco the Reaper".
- Seishiro and Himari carry guilt over the troops they couldn't save; Vasage sensed the disaster in advance.
- Gosterro survived and escaped by shuttle, still obsessed with Eiji.
- Connect has a giant warp gate (Mucus Core module, a secret) and a relay station in the Grados colonial system; ISTO's reinforcements are slow to arrive.
- Le Cain rules the Planet of the Seal and has met Eiji; Chapter 5 begins with the planet's liberation.
- DG Cells are fusing with the Mucus and may push its evolution further.
- Leena Toros and Naomi Fluegel have joined Connect Force as a reluctant pair.
