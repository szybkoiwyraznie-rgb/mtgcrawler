# Episode 00 — A rookie operator joins Connect Force, and Exart's first real sortie saves White Base from the Red Comet

Prologue episode. Narration sets up a battered Universal Century where Earth has survived Dr. Hell, the Devil Gundam Incident, the Invaders, the Muge Empire, the Evil God Drago, the Mechanization Empire, the Jaku Empire, the Great Demon World and others: crises from many series are treated as past history. Seishiro Kusanagi (the protagonist; name tokens ＜主人公名＞/＜主人公姓＞, default "Seishiro Kusanagi") is the pilot of Exart in Connect Force, the secret combat arm of the civilian space-trade company Connect. Scene 1 is the tutorial: training mission plus a flashback to rookie operator Himari Yaegaki's chatterbox first day. Scene 2 opens the One Year War storyline (Mobile Suit Gundam ep. 1-2): Director Suol Douglas sends Exart to protect the Side 7 refugees aboard White Base, and Seishiro meets Char. Connect's hidden purpose (preparing humanity against non-human threats, i.e. the Mucus) is hinted at but kept from Himari. The episode leads straight into Episode 01, where Connect keeps working alongside White Base on Earth.

## Scene 1 — Basic Training 1 (U.C. 0079, Earth Sphere, Neutral Zone; Connect Station; Connect Station / Hangar; "A few days earlier...")
- Present: Seishiro Kusanagi, Himari Yaegaki.
- Synopsis: Opening narration on the state of the world. Seishiro's inner monologue: Connect is famous as a company but its origins and true purpose are unknown to the public, and he prefers it that way. Training sortie: Himari opens comms with a mic-test ("Testing, testing, today's weather is fine"), launches Exart (CF-00), and walks him through Move, EN, Supplies, Parts/containers (repair kit) and combat against realistic new simulated targets. Seishiro praises her when she force-moves Exart by remote; she beams "Really? Hehe" and he reminds her to stay alert. After the mission he jokes about her first day. Flashback: Himari wanders into the restricted Exart hangar gushing "What an amazing robot!", introduces herself (transferred from General Affairs 44, first year at the company, from Japan, engineering degree, skipped grades to a master's) and then won't stop talking: yakiniku, ramen and dessert buffets, caramel macchiato, no driver's license, hates the cold, loves baths. Seishiro keeps getting cut off mid-sentence ("Good to work wi-", "I s-") until he shouts "That's enough!". He explains Connect Force is a combat unit and Exart is a cutting-edge weapon; Himari is shocked a civilian company has weapons. He says she was picked from hundreds of thousands of candidates; she preens ("Am I one of the Chosen?"). Back in the present, she does not know how to send data to HQ; Seishiro patiently teaches her. Inner monologue: he expected a veteran operator, not a new graduate, but trusts the organization's choice and thinks she must be essential to the project.
- Translator notes: Light comedy and warmth; Himari is bubbly, apologetic, fast-talking, Seishiro is a calm, kindly senior with a dry sense of humor. The interrupted-sentence gag needs the cut-off lines to stay visibly truncated in English. Himari calls him 先輩 constantly; per style, drop it or use the name token. Tutorial terms in quotes ('Move', 'EN', 'Supplies', 'Parts', 'Container', 'Attack') must match the game's UI terms consistently. "本日は晴天なり" is the Japanese mic-check phrase; render as a natural mic check ("Testing, testing, one two"). Hints that Himari's assignment is no accident (setup for the Himari Sensor / Sage storyline later). Mission title in pilot: Episode 0 "Basic Training 1". 望遠カメラ小窓 = "Telephoto Camera Inset" (stage direction label).
- New names: 第４４総務部 → General Affairs 44 (pilot translation; high).

## Scene 2 — (no title) (Connect Station / Lounge; space near Side 7; Connect Station / Hangar)
- Present: Himari, Seishiro, Suol Douglas, Bright Noa, Amuro Ray, Char Aznable, Mirai Yashima, Sayla Mass, Fraw Bow, Paolo Cassius, Marker Clan, Oscar Dublin, Slender, Crown.
- Synopsis: Gundam opening narration (colony emigration, Side 3 declares itself the Principality of Zeon, half of humanity dead, eight months of stalemate). In the lounge Himari asks why Connect doesn't help the Federation against Zeon's nukes and colony drop; Seishiro explains Connect is neutral and the Federation's mistreatment of spacenoids is the root of the war. Suol calls in (Seishiro calls him "Chief", which confuses Himari, who knows him as Director) and orders an emergency sortie to rescue a new Federation ship. Seishiro objects on neutrality grounds; Suol says Side 7 civilians are aboard, so the mission is civilian protection. Himari eagerly cheers "Let's go!", and Seishiro teases that she isn't the one sortieing. Canon scene: White Base launches, wounded Captain Paolo, "Gundam, launching!", Char approaching at three times normal speed, "Let me see what this Federation mobile suit can do!". Exart arrives; Minovsky particles garble Himari's frantic comms ("Seniooor... do your beeest..."), a comic beat. Bright introduces himself as Ensign Bright; Seishiro agrees to escort only as far as Luna II. Char recognizes Connect's new machine, said to be designed against non-human species; Crown calls the support a treaty violation, Char calls it a Federation ploy and decides not to waste effort on it. Seishiro appeals to Char that humans should not be fighting each other now; Char replies he is neither overconfident nor a dreamer enough to think one pilot can change the war. Char withdraws, calling the ship a "Trojan Horse" (木馬). Back at the station Himari, with a sports drink, asks if he trounced Zeon; he spits his drink ("Pfft!") and says it was a skirmish. Seishiro says he wants even Zeon pilots to live, because when humanity must unite, Zeon soldiers will be comrades too, and Connect exists to make that impossible thing possible. Himari reveals she lost her parents before she could remember and grew up in an orphanage, so being stationed in space is no hardship.
- Translator notes: Mix of serious and comic. Canon lines from Mobile Suit Gundam ep. 1-2 must use the glossary forms: "Let me see what this Federation mobile suit can do!", "the Red Comet", "Trojan Horse", Amuro's "Gundam, launching!" (pilot uses this). Paolo's "five battleships at Loum" is Char's canon Battle of Loum record (ルウム戦役 → Battle of Loum). Rank: Bright is 少尉 → Ensign; Char is 少佐 → "Lieutenant Commander", addressed "Commander". Seishiro's line 非人類種 (non-human species) foreshadows the Mucus; keep the phrasing "non-human" consistent. Himari is emotionally anti-Zeon here; Seishiro is the voice of moderation. Neutrality of Connect and the "escort to Luna II" limit are plot points. Himari's orphan backstory is important later.
- New names: ルウム戦役 → Battle of Loum (official Gundam English; high). サイド７ → Side 7 (official; high). コロニー公社 → Colony Public Corporation (literal; medium). 中立宙域 → Neutral Zone (pilot; high).

## Character state at end of episode
- Seishiro knows Connect's true purpose; Himari does not, and he has explicitly told her she is not yet experienced enough to be told.
- Connect publicly presents itself as a space-lane safety / space-trade company and is officially neutral in the One Year War.
- Suol Douglas is Connect's Director; Seishiro calls him "Chief" (団長), a title Himari does not understand yet.
- Connect has now openly helped White Base (under the civilian-protection pretext); Zeon (Char's unit) regards this as a treaty violation.
- Char has noted Exart as a machine built against non-human species and chose not to fight it seriously.
- Bright and White Base crew are grateful to Connect; the escort was promised only as far as Luna II.
- Himari is strongly anti-Zeon; Seishiro wants to spare Zeon pilots and believes all humans will eventually have to unite.
- Himari is an orphan who grew up in an institution; Seishiro now knows this.
- Seishiro wonders why a fresh graduate was assigned to such a critical project; he assumes she is essential to it.
