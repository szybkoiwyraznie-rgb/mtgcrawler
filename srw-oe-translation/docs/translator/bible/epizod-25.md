# Episode 25 — Drago falls in Earthtia, Giwaza is repelled, and Raven returns on Zi

Connect Force splits to defend Eldgear from Drago-swayed Dark Dragons while the Macross Quarter and White Base storm the Earth Blade. There the Evil God Drago reveals he is already revived, that the "Drago Empire" never existed, and that he plans to break the Mystic Shield and merge Earthtia with the Dark Dragon Realm. Ken nearly dies, Ryu Dolk returns from the good god Argama, and the united hearts of Connect Force destroy Drago, wiping out every Mucus Gate he had absorbed. In the Planet Zi system the team finds Sleeper Zoids using Mucus Gates, then in Pentagona drives off Giwaza's fleet with the new L-Gaim Mk-II under Zero's plan. Back on Zi, Raven reappears harvesting Zoid cores, while Hiltz and the Dark Kaiser prepare a monstrous Zoid and plan to discard Raven.

## Scene 193 — The Defense of Eldgear
As the Mucus Gate seizes the Earth Blade, the Turner group stays to guard Eldgear with Galden, Nagy and White Dragon. A Dark Dragon army demands Eldgear's surrender, claiming the evil god's will awakened them and promising a new age after the late Emperor. Galden is disgusted at their lost pride and calls his atonement a long road; Keroro jokes about being a frog before snakes and Natsumi threatens to feed him to them. The attack is repelled, and word comes that the Macross Quarter has engaged the enemy fleet.

## Scene 194 — To the Earth Blade
The Macross Quarter draws the enemy fleet to open a path for White Base's assault landing. Ozma and Ranka, Alto and Sheryl trade worried banter; Ryoma surprises Benkei by acting like an elder to back up Ken. The fleet breaks through and White Base begins its assault while Wilder guards its rear.

## Scene 195 — The Evil God Drago
White Base lands at the giant, regenerating Mucus Gate fed by Drago's aura and destroys it, but a new Gate disgorges the Evil God Drago himself: he declares there is no Drago Empire and that Earthtia's war-torn Dark Dragon Realm revived him. He means to pull the Earth Blade, end the Mystic Shield and make a world of chaos; he absorbs the Mucus, and Ken is struck down, until Ryu Dolk answers Ken's call and returns through the door of Argama. Dozens of pilots reject Drago's claim that malice is human nature (Daba: goodness has always crushed his ambitions; the Raijin-Oh kids liken him to an Evil Beast), and Ranka and Sheryl's song breaks his hold. Ken and Ryu Dolk finish him (God Thunder Blizzard unlocked); his servants dissolve, every Mucus Gate he absorbed and the Metal Knights vanish, and the Dark Dragons surrender. At Eldgear, Ken's Lightning Skirt-Flip on Himari is stopped by Seishiro, who promises him strict retraining, and Connect Force leaves Doki Doki Space.

## Scene 196 — The Evil God Drago (variant)
An alternate copy of four lines from Scene 195: Adeu vows to protect the Earth Blade from Drago, Drago dismisses him as an Earthtian Ryu Knight, and Ryu Dolk tells the dying Drago that the world's good hearts are what defeat him. The translation mirrors Scene 195 exactly.

## Scene 197 — Operation: Sleeper Zoid Capture
In the Planet Zi system, Connect Force investigates Sleeper Zoids on a moon, as the Guardian Force has no space forces. The Zoids attack at once, and a Mucus Gate appears, revealing they travel through Mucus Gates. Fiona senses someone controlling them and a great disaster approaching Zi; battle data confirm deliberate control, but the samples reveal no culprit.

## Scene 198 — Giwaza Lowaw
In Pentagona, the Connect-backed rebels grow while ISTO cannot reinforce Posaydal and Balarant is busy with Mucus. Giwaza leads a fleet at the asteroid-belt Connect Station; Zero makes Connect Force the main force and the rebels support. Kyao unveils the L-Gaim Mk-II (lead developer Mesh Maker), and Daba spares a retreating Posaydal ship, to Leccee's concern. Giwaza and Nei arrive to take Daba's head, Zero introduces himself as the rebels' strategist, and Giwaza withdraws with minimal losses, foreseeing a final battle at Gastogal.

## Scene 199 — The Geno Breaker Returns
Raven in the Geno Breaker wipes out a Guardian Force squadron and rips out the Zoids' cores, hinting a strong Zoid will soon be born. He toys with Van, Schubaltz and Thomas, then leaves behind Sleepers and Mucus; Beek's analysis shows the Geno Breaker now surpasses the Death Saurer. Civilians are evacuated across Zi, and Connect Force waits in orbit. Underground, Hiltz feeds cores to a monstrous Zoid, and the Dark Kaiser declares Raven a disposable pawn to be pitted against Van.

## Character state at end of episode
- The Evil God Drago is destroyed; all Mucus Gates he absorbed (including in other universes) and Earth's Metal Knights are gone. Earthtia and the Mystic Shield are safe.
- Ryu Dolk is back and fights alongside Ken; Thunder Liger / Maryu-Oh Dolga have God Thunder Blizzard.
- Galden continues his atonement; most swayed Dark Dragons surrendered.
- Ken is still skirt-flipping; Seishiro promised to retrain him.
- Daba now pilots the L-Gaim Mk-II (tuned by Kyao); the rebels gained momentum, Giwaza retreated intact, and a final battle at Gastogal looms. Zero openly serves as the rebels' strategist.
- Sleeper Zoids travel via Mucus Gates; someone controls them.
- Raven has left Guylos, works for the Dark Kaiser, and harvests Zoid cores; his Geno Breaker now outclasses the old Death Saurer. The Dark Kaiser plans to throw him away against Van.
- Hiltz is about to awaken a monster Zoid (a new "Death Saurer"); Fiona senses it growing. Zi civilians are being evacuated; Connect Force waits in orbit.
