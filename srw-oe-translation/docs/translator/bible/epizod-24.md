# Episode 24 — A mother's memory, a rival's duel, and Earthtia under siege

A record Mucus swarm converges on Connect Station, led by a Benthos Carrier that carries visions of Himari's late mother. Himari decides it is no longer her mother, links minds with it, finds its weak spot, and the Exart destroys it. Viral breaks into the Warp Gate, proposes a rules-based duel with Vasage's tacit approval, loses, and joins Connect for as long as he stays in this world. Himari's last vision reveals the Recoral Galaxy and a route there with Vasage's help, along with the true, staggering scale of the Mucus. Connect Force then moves to Earthtia, where Show digs up the Sirbine, and the Drago Empire uses Mucus Gates and Metal Knights to harvest negative energy, ending with a giant Mucus Gate at the Earth Blade.

## Scene 188 — A Mother's Memory
Vasage detects a massive Mucus swarm heading for Connect Station, and Connect Force gathers to defend it, with the Macross Quarter group on the front line. A Benthos Carrier leading the swarm projects visions of Himari's mother, a Sage who could commune with Vasage and was absorbed by that Carrier during a Mucus attack. Dr. Kusanagi suspects a causal link to its target, while Zero and C.C. trade barbs about facing one's past (C.C. calls him Lelouch mid-battle). Himari declares "That is not my mother!", feels the Mucus's senses flood her mind, and finds its weak spot. Seishiro praises her growth, she glimpses a new vision, and Viral is reported breaching the Warp Gate.

## Scene 189 — Duel with Viral
Team Dai-Gurren holds the Warp Gate against Viral, whose machine regenerates through Lordgenome's gift of immortality and Mucus life energy. Viral reveals he fights with the Mucus only to return to his world and proposes a duel by rules: if he loses he will help Connect and fight the Mucus. Vasage signals approval, Himari and Seishiro trust his word, and Kamina accepts. Viral loses, the Mucus withdraw, and he keeps his word, gruffly, promising to purge humans once back home and telling them a gate home lies on the Planet of the Sages (Gurren Lagann gains Giga Drill Break). In the Information Room, Himari tells Seishiro she can picture the route to the Recoral Galaxy with Vasage's help, then breaks down at the sheer number of the Mucus, and he comforts her, suggesting she may be the key and humanity's hope.

## Scene 190 — Unearthing the Sirbine
A Connect survey team in Earthtia detects a Dunbine-like object in mountain rock but is driven off by Dark Dragon thugs. Simon digs it out flawlessly with Gurren Lagann's drill, and Show finds it is an Aura Battler that moves like his own limbs. He fights the ambushing Dark Dragons in it at once. Show suspects it was thrown here by a space-time distortion like him, and Leccee worries it is an omen.

## Scene 191 — Metal Knight Invasion
Mucus Gates in Earthtia are spewing Metal Knights, and Himari senses they are sent in already active, likely by the Drago Empire, which seems to have absorbed the Mucus. Bright wonders why the Empire targets Earthtia rather than Earth. Adeu takes "Metal Knight" as an insult to knighthood. After clearing the area, White Base heads to Earthtia Defense HQ in Eldgear to stand by.

## Scene 192 — Earthtia Ravaged
A thieves' band begs Eldgear for help, and the Turner group finds their hideout overrun, with a Mucus Gate absorbing their suffering. Ken explains the Drago Empire gathers negative energy to revive the Evil God Drago. Galden scolds a fleeing thief but saves him, and the team rescues the survivors for Pafuricia's soldiers to take in. Nagy then reports a giant Mucus Gate at the Earth Blade and recalls Connect Force to Eldgear.

## Character state at end of episode
- Connect Station survived the largest Mucus attack yet; Mucus activity in the solar system has become sporadic.
- Himari's mother (a Sage) was absorbed by the Benthos Carrier; Himari can now link with Mucus minds and sense their weak spots, which exhausts and frightens her.
- Himari can picture a warp route to the Recoral Galaxy with Vasage's support; Seishiro believes she is the key to stopping the Mucus. They are about to report to Suol.
- Viral lost the duel and now aids Connect "for a time", still vowing to purge humans once back in his world; he needs Vasage to get home.
- A gate to other worlds is said to exist on the Planet of the Sages; Suol says Connect has a counteroffensive plan that requires uniting humanity.
- Show now has the Sirbine, a space-time castaway like himself.
- The Drago Empire uses Mucus Gates and Metal Knights in Earthtia to harvest negative energy and revive the Evil God Drago.
- A giant Mucus Gate has appeared at the Earth Blade; Connect Force is regrouping at Eldgear.
