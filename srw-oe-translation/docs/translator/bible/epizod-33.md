# Episode 33 — The Domain of Harmony: the Sages' trial, the truth about the Mucus, and the plunge toward Genesis

Connect Force reaches the Commune ("Domain of Harmony") and the crystal tower over the Planet of the Sages, the Gate of Harmony that leads to the galactic center. It is also the gate Viral has been looking for to go home. Vasage goes down to report to its people, and the Planet of the Sages judges whether humanity is worthy. The verdict is yes, but the Gate sends elite "gatekeeper" Mucus as a trial. White Base and the Exart fight without Vasage's help, while the Macross Quarter holds an orbital line. Opening the Gate ends the Commune's sanctuary status, because the Sages have chosen to change their relationship with the Mucus. A breather follows: video messages from home for the Earth Defense Class, the Ganba Team and the Saurers, plus the Lamune cast's homesickness gag. Then the big reveal, the lore centerpiece of the game: the Mucus are a chimera born from a lab accident with the benign space plant "Recoral". They wiped out the galaxy's humans except the Sages, whose bond with the mutant Vasage spared them. The real plan is to touch Vasage to the Genesis core and change the Mucus, freeing them from suffering. Connect Force warps to Mucus Genesis, clears the gas cloud, and rides the Ark Ship through the deadly spatial barrier. It ends with a quiet Seishiro/Himari talk about life after the war (Connect will be dissolved; Himari will follow Seishiro). This leads straight into Episode 34's final battle.

## Character state at end of episode
- The Planet of the Sages has entrusted humanity with the future, and the Commune's neutrality and harmony with the Mucus are deliberately broken.
- Everyone now knows the truth: the Mucus are a human-made chimera born from the Recoral plant, and Vasage is a Mucus mutant. The aim is to transform the Mucus, not merely kill them, and sympathy for the Mucus spreads through the cast.
- Viral now knows the Gate of Harmony is his way home. He stays an honorable rival-ally, and Kamina vows to punch Lordgenome someday.
- Himari is fatigued when she fights without Vasage. She has implied she wants to stay with Seishiro for good. Seishiro takes it as professional loyalty.
- Seishiro has told Himari that Connect and Connect Force will eventually be dissolved, by the Chief's design.
- The Eldran kids are emotionally recharged by the messages from home and determined to return to Earth together.
- Connect Force is on Genesis's surface with a roughly 24-hour (later 18-hour) window. White Base, Turner and the Macross Quarter have assigned roles for the push.
