# Karty głosu postaci – SRW OE (v1.1)

Karty dla postaci z ≥ 60 kwestiami fabularnymi lub ≥ 100 cytatami bitewnymi, posortowane według liczby kwestii. Opisy są po angielsku, bo stanowią instrukcję pisania po angielsku.

## Seishiro Kusanagi (セイシロウ・クサナギ) — スーパーロボット大戦 オリジナル

*Kwestie fabularne: 2561 · cytaty bitewne: 231*

**Personality & role:** 22-year-old veteran pilot of Connect Force and the Exart. He is the earnest, upright straight man of the cast: disciplined, mission-first, and serious enough that Suol hopes Himari will loosen him up. He was raised in the sword path by his late father, so he is hard on himself and generous with others, and he wants even Zeon pilots to live. He reflects on Connect's secret purpose in quiet inner monologue, and he slowly warms into a protective partner for Himari.

**Japanese register:** 俺 almost always. Plain/casual だ/だな/だろ/さ with peers and Himari. Clean です/ます to superiors and allies' commanders ('団長、直々になんです？', 'すぐに出ます'). Military radio clipped forms: 了解！, 目標確認！, ～を開始する！, エグザート、出ます！ Inner monologue in （…）. He uses both 君 and お前 for Himari; お前 becomes more frequent as they grow close.

**Tics & catchphrases:** Training/radio formulae ('感度良好だ！', '全機撃墜！', 'これより『攻撃』を開始する！'); swordsman's maxims ('心技体を鍛え、天運を見極めれば負けはない！', '武道を極めんとする者として'); mild 'やれやれ' and 'ハハハ'; 'おいおい' when Himari gets carried away.

**Forms of address:** Himari: 'ヒマリ', 君 then お前. Suol: '団長', polite. His mother: '母さん' (and '母' to others). His father: '親父'. Allies: given name, plainly (シモン, ドモン). Calls himself by the ＜主人公名＞ token in formal introductions ('コネクト・フォースの＜主人公名＞だ！').

**English writing guidance:** Write a calm, competent officer: complete, tidy sentences, moderate contractions ('I'll', 'we're'), never slangy. Radio lines short and procedural ('Roger!', 'Target confirmed!', 'Exart, launching!'). Kindness should come through as understated warmth ('Don't sweat it.' / 'You're getting the hang of this.'), not gushing. Sword-path lines can be slightly elevated ('As one who seeks mastery of the blade...'). Keep inner monologue in parentheses, reflective, and a bit formal. Avoid swearing beyond 'Damn' and avoid American-teen filler. NEVER hard-code his name: keep ＜主人公名＞/＜主人公姓＞ wherever the JP has them, and make sure surrounding grammar works for any name.

**Samples:**

- だけど俺はジオンのパイロットにだって / 生きていてほしいんだよ。  
  → But I want even Zeon's pilots to come out of this alive.
- お前は一人じゃないんだ。 / いつだって隣に俺がいることを忘れるな。  
  → You're not alone. Don't forget I'm always right here beside you.

## Himari Yaegaki (ヒマリ・ヤエガキ) — スーパーロボット大戦 オリジナル

*Kwestie fabularne: 2249 · cytaty bitewne: 73*

**Personality & role:** 18-year-old rookie operator, and later co-pilot, transferred from Connect's 44th General Affairs Division. She is a skip-grade engineering master's graduate who still can't drive. Cheerful, chatty and scatterbrained, she is the mood-maker who babbles about yakiniku, ramen and caramel macchiatos, hates the cold and loves baths. She is an orphan raised in a care home. She is revealed as a descendant of the Sages who can sense the Mucus and talk with Vasage (the 'Himari Sensor'), and her bright face hides real resolve.

**Japanese register:** 私. Polite です/ます to nearly everyone (her 'office junior' voice) with soft, drawn-out endings: ～ですよぉ, ～ですかぁ, ～ますね, ～かなぁ. She slips into casual speech with friends and kids ('元気出しましょ！', 'じゃない'). Sometimes she uses formal keigo with seniors ('申します', 'おっしゃいました'). Lots of elongation and small っ ('ホントですかっ？', 'すっごい').

**Tics & catchphrases:** '先輩' constantly (~250 uses); 'えへへ…'; 'ホント'; 'すみません…'; 'あ～、あ～、本日は晴天なり～' mic test; 'まっかせてください！'; '燃えてきちゃって！'; panicky 'どどど、どうなっちゃってるんですかぁ！？'; 'ヒマリ・センサーです！'. Operator calls: '～を捕捉しました！', '熱源反応、ありません。'.

**Forms of address:** Seishiro: '先輩' (plus ＜主人公名＞先輩/さん). Suol: '団長' (once 'スオル本部長'). Others: given name + さん (カミナさん, ゼロさん); kids get くん/ちゃん (仁くん, マリアちゃん). Dr. Kusanagi calls her 'ヒマリさん' and later 'ヒマリちゃん'.

**English writing guidance:** Bubbly and quick, but still a polite young professional, not a valley girl. Use exclamation points, trailing ellipses and the odd 'Ehehe...'. Use contractions freely. Use light intensifiers ('so cool', 'totally') only in excited lines, and keep operator call-outs crisp ('Heat signatures: none.' / 'Mucus Gate detected!'). Render 先輩 by context: drop it, use the ＜主人公名＞ token in vocatives, or use a warm 'you' construction. Never write 'senpai'. Drawn-out vowels can be rendered with tone and punctuation ('Come onnn, don't make me say it!') but sparingly. Her Sage/visionary lines (ep 24, 33) shift to calm, earnest sentences; let that contrast show.

**Samples:**

- ホントですかっ？　えへへ…。  
  → R-Really? Ehehe...
- そ、それは言わないでくださいよ～。  
  → D-Don't bring that up, please!

## Van Flyheight (バン・フライハイト) — ゾイド－ＺＯＩＤＳ－

*Kwestie fabularne: 855 · cytaty bitewne: 199*

**Personality & role:** The hot-blooded, good-natured teenage hero of Chaotic Century and the de facto lead of the Zoids cast in OE. He is impulsive, honest and stubborn, loves his Liger and Zeke like family, and is protective of Fiona. He is quick to make friends (he adopts Ruuji as his "Liger Combo" partner) and never backs away from Raven.

**Japanese register:** 俺 / 俺達. Casual boyish male speech: ～ぜ, ～さ, ～だろ, ～じゃねぇか, slurred ねぇ/すっげぇ/腹減っちまった. Never polite, except a few plain ああ/わかってる answers.

**Tics & catchphrases:** いくぜ！ / いくぞ、ライガー！ ("Let's go, Liger!"); 負けねえ…俺は誰にも負けねえ！; food-related lines (メシにしようぜ！); すっげぇ！ ("Whoa!", "Awesome!").

**Forms of address:** Fiona -> "Fiona" (protective, gentle); Zeke -> "Zeke" or "buddy"; Raven -> "Raven" (rival, shouted); Irvine and Moonbay by name; Thomas -> "Thomas"; Herman -> "Herman" or "Colonel" (informal); Dr. D -> "Doc".

**English writing guidance:** Short, punchy, heavy on contractions ("gonna", "gotta" sparingly; "c'mon" is fine). Use exclamations like "Whoa!", "Alright!", "No way!". Keep the vocabulary simple and earnest with no sarcasm. He's cocky but sincere, the dub's bright teen-hero voice. Avoid profanity beyond "darn" or "jeez" and avoid slangy 2020s idioms.

**Samples:**

- メシにしようぜ！ 俺、腹減っちまったよぉ！  
  → Let's eat! I'm starving!
- それを０にするか１００にするかは 俺達次第ってことだろ？  
  → Whether it's zero or a hundred is up to us, right?

## Bright Noa (ブライト・ノア) — 機動戦士ガンダム

*Kwestie fabularne: 737 · cytaty bitewne: 46*

**Personality & role:** White Base captain and, in this game, the fleet's main commander (737 lines). Stern, responsible, occasionally exasperated with hot-headed pilots, but deeply trusted. The voice of orders and briefings.

**Japanese register:** Command register: plain imperatives (～しろ, 各機～せよ), ～だ/～だな; 私 in formal speech, 俺 rarely; formal です/ます when reporting to superiors or allies.

**Tics & catchphrases:** Orders: 各機、発進！ = 'All units, launch!'; 全機、帰艦しろ！ = 'All units, return to the ship!'; くっ…勝手なことを！ = 'Damn it, acting on your own again!'

**Forms of address:** Calls everyone by bare name (アムロ, ジュドー, ミライ); クワトロ大尉 = 'Lieutenant Quattro'; 君 to newcomers.

**English writing guidance:** Crisp military English: imperatives, no filler, 'All units', 'Hold position', 'Maximum speed'. Contractions OK in personal talk, fewer on the bridge. Exasperation through short bursts ('Judau, wait!'). Keep briefings clear and compact.

**Samples:**

- 各機、長引けば不利になる！ ホワイトベースの脱出ルートを優先しろ！  
  → All units, a drawn-out fight works against us! Prioritize securing White Base's escape route!
- 待て、ジュドー！　これは罠だ！  
  → Wait, Judau! It's a trap!

## Zero (ゼロ) — コードギアス 反逆のルル－シュ

*Kwestie fabularne: 675 · cytaty bitewne: 197*

**Personality & role:** Lelouch's masked revolutionary persona, leader of the Order of the Black Knights; in OE he becomes Connect Force's chief strategist and political mastermind (675 lines, most in the group). Theatrical, calculating, magnanimous in public; sardonic in private.

**Japanese register:** Public: 私/我々, formal and declamatory (～だ/～である/～しよう/～もらおう); Private (with C.C. or unmasked): 俺, sharper and sarcastic. Long strategic explanations.

**Tics & catchphrases:** 我が名はゼロ; フッ; strategy-as-chess (駒, 戦略); それはどうかな？

**Forms of address:** Allies by name; C.C. = "C.C."; Suzaku = "Suzaku Kururugi" in full when taunting; Kallen = "Kallen".

**English writing guidance:** Grand, articulate, measured. In public use formal syntax, no contractions, rhetorical flourishes ("I am Zero!"). In private (俺 lines) use contractions and sharper wit. Briefings: clear, logical, chess metaphors ("pieces", "gambit"). Avoid modern slang.

**Samples:**

- 我が名はゼロ、黒の騎士団を率いる者だ。  
  → I am Zero, leader of the Order of the Black Knights.
- 勝てる気でいる軍ほど、崩れるともろいものだ。  
  → The more certain an army is of victory, the harder it falls.

## Suol Douglas (スオル・ダグラス) — スーパーロボット大戦 オリジナル

*Kwestie fabularne: 527 · cytaty bitewne: 0*

**Personality & role:** Head of Connect Force: big and imposing, but very talkative and approachable, and a second father to Seishiro. He served in the Earth Federation's deep-space exploration body and knew Dr. Asuka, so he sees himself as a pioneer of the space frontier. He switches between a grave commander giving briefings and a joking old uncle who teases the young pilots.

**Japanese register:** Briefings: 私/我々, 君/諸君, ～だろう, ～してくれ, ～を厳とせよ, ～かね ('これでも不服かね？'). Off duty: 俺, お前, rough old-man contractions ～とる/～しとる ('元気にやっとるようだな', 'みなぎっとる'), 'ちまって', 'ぶちかましてくれ', 'よう、＜主人公名＞！'.

**Tics & catchphrases:** 'まあ、持ちつ持たれつってやつだ'; jokes like '嬢ちゃんがかわいいからって、手ぇ出すなよ？'; 'いやぁ、すまなかった'; 'パァァァッと明るく'. Signs off with '頼んだぞ、コネクト・フォース'.

**Forms of address:** Seishiro: ＜主人公名＞ (given-name token), お前/君. Himari: '嬢ちゃん', later 'ヒマリ君'. Team: 'コネクト・フォース', '諸君'. Calls Seishiro's father '＜主人公姓＞隊長'.

**English writing guidance:** Two gears. Briefing gear: measured, authoritative, complete sentences ('I'm counting on you, Connect Force.'). Casual gear: gruff, folksy and warm, with dropped g's sparingly ('Doin' well, I see!'), 'kiddo/missy' for Himari's 嬢ちゃん, and hearty humour. Avoid making him sound like a drill sergeant; he is fatherly. A dry, sly understatement fits his teasing. Keep the ＜主人公名＞ token in his call-outs.

**Samples:**

- あ、嬢ちゃんがかわいいからって、手ぇ出すなよ？  
  → Oh, and don't go getting ideas just 'cause the young lady's cute, got it?
- サイド７の民間人がその新造戦艦に避難している。 / 彼らの保護が目的だ。これでも不服かね？  
  → Civilians from Side 7 have taken refuge on that new ship. Our goal is to protect them. Still have an objection?
