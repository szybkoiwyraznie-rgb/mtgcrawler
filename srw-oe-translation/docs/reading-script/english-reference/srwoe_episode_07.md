## Episode 7-1: Mt. Ossa Fortress

On Planet Zi, the war between the Helic Republic and the Guylos Empire was nearing a turning point.

With Guylos Emperor Zeppelin II on his deathbed, Imperial Marshal Prozen had seized real power and launched an assault on the Republic's capital.

Connect Force's arrival in the Helic Republic was expected to disrupt the Empire's plans, but Prozen was not a man to be so easily thwarted.

The Empire answered by pouring even more troops into its campaign to take Mt. Ossa Fortress, the stronghold guarding the Republic's capital.

Among them was Major Schubaltz's unit of the Imperial First Armored Division, now joined by Suzaku Kururugi, a pilot from another world.

Meanwhile, another force was closing in on the capital, New Helic City, from the sea.

It was Prozen's personal landing force, carried by a mobile base disguised as a giant iceberg.

Showering the area around it with jamming snow, the iceberg carrying the landing force crept slowly toward the coast near the capital…

## Episode 7-2: Schubaltz's Offensive

The Republic army had abandoned Mt. Ossa Fortress, pulling all units back to the capital's last line.

The deliberately abandoned fortress was cleverly rigged with traps, from automated batteries to unmanned Sleeper Zoids, buying them ample time.

Even so, sheer numbers won the Empire the outer works and a clear route to the Republic's capital.

And while the main force set about taking the fortress completely, Major Schubaltz's First Armored Division began its march on the capital.

Meanwhile, the White Base's unit had pulled off a masterful fighting retreat. After resupplying in the capital, it readied to meet the landing force.

Knowing the strengths of the mobile Connect Force, Zero's command was as sharp here as ever.

The fortress battle alone had cost the Empire dearly, while the Republic's losses were light.

The battle for the capital was now on two fronts, land and shore, and a clash of armies was at hand.

## Editorial notes

- **Translation:** Dialogue and narration comes directly from the current project `pakiet_EN/00_dialogue.jsonl`, without rewriting or summarizing. The old example is not a translation source. In-game line wrapping is joined into normal paragraphs; meaningful narration paragraphs are retained, and parenthesized thoughts are italicized.

- **Names:** Customizable protagonist-name tokens use the project defaults **Seishiro Kusanagi**. English speaker names come from the character tables. Explicit anonymous or radio-channel cues remain anonymous or channel-based rather than inventing an identity. Inline engine cue prefixes are removed from the spoken text.

- **Shared given names:** Distinct characters who share a short name in this episode are distinguished by their full English names from the project tables.

- **Structure:** Mission titles come from the translated mission tables. Translated place/time entries are used as headings; other headings are editorial signposts, not additional game narration. There is no opening list of navigation links.

- **Conditional exchanges:** Blockquotes distinguish conditional or situational tutorial/battle exchanges and alternate resolutions. Not every such exchange is necessarily optional. Exact triggers are not asserted, and their source order is not presented as a guaranteed playthrough sequence.

- **DLC versions:** Identical passages in re-released versions of the same mission are represented once. Where a later version changes wording, its alternate passages are shown separately, with immediate context, under **DLC text variant**. These are not subsequent events in the story.

- **Coverage:** All **2,248 dialogue entries** and **82 narration entries** in this episode are accounted for. **1,458 entries** are displayed; **872 identical DLC-copy entries** are represented by their matching passages. Standalone objectives, tutorial help panels, engine/camera labels, and empty spacers are omitted.

- **Source edition:** Japanese **1.08**, **NPJH50521**. Scene grouping comes from `06_scenes.jsonl`; mission titles and character labels come from `05_mission_text.jsonl` and `07_names_characters.jsonl`.
