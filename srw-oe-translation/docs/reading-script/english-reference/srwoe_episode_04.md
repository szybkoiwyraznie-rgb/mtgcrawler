## Lelouch of the Rebellion

The Pentagona World is a star system of five planets, Gastogal, Tridetoal, Fa, Coam and Mizun, orbiting the twin suns known as Sans.

All but Fa are inhabited, and each once had its own thriving dynasty, until Posaydal of Gastogal unified them into the Pentagona Federation.

Ever since, Oldna Posaydal has ruled the system through overwhelming military might, but of late, resistance movements have arisen on every planet.

However, these movements were divided planet by planet, not unified as a single rebel army…

The planet with the most rebel activity was Mizun.

Mizun, once home to the mighty Yaman dynasty, resisted Posaydal's army to the very end, and paid with a genocidal purge called the Yaman Hunt.

Perhaps because of this, anti-Posaydal sentiment ran high, and a rebel army formed around Little Sei, a base seized from Posaydal's regulars.

But now the regular army had launched a full-scale crackdown, and the rebels holed up in Little Sei faced annihilation…
