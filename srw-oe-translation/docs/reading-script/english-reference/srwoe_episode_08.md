## Episode 8-1: Earthtia

The Dark Dragon Emperor fell, and peace returned.

But Dark Dragon remnants still lurked on Earthtia, at times raiding towns and villages.

Among the Ryu wielders who fought those remnants was a knight who rode Ryu Knight Zephyr.

His name was Adeu Waltham, the hero who had dealt the Dark Dragon Emperor his final blow.

Adeu went on with his journey of training, defeating Dark Dragons and saving many lives.

But the Dark Dragons' plots reached past Earthtia. Fearing for Doki Doki Space, the Great Sage Nagy resolved to entrust Adeu with a new mission.

And so Adeu was summoned to Eldgear, a deserted ancient city, where Nagy and White Dragon waited.

### The next ally

## Da Cider Returns

Besides Earthtia, Doki Doki Space held another world, known as Hara-Hara World.

Hara-Hara World's Kingdom of Arara was famous as home to Hero Lamuness and the Guardian Knights, who had defeated the Demon God Goburiki when he once plunged Doki Doki Space into peril.

The kingdom was close to Earthtia's Kingdom of Pafuricia, and was on friendly terms with Connect as its point of contact in Hara-Hara World.

Lately, however, the Monskar once commanded by the supposedly vanquished Goburiki had grown active, and creatures resembling the Mucus had been seen.

Taking the matter seriously, Connect asked the kingdom to join the Connect Force in response, and King Yokkora III gladly agreed.

On receiving the news, the Connect Force came to Hara-Hara World to meet its new members and requested an audience with the King.

## Rare Metal Mining

Though it now had the Connect Force's help, Earthtia was still far from stable.

To strengthen their cooperation, they needed to clear out some of the Dark Dragons in hiding and ease the burden on the Ryu wielders.

To that end, the Connect Force surveyed Earthtia from above with its own methods, compiling precise terrain data of both surface and underground.

With this data, areas where Dark Dragons might hide could be largely pinpointed, and the search made dramatic progress.

The survey also yielded an unexpected bonus: it revealed an area holding vast deposits of rare metals, precious even in space.

But it was a dangerous region filled with volcanic gas, normally impossible for people to enter.

Naturally, the search for Dark Dragons there had made no headway.

With the consent of Earthtia's nations, Connect Force planned a resource-mining mission that would double as a Dark Dragon search.

A briefing on the operation was to be held at Pafuricia Castle…
