## Episode 15-1: Rise of Neo Zeon

The asteroid Axis was built by the Principality of Zeon as a semi-permanent resource colony.

Steadily expanded in the Jovian asteroid belt, Axis had grown into a mobile fortress, complete with nuclear engines and weapons factories.

During the war, Axis secretly headed for Earth, gathering recruits from mining asteroids.

Then, when Zeon and the Federation agreed to a ceasefire, it declared independence as Neo Zeon.

Its nominal head was Mineva Zabi, Dozle Zabi's daughter, but as she was still a child, policy and command lay with her regent, Haman Karn.

Working closely with the Principality of Zeon, Haman brought Axis close to lunar orbit and began intervening militarily across the Earth Sphere.

The Federation answered by massing forces at A Baoa Qu to keep Neo Zeon and Zeon in check.

White Base, too, was ordered back to its parent unit and assigned to A Baoa Qu.

Amid all this, Connect received classified intelligence from Kycilia's faction.

According to the report, Neo Zeon was moving ahead with a plan to drop a derelict colony onto Earth.

A derelict colony was indeed spotted on the move, and so, on humanitarian grounds, Connect Force set out to stop the operation.

## Battle of the Burning Sands

After the defeat at Odessa, Zeon's ground forces retreated into space one after another, and Earth seemed to be firmly under Federation control.

But when Neo Zeon shattered A Baoa Qu along with the Federation fleet, units hidden on every continent answered with guerrilla campaigns.

The fighting on the African front was fiercest, and Neo Zeon sent units down from orbit to support them.

Most of the Federation's ground troops were poorly trained; overwhelmed by Axis's new MS, they lost base after base in central Africa.

Leading this African onslaught were Zeon's veterans under Commander Rommel, and the unit of the Blue Giant, Ramba Ral.

On every front, the Federation always had more troops, but these heroic Zeon elites ran rings around them, bleeding their strength for nothing.

Fed up, the Federation once again recalled the White Base team from Connect Force to break the deadlock.
