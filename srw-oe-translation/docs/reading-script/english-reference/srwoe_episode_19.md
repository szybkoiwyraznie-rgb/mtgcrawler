## Ep. 19-1: Breaking the Axis Defenses

Neo Zeon's Axis-drop operation had briefly frozen the Federation and Zeon in a stalemate, but both sides rearmed, and tension was mounting again.

The Federation government condemned the Axis drop as Zeon's unilateral breach of the ceasefire, and public opinion followed its lead.

Zeon, meanwhile, held that the ceasefire had been void from the start, dismissed the Federation's claims as great-power arrogance, and pushed back.

Connect watched the situation with concern, but no path to a solution was in sight...

And when the Federation at last launched its assault on Axis, Connect Force was told that the White Base team was being recalled.

The Federation's main fleet moved on the S Field. The White Base team was to head for Axis with the fleet attacking from the N Field.

Zeon and Neo Zeon, under the overall command of Supreme Commander Gihren, stood ready to meet them at Axis, now turned into a mighty fortress.
