## Episode 5-1: Warp Gate

Having repelled the Grados advance force and swept away the Invaders, Mechanized Beasts and Mucus in the surrounding space, Connect Force turned in earnest to activating the Warp Gate.

Their destination: the Frontier system…

It was home to Macross Frontier, the fleet that had dispatched Skull Squadron to Connect Force.

Originally an emigrant fleet, Macross Frontier had partnered with Connect to build and defend the Warp Gate between Mars and the Frontier.

This voyage would also be a chance to rethink both systems' defenses in light of the changes ahead.
