## Episode 18-1: Prelude to Liberation

Aiming to free the Planet of the Seal from Grados, Connect sent a cutting-edge warp gate into the Grados system, partly to test its nearly finished giant gate, and opened a route all its own.

ISTO, for its part, held a warp device linked to the Grados homeworld, the "Station Satellite", but it could only move within a single universe, and its capacity was nowhere near Connect's.

To defeat ISTO and its overwhelming numbers, Connect had only one option: a swift, decisive battle before enemy reinforcements could arrive.

On the Seal, Grados still silenced free speech, ground the planet's culture into dust, and taught that ISTO's order was absolute.

If they did not hurry, the resistance that Eiji had once fought alongside might be wiped out entirely.
