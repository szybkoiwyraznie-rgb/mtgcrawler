## Episode 21-1: The Dark Zwarth

The space-time distortion around Central Connect Station kept growing worse by the day.

From the abnormal magic it was observing, Connect grew certain Goburiki had returned.

According to Da Cider, Goburiki's army commanded not only Monskars, but Dark Dragon Soldiers and a vast horde of Mucus as well…

But against an army that could appear out of another dimension without warning, Connect could do little more than post patrols at key points.

Connect Force, naturally, was the linchpin: a rapid-response unit with a special mission to intercept the enemy's main force.

The plan: target the Black Knight's guard and Goburiki's main army, and punch straight through.

Two task forces were formed around the flagships White Base and Macross Quarter, and with support units, they stood ready at Connect Station.

## Episode 21-2: Goburiki's Ultimate Form

But just as Zero had predicted, Goburiki's main army had used the Black Knight as a diversion and broken through the thinly held defense line.

If even one Warp Gate fell, the Mucus could be unleashed to overrun this sector. That would mean the end of all humanity's hopes.

To defeat Goburiki, Connect Force sent an elite unit led by the flagship Macross Quarter into a decisive battle.

- **DLC versions:** Identical passages in re-released versions of the same mission are represented once. Where a later version changes wording, its alternate passages are shown separately, with immediate context, under **DLC text variant**. These are not subsequent events in the story.
