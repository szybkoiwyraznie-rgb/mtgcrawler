## Episode 20-1: The 203rd Warp Gate

The 203rd Warp Gate.

It sits at Central Station, Connect's largest base, and it has long been sealed.

Beyond it lies a resource-rich sector, to which Connect once dispatched a resource survey team.

Some ten-odd years ago, aided by a traitor within Connect, the Balarant army seized that sector along with its Warp Gate.

Hoping to strike at Connect's home base, Balarant tried to force the Warp Gate into operation, but this sent the Core Module out of control.

It summoned vast numbers of Mucus into the sector, and the Balarant force was annihilated. Connect, too, was forced to abandon the Warp Gate.

So the 203rd Warp Gate was sealed. But recent survey reports confirmed that the faint Mucus readings once leaking from the far side had now disappeared.

That meant the Mucus had either left the sector beyond the gate, or at the very least were far less active there.

To investigate, a plan was drawn up to bring the 203rd Warp Gate back online.

With Mucus combat expected at the warp-out point, Connect Force was to be dispatched, accompanied by Dr. Kusanagi, the leading authority on Core Module research.

It was an extremely dangerous mission, but also a rare chance to learn how a sector overrun by the Mucus had changed in over a decade.

Connect readied full backup support and sent Connect Force on its way.
