### The DG Cell threat ends, the search continues

The Devil Gundam at the heart of the DG Cells was destroyed, and the solar system was saved from it.

The scattered Mucus Gates were swept away too, and the seeds of the DG Cells were slowly purged.

But the situation was far from reassuring.

Studies of the destroyed giant Mucus Gate revealed that several Mucus Gates of a similar size could still be lurking within the solar system.

The Devil Gundam had merely used one of them as its doorway into the solar system.

And so, even after the DG Cell threat had passed, a large-scale search for Mucus Gates continued throughout the solar system.
