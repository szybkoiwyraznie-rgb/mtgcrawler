## Prologue

Decades have passed since the Earth Federation unified the world. Science, technology, and trade advanced ever faster, giant space colonies rose around Earth, and the Universal Century began.

In time, humanity spread across the solar system, growing rich on its vast resources and energy. But that breakneck expansion magnified not only prosperity, but every problem along with it.

Economic growth bred inequality, and funneled vast funds, resources, and talent into criminal hands.

Terrorists and pirates grew bolder, and some, like Dr. Hell, openly declared their plans for world conquest and challenged all of humanity.

The Federation Forces were overwhelmed, and the former nations and the space colonies alike were forced to arm themselves for their own defense.

Naturally, this arms race only heightened tensions between nations and peoples, and it did not take long for the ideals of a unified government to be lost.

Ironically, war drove science forward even faster, spawning one runaway technology after another, the Devil Gundam Incident chief among them.

Likewise, research into unknown energies has altered the Earth's energy balance and invited invasions by races other than our own.

The Invaders, the Muge Empire, the Evil God Drago, the Mechanization and Jaku Empires, Daimakai… Those unearthly assaults remain fresh in memory.

Yet humanity overcame each of these crises, rebuilding and advancing time and again.

Ever uneasy about the next threat to come, people still live each day with resolve.

## Interlude

Half a century had passed since humanity began sending its excess population into space.

The colonies, giant cities floating in space, became humanity's second homeland, where people bore children, raised them, and died.

Universal Century 0079. Side 3, calling itself the Principality of Zeon, declared a war of independence on the Federation.

In little more than a month of fighting, half of humanity's total population perished, and people trembled at what they had done.

That humanity, having survived so many crises, would plunge into such carnage was a tragedy.

The war then ground into a stalemate, and now, more than eight months have passed…

## Editorial notes

- **Translation:** All dialogue and narration comes directly from the project's `pakiet_EN/00_dialogue.jsonl`. The example document was used only as a layout reference, not as a translation source. No dialogue has been rewritten or summarized.

- **Reading format:** In-game line wrapping has been joined into normal paragraphs. Parenthesized thoughts are italicized; meaningful paragraph breaks in narration are retained.

- **Names:** The five occurrences of the customizable protagonist's given-name placeholder are rendered as **Seishiro**, using the project's default **Seishiro Kusanagi**. Speaker names come from the English character tables; Suol uses the full-name label **Suol Douglas** rather than the short label **Chief Suol**.

- **Structure:** The mission titles are **Basic Training** and **Rescue White Base**, as translated in `05_mission_text.jsonl`. Place/time headings use their translated scene entries. Other headings, including the conditional-exchange labels, are editorial signposts, not additional game narration.

- **Conditional exchanges:** Blockquotes keep situational tutorial and battle dialogue distinct from the surrounding story. Their exact activation conditions and order are not asserted. The document preserves the source order of the selected text entries.

- **Coverage:** Both story scenes of Episode 0 are included, with all **242 dialogue entries** and **11 narration entries** retained. Standalone victory/defeat conditions and engine/camera labels are omitted. Training conversations are retained, including their conditional variants.

- **Source scope:** `06_scenes.jsonl` identifies these as the two Episode 0 story scenes. The reading script is based on the project's translation of the Japanese **1.08** release (**NPJH50521**).
