## The 30 Bunch Incident

Universal Century 0079. In the Earth Sphere, the war between Zeon and the Federation had slowly turned in the Federation's favor since its victory at Odessa.

The rebuilt main Federation space fleet massed at Luna II, ready to invade Zeon at any time.

But wary of a Zeon ground counterattack exploiting the Mucus incursions, the Federation postponed its great space offensive, and the front stalled.

Even so, backed by sheer material might, the Federation slowly squeezed Zeon's hold on space, and several colony clusters fell into its sphere.

Some hailed it as the colonies' liberation, but for many colonists, the Federation government was hardly a welcome presence.

Amid this, in one colony newly under Federation control, citizens' groups held a small protest against the Earth Federation government.

Years of prewar Federation oppression had stoked discontent among the citizens, and the protest soon grew into a mass demonstration under an anti-Federation banner.

Fearing public opinion would rally behind it, the government asked Jamitov Hymem, whose clout in the military was growing, to quell the protests.

Jamitov, an Earthnoid supremacist, believed an uncompromising policy toward colony residents was necessary.

Commodore Blex, who saw this ideology as a threat, secretly tracked their moves and learned of a plan to use G3, a poison gas weapon, on the colony.

As preparations to expose Jamitov advanced, a special unit launched to stop the disaster.
