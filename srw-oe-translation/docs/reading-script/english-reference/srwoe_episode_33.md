## The Mists of Genesis

Having pushed through the Recoral Galaxy to reach the Commune system, humanity was permitted to advance through the Gate of Harmony to the core.

Observations from the Commune system toward the galactic center confirmed the existence of a colossal Mucus several light-years across.

Connect named this colossal Mucus "Mucus Genesis," designating it by the codename "Genesis."

Genesis's Mucus Core was the final objective of Operation Extend.

Preparations moved steadily ahead during the observations, and fleets gathered around the Gate, but the Ark Ship, the key to it all, was expected to need a little more time to arrive.

For Connect Force, this would be their last chance to rest…
