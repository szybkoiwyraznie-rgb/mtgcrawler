## Episode 12-1: Guardians of the Colonies

As Zeon and the Federation moved toward a ceasefire, a wave of runaway Labor incidents swept the space colonies, keeping the Federation Forces and the colonies' defense units stretched thin.

The Mechanization Empire was believed to be behind the rampages, launching a coordinated uprising in answer to the destruction of its Tokyo Bay plant.

In response, every human faction, Federation and Zeon alike, agreed to join forces against it. The Connect Force, naturally, was among them.

Amid the fighting, the most striking feats came from a group of Gundams of unknown affiliation.

They appeared without warning and saved colony after colony, until people came to call them the "Guardians of the Colonies"…

Now word had arrived that an industrial colony was in dire straits, its port and most of its factory blocks seized by the Mechanization Empire.

The Connect Force immediately dispatched a relief force to bring the situation under control.

### The colony retaken

The industrial colony overrun by the Mechanization Empire was retaken thanks to the Connect Force.

Without their production plant, the Mechanized Beasts were hunted down and the threat removed.

Yet with the chance that the Empire might open new Mucus Gates on Earth or other planets, constant vigilance was still required.

A real solution meant not only finding the Mechanization Empire's home base, but stopping the Mucus from spreading.

Still, this incident made the people of the Earth Sphere clearly aware of a threat to all humanity.

And with the ceasefire easing press restrictions, long-hidden information gradually came to light.

The Connect Force itself became more widely known, and a movement to support its activities began to take shape.

Its work in the Earth Sphere a success, the Connect Force and its new members prepared to leave the solar system once more.

## Assassinate Rudolph

On the surface, relations between the Helic Republic and the Guylos Empire seemed good.

But Zero, who led the Republic's intelligence work, learned that Imperial Regent Prozen was pulling all sorts of strings behind the scenes.

Officially, the visit was to discuss the Empire's future diplomatic ties with ISTO.

## Editorial notes

- **Translation:** Dialogue and narration comes directly from the current project `pakiet_EN/00_dialogue.jsonl`, without rewriting or summarizing. The old example is not a translation source. In-game line wrapping is joined into normal paragraphs; meaningful narration paragraphs are retained, and parenthesized thoughts are italicized.

- **Names:** Customizable protagonist-name tokens use the project defaults **Seishiro Kusanagi**. English speaker names come from the character tables. Explicit anonymous or radio-channel cues remain anonymous or channel-based rather than inventing an identity. Inline engine cue prefixes are removed from the spoken text.

- **Shared given names:** Distinct characters who share a short name in this episode are distinguished by their full English names from the project tables.

- **Structure:** Mission titles come from the translated mission tables. Translated place/time entries are used as headings; other headings are editorial signposts, not additional game narration. There is no opening list of navigation links.

- **Conditional exchanges:** Blockquotes distinguish conditional or situational tutorial/battle exchanges and alternate resolutions. Not every such exchange is necessarily optional. Exact triggers are not asserted, and their source order is not presented as a guaranteed playthrough sequence.

- **DLC versions:** Identical passages in re-released versions of the same mission are represented once. Where a later version changes wording, its alternate passages are shown separately, with immediate context, under **DLC text variant**. These are not subsequent events in the story.

- **Coverage:** All **1,067 dialogue entries** and **73 narration entries** in this episode are accounted for. **787 entries** are displayed; **353 identical DLC-copy entries** are represented by their matching passages. Standalone objectives, tutorial help panels, engine/camera labels, and empty spacers are omitted.

- **Source edition:** Japanese **1.08**, **NPJH50521**. Scene grouping comes from `06_scenes.jsonl`; mission titles and character labels come from `05_mission_text.jsonl` and `07_names_characters.jsonl`.
